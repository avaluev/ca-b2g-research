# DisasterAlertKG

_Free Telegram tool aggregating MoES landslide/earthquake/mudflow alerts in Kyrgyz/Russian/English with safety checklists_

## Pain point

**Evidence**: https://mes.gov.kg/


**Target customer**: Bishkek residents + expats + tourists; secondary: parents

- **Year-1 target**: $4,000
- **Year-3 target**: $30,000
