# OutsourceToUZ

_Manifesto + paid services directory: 'why hire Uzbek IT talent' for foreign tech companies, with vetted candidate sourcing_

## Pain point

IT Park UZ has 30K+ residents seeking foreign clients. Foreign companies (US, EU) lack visibility into UZ talent. LinkedIn search is noisy. No curated 'hire Uzbek talent' resource.

**Evidence**: https://it-park.uz/


**Target customer**: US/EU tech founders + hiring managers exploring nearshore + Eastern outsourcing options


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $200.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $130,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 12 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Write 5,000-word manifesto on UZ IT talent
- [ ] Cross-post on Hacker News, IndieHackers, LinkedIn
- [ ] Carrd directory of 50 vetted UZ devs
- [ ] $200/mo for featured profile, or 10% placement fee
- [ ] Soft-launch via IT Park UZ + Habr UZ + LinkedIn


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 manifesto reads + 5 paid placements in 90 days
- **Window**: 90 days
- **Channels**: Hacker News, IndieHackers, LinkedIn, IT Park UZ


**Tech stack**: Substack, Carrd, Notion, Click + Stripe

**Capability required**: English + Russian, long-form content, B2B sales, hiring-domain familiarity


## Moat potential

Diaspora-bridge brand; direct foreign-tech relationship; commission revenue compounds.
