# PolicyDigestUZ

_Manifesto-style weekly newsletter analyzing one new Uzbek decree per week — for diaspora investors and consultants_

## Pain point

Diaspora and foreign investors lack a curated lens on Uzbek decrees. Generic news feeds don't analyze implementation odds, budget realism, vendor opportunities. Existing English-language newsletters (Eurasianet, etc.) cover too broad.

**Evidence**: https://eurasianet.org/region/uzbekistan


**Target customer**: Uzbek diaspora professionals in Dubai, London, Almaty, Moscow + foreign consultants + EBRD/ADB program officers + LP/VC scouts


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $49.00
- **Year-1 target**: $22,000
- **Year-3 target**: $220,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 4 days
- **Build cost**: $0

**Steps:**
- [ ] Write founder's-thesis post: 'why one decree per week beats macro analysis'
- [ ] Substack page + paid tier
- [ ] Free first 5 issues, then $49/mo paywall
- [ ] Cross-post on LinkedIn + Twitter X
- [ ] Soft-launch in 'Uzbeks Abroad' LinkedIn group


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 500 free signups + 30 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days

**Tech stack**: Substack, Notion (research), Stripe via Substack

**Capability required**: Russian + English, decree analysis, investment-grade writing

Authority brand on Uzbek policy; spin-out into a paid research subscription service for funds.
