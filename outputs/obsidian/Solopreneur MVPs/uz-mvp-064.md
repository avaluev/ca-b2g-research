# ScholarshipFinderUZ

_Carrd directory + Telegram bot listing every active scholarship for Uzbek students applying abroad_

## Pain point

Uzbek students 18-25 hunt scholarships across multiple sites (DAAD, Chevening, KOICA, ISDB, etc.) — no aggregator exists. Forum threads on kun.uz education show demand.

**Evidence**: https://kun.uz/ru/category/society/


**Target customer**: Uzbek students 17-25 in their senior year of school or undergraduate, planning to study in EU/Korea/UK/Russia


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 50 active scholarships with deadlines + eligibility
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram alert bot
- [ ] Free for students, sponsor slot for prep services + IELTS coaches
- [ ] Soft-launch via DTM-prep + IT Park UZ Telegram
- [ ] Affiliate to language schools


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,500 visits + 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: DTM-prep Telegram, IT Park UZ student community, kun.uz education


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Telegram Bot

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin + English, research


## Moat potential

Authority + sponsor compounds; can scale to MENA + Central Asia.

## Risks

- **Deadlines change** — _Mitigation_: Monthly review
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Affiliate to language schools
- **Competition from Chevening UZ** — _Mitigation_: Lean on bilingual UX
