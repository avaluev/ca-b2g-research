# BeeKeeperUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz subscription delivering monthly hive-management plans + weather alerts to Uzbek beekeepers_

## Pain point

Uzbekistan has 15K+ registered beekeepers with average 30 hives. They suffer from CCD-like losses due to poor disease management. Forum threads on agro.uz show persistent questions on Varroa mite control. No localized advisory exists.

**Evidence**: https://agro.uz/


**Target customer**: Beekeepers 35-65 in Tashkent oblast, Samarkand, Bukhara managing 30-200 hives, especially organic-honey exporters


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $6.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Partner with one professional beekeeper-mentor in Tashkent ($200/mo retainer)
- [ ] Telegram newsletter: monthly plan + weather + disease alerts
- [ ] Free first month, $6/mo after
- [ ] Cross-post on agro.uz and beekeeper Telegram groups
- [ ] Add affiliate links to equipment dealers


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 100 subscribers + 30 paying in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: agro.uz beekeeping, Tashkent beekeeper Telegram, honey-export Telegram channel


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Buttondown, Notion CMS, Click subscription

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, managed-service operations, expert-network outreach


## Moat potential

Mentor + farmer relationships compound; possible spin-out into a beekeeping app.

## Risks

- **Mentor unavailability** — _Mitigation_: Pre-record monthly plan video + hire 2 mentors
- **Niche too small** — _Mitigation_: Expand to general livestock advisory
- **Seasonal revenue** — _Mitigation_: Annual prepay 20% discount
