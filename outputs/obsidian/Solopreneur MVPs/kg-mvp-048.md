# PharmacyDirectoryKG

_Searchable directory of 5,000+ Kyrgyz pharmacies with stock-availability search for hard-to-find drugs_

## Pain point

Patients call 5-10 pharmacies hunting rare drugs. No central availability tool. Forum threads on 24.kg ask 'who has X drug?'

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Bishkek patients 30-65 hunting rare meds, imported drugs, chronic-disease specialty


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $12.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 300 Bishkek pharmacies
- [ ] Carrd searchable + Telegram bot
- [ ] Free for patients, $12/mo featured + bulk
- [ ] Mbank micro-payment
- [ ] Soft-launch via 24.kg health + pharmacy distributor


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 2,500 visits + 5 paid pharmacies in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: 24.kg health, Bishkek pharmacy distributor, Mbank family


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS + Airtable, Telegram Bot

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, field outreach


## Moat potential

Yandex Maps of pharmacies; sponsorship.

## Risks

- **Stock data goes stale** — _Mitigation_: Daily check-in + commission
- **Pharmacies don't pay** — _Mitigation_: Distributor sponsorship
- **Coverage gaps** — _Mitigation_: Regional rollout
