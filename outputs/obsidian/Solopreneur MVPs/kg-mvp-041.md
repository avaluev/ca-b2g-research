# VaccineRecordKG

_Free Telegram tool: upload child vaccine card, get next-due alerts and digital backup; family-friendly Russian/Kyrgyz_

## Pain point

Paper vaccination cards lost. Parents miss boosters. UNICEF KG supports immunization tracking.

**Evidence**: https://www.unicef.org/kyrgyzstan/


**Target customer**: Bishkek and Osh parents 25-40 with under-5 children, especially first-time mothers


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $4,000
- **Year-3 target**: $30,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] Tesseract OCR for vaccine card
- [ ] Telegram bot photo + DOB
- [ ] Push notification 7 days before vaccine
- [ ] UNICEF KG grant pitch ($25K)
- [ ] Soft-launch in Bishkek parent Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 400 children registered in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Bishkek parent Telegram, Osh parent groups, UNICEF KG partners


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Tesseract OCR, Supabase

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, OCR, child-health


## Moat potential

UNICEF/WHO grant continuity; possible OEM to MoH KG.

## Risks

- **OCR accuracy** — _Mitigation_: Manual review for edge
- **Privacy** — _Mitigation_: Minimal retention
- **Govt competitor** — _Mitigation_: Polikliniki branding partnership
