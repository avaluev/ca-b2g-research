# ClinicAppointmentUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz service that books appointments at top Tashkent private clinics in Russian/Uzbek via Telegram_

## Pain point

Top Tashkent clinics (Akfa, Centrum) have phone-only or web booking with limited slots visibility. Working professionals can't call during business hours. No aggregator exists.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/health/


**Target customer**: Tashkent professionals 28-50 booking specialist appointments for themselves or aging parents


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $5.00
- **Year-1 target**: $14,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Map 10 top Tashkent clinics + their booking processes
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: specialty + preferred date + insurance
- [ ] Founder books on customer's behalf within 4h
- [ ] Click payment 60,000 UZS
- [ ] Soft-launch in TBC Bank corporate-health Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid bookings in 30 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: TBC corporate Telegram, spot.uz health chat, Tashkent expat WhatsApp


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, phone fluency, clinic relationships


## Moat potential

Clinic relationships compound; affiliate revenue from clinics.

## Risks

- **Low margins given operational time** — _Mitigation_: Premium tier $20 for VIP clinics
- **Refund disputes** — _Mitigation_: Refund if booking fails
