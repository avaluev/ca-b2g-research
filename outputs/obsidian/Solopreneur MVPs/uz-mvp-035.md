# AralWaterPulse

_Free public dashboard tracking real-time water-quality and salinity monitoring stations across the Aral Sea basin_

## Pain point

Aral Sea basin water quality data is collected but rarely public. Karakalpakstan farmers, environmental researchers, donor-program TTLs lack a real-time view. ADB and WB programs need monitoring tools.

**Evidence**: https://www.adb.org/projects/country/uzb


**Target customer**: Karakalpakstan environmental researchers, donor program officers, Karakalpak agronomists, environmental NGOs


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 10 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Aggregate open data from MERIDIAN, NSDI, regional water agencies
- [ ] Build Vercel + Next.js dashboard with map and time series
- [ ] Cross-post in environmental NGO networks
- [ ] ADB/WB/UNDP grant pitch ($30K)
- [ ] Soft-launch via Karakalpak environmental Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 unique users + 1 donor-program citation in 60 days
- **Window**: 90 days
- **Channels**: Karakalpak environmental Telegram, EU Delegation Tashkent, UNDP Uzbekistan


**Tech stack**: Next.js, Vercel, Mapbox, Supabase

**Capability required**: Russian + Karakalpak basics, data wrangling, dashboard dev


## Moat potential

Donor-grant continuity; possible OEM to Karakalpakstan government via UNDP partner.

## Risks

- **Open data limited** — _Mitigation_: Partner with one university for additional sensors
- **Sensitive politically** — _Mitigation_: Stay descriptive; partner with established NGOs
- **Low monetization** — _Mitigation_: Donor-grant only
