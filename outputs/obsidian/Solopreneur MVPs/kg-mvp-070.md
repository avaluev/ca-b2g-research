# ORTQuestionBank

_Free Telegram bot serving daily Kyrgyz-language ORT-style practice questions with explanations_

## Pain point

ORT (Republican Test) bottleneck for university entrance. Question banks paper or expensive private platforms.

**Evidence**: https://testing.kg/


**Target customer**: Grade 11 students 17-18 across KG; secondary: Grade 10 starting prep


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 400 sample ORT questions
- [ ] Telegram bot daily-question
- [ ] Free 3/day, $3/mo unlimited + tracking
- [ ] Mbank subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch in ORT-prep + parent Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 800 users + 40 paid in 60 days during prep season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: ORT-prep Telegram, Bishkek parent groups, MathTutorKG user base


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase, Mbank

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, ORT domain


## Moat potential

Question bank moat; cross-promo TutorMatchKG, MathTutorKG.

## Risks

- **Copyright** — _Mitigation_: Generate original ORT-style
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Year-round Grade 9-11
- **Free competitors** — _Mitigation_: UX + tracking
