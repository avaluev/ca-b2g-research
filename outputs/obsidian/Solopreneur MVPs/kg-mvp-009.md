# BishkekMonitor

_Anonymous Telegram channel + bot for Bishkek residents to flag broken streetlights, garbage, water issues_

## Pain point

Bishkek Mayor's office runs 1108 hotline (phone). Citizens want anonymous + visual reporting. UNDP supports civic tech.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Bishkek apartment-block residents 25-55; secondary: Osh residents


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $40,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Telegram bot: photo + geo-tag + category
- [ ] Auto-publish to district-level channel + Vercel dashboard
- [ ] Pre-brief 1 progressive Bishkek city council member
- [ ] UNDP grant pitch ($25K)
- [ ] Soft-launch in kaktus.media community + 24.kg


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 reports + 1 mayor's-office reference in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: kaktus.media, 24.kg, Bishkek district WhatsApp


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase, Vercel + Next.js, Mapbox

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, civic tech network


## Moat potential

Citizen issue dataset to mayor + UNDP; possible licensing.

## Risks
