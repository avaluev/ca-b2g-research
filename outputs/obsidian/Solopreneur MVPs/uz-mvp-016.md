# MahallaCalendar

_Hyperlocal Telegram channel listing every public hearing, mahalla meeting, and government open day in your district_

## Pain point

Mahalla meetings, hokimiyat reception hours, public hearings are announced via paper bulletins or rarely-updated websites. Citizens miss participation windows. EU-GIZ governance program looks for citizen-engagement amplifiers.

**Evidence**: https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/list/society/


**Target customer**: Civic-engaged Tashkent residents 25-55, parents of school-age children, small business owners attending hokimiyat receptions for permit follow-ups


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $50.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Map 12 Tashkent districts and create Telegram channel for each
- [ ] Aggregate weekly events from hokimiyat.uz + mahalla raisi WhatsApp groups
- [ ] Add Notion-driven publishing pipeline
- [ ] Sell sponsored slot to one civic NGO ($50/mo)
- [ ] Pitch EU-GIZ for $20K civic-engagement micro-grant


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 subscribers across 3 districts in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent district WhatsApp groups, spot.uz civic chat, Buyuk Kelajak community


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Notion, Buttondown for email mirror

**Capability required**: Russian and Uzbek-Latin, civic outreach, content cadence


## Moat potential

Hyperlocal subscriber loyalty; donor-grant continuity; can extend to remote-Fergana-Valley districts.

## Risks

- **Information drying up if mahalla raisi deprioritize** — _Mitigation_: Build network of 30+ raisi via volunteer outreach
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Lean on grant funding
- **Competition from official channels** — _Mitigation_: Aggregate across districts faster than any single one can
