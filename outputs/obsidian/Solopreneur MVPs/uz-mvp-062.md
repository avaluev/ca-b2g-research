# EnglishCoachUZ

_Subscription Telegram bot: AI-powered English-conversation practice for Uzbeks preparing for IELTS or job applications_

## Pain point

Uzbeks 20-35 need English for IT Park work, study abroad, freelance income. IELTS prep classes cost $200-500/mo. AI conversation practice is missing in Uzbek edtech.

**Evidence**: https://it-park.uz/


**Target customer**: Tashkent and Samarkand professionals 22-35 wanting English fluency for career; secondary: university students prepping for IELTS


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $9.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $150,000


## MVR plan (ad_booking)

- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build Telegram bot with GPT-4o-mini conversation
- [ ] IELTS-aligned topics + grading rubric
- [ ] Voice via Whisper for speaking practice
- [ ] $9/mo subscription via Click
- [ ] Soft-launch in IT Park UZ + DTM-prep Telegram
