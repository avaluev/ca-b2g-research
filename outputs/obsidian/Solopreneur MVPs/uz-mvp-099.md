# AdvisorMatchUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz: matches Uzbek SMEs with sector-expert advisors (legal, finance, tech) for 1h paid calls_

## Pain point

Uzbek SME owners 30-50 face strategic decisions (raise capital, expand to RU, hire CTO) without trusted advisors. Avloni and KPMG focus on enterprise. Clarity-style advisor marketplace doesn't exist for UZ.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/


**Target customer**: Uzbek SME owners 30-50 in IT, retail, manufacturing seeking 1h advisor calls; secondary: aspiring founders


## Monetization

- **Model**: marketplace
- **Price point**: $80.00
- **Year-1 target**: $14,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 30 verified advisors across legal, finance, tech
- [ ] Carrd + Telegram intake
- [ ] $80/call + 20% platform fee
- [ ] Click + Stripe
- [ ] Soft-launch in TBC SME + IT Park UZ + ICT Week 2026


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid calls in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME, IT Park UZ, ICT Week 2026, Spot.uz business


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Calendly, Notion (advisor roster), Click + Stripe

**Capability required**: Russian + English, advisor outreach, B2B sales


## Moat potential

Advisor + SME network compounds; possible foundry for UZ angel investing.

## Risks

- **Quality variance** — _Mitigation_: Reviews + tier system
