# AskMyMinistry

_AI tool that drafts a perfectly-formatted Russian-language inquiry to any Uzbek ministry, ready to submit via virtual reception_

## Pain point

President's virtual reception (pm.gov.uz) and ministry virtual receptions are major channels but require formal jargon. Citizens write informally and get rejected. Spot.uz forum threads regularly ask 'how do I write to the ministry?'

**Evidence**: https://pm.gov.uz/


**Target customer**: Educated Uzbek citizens 30-55 with a specific grievance, preferring written channels over phone hotlines


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build a Carrd page with input box for grievance + ministry picker
- [ ] GPT-4o generates formal Russian draft
- [ ] Free 1 draft per week, $3 unlimited per week
- [ ] Click micro-payment integration
- [ ] Soft-launch in spot.uz civic chat


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 drafts generated in 30 days, 30 paid upgrades
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz civic, kun.uz Telegram, Tashkent district groups


**Tech stack**: Carrd, OpenAI GPT-4o, Click

**Capability required**: Russian, prompt engineering


## Moat potential

Generate prompts library by ministry; partner with one ombudsman office for branding.

## Risks

- **AI output legally weak** — _Mitigation_: Add disclaimer + optional paralegal review
- **Govt launches official tool** — _Mitigation_: Lean on speed and Telegram-native UX
- **Low willingness to pay** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with complaint-fulfillment service
