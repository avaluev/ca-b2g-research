# PharmacyShelfKG

_SaaS for independent Kyrgyz pharmacies: photograph shelf, get OCR-based stock list and reorder alerts_

## Pain point

5,000+ independent pharmacies in KG track manually. Digital Code drives e-prescription. Enterprise solutions cost $300+/mo.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Independent pharmacy owners 35-55 in Bishkek, Osh with 1-3 outlets

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $20.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $180,000
