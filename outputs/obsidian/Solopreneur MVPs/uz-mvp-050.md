# ParentingExpertUZ

_Manifesto + paid course: research-backed Uzbek-language parenting content for first-time parents 25-35_

## Pain point

First-time Uzbek parents 25-35 access conflicting WhatsApp advice + outdated polikliniki guidance. UNICEF supports early-childhood development. Existing Russian-language content (Komarovskiy) doesn't address Uzbek context.

**Evidence**: https://www.unicef.org/uzbekistan/


**Target customer**: First-time Uzbek parents 25-35 in Tashkent, Samarkand, Andijan with smartphones and aspirational parenting culture

- **Price point**: $39.00

- **Year-3 target**: $100,000
