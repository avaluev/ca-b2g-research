# DocSignerKG

_Demo videos + Wizard-of-Oz: helps Kyrgyz citizens obtain electronic signature (ES) for Tunduk and tax filing_

## Pain point

Digital Code mandates ES for many services. Setup spans 3 portals. Self-employed and SMEs lose hours. No private helpers exist yet.

**Evidence**: https://salyk.kg/


**Target customer**: Bishkek SMEs + self-employed (designers, lawyers, IT freelancers); secondary: returning migrants needing ES for resettlement
