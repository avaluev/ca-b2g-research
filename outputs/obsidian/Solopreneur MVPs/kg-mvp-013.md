# KGGovDocVault

_Encrypted Telegram vault for Kyrgyz citizens to store digital copies of all government documents, with AI search_

## Pain point

Kyrgyz citizens lose documents when phones break. Tunduk doesn't aggregate. Banks demand fresh copies.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Bishkek and Osh professionals 30-50 with kids, property, small business

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $4.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000
