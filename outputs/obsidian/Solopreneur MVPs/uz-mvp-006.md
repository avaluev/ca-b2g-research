# UZBudgetView

_Plain-language explainer of openbudget.uz datasets — a Telegram bot answering 'how much did my mahalla get?' in Uzbek and Russian_

## Pain point

Openbudget.uz is one of the most progressive open data portals in CIS but its UI is Excel-heavy and Russian-only. Citizens have no answer to 'how much did my district receive this year for roads?' Local journalists in spot.uz repeatedly ask the question.

**Evidence**: https://openbudget.uz/


**Target customer**: Civic-active Uzbeks 25-45 who use Telegram and read spot.uz/gazeta.uz; secondary: investigative journalists, mahalla raisi who want to benchmark their allocation


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 9 days
- **Build cost**: $40

**Steps:**
- [ ] Download openbudget.uz CSV dumps for 2023-2025 budgets
- [ ] Build retrieval index in Supabase + pgvector for natural-language queries
- [ ] Telegram bot answers in Uzbek and Russian with chart screenshot generated by Quickchart.io
- [ ] Soft-launch with 3 spot.uz journalists for case studies
- [ ] Add EU-GIZ governance grant pitch deck for $20-30K civic-tech grant


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 unique users + 1 journalist mention in 45 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz Telegram, gazeta.uz reader chat, Buyuk Kelajak Telegram (civic activism), EU Delegation Tashkent civic tech mailing list


**Tech stack**: Python + pgvector, Supabase, OpenAI, Quickchart.io, Telegram Bot API

**Capability required**: Uzbek + Russian, data wrangling, civic-tech grant writing


## Moat potential

First-mover position with the openbudget.uz dataset; possible spin-out as the 'OpenSpending of UZ'; donor grant continuity.

## Risks

- **Dataset structure changes break parser** — _Mitigation_: Schema-detection layer; alert on parse failure
- **Government opposes 'critical' framing** — _Mitigation_: Position as celebratory transparency tool; partner with UNDP open-data work
- **Low organic traction without ads** — _Mitigation_: Lean on civic-tech grant rather than user revenue
