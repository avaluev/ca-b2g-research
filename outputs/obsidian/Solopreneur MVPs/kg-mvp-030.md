# DairyMarketKG

_Telegram-based directory of regional dairy buyers and prices for smallholder Kyrgyz dairy farmers_

## Pain point

Smallholder dairy farmers (5-30 cows) in Issyk-Kul, Chui sell at below-market. No transparent marketplace.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Dairy farmers 35-55 in Issyk-Kul, Chui with 5-30 cows producing 50-300L/day


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $6.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 30 dairy buyers with weekly prices
- [ ] Telegram channel + Carrd directory
- [ ] Free for farmers, $6/wk for buyer featured listing
- [ ] Mbank micro-payment
- [ ] Soft-launch in Issyk-Kul farmer Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 200 farmer subs + 5 paid buyers in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Issyk-Kul farmer Telegram, Chui farmer Telegram, kaktus.media agri


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram, Notion CMS

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, field outreach


## Moat potential

Trust + buyer list compounds.

## Risks

- **Buyer pushback** — _Mitigation_: First month free + anonymization
- **Low payments** — _Mitigation_: Bundle input-dealer ads
- **Regional fragmentation** — _Mitigation_: Start with one region
