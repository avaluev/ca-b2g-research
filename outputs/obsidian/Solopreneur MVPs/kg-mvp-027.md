# VetCallKG

_Wizard-of-Oz: photo + voice description of sick livestock, get 24h Russian/Kyrgyz vet response for $5_

## Pain point

Naryn and Issyk-Kul have 1 vet per 8K livestock. Farmers wait 2-5 days. Local WhatsApp vet groups charge ad-hoc.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Smallholder livestock farmers 35-60 in Naryn, Issyk-Kul, Talas


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $5.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $150

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 3 vet college graduates at $200/mo retainer
- [ ] Telegram bot intake
- [ ] Vets respond 24h
- [ ] Mbank 450 KGS per consult
- [ ] Soft-launch via Naryn farmer Telegram + AKDN partner network


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid consults + 5-star rating in 30 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: Naryn farmer Telegram, AKDN rural network, kaktus.media


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Mbank

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, vet network management


## Moat potential

Vet network; medication delivery via local pharmacy partner.

## Risks

- **Vet liability** — _Mitigation_: Disclaimer + emergency-call routing
- **Vet retention** — _Mitigation_: Performance bonuses
- **Regulatory** — _Mitigation_: Position as 'preliminary advice'
