# FreightTrackerUZ

_SaaS for small Uzbek logistics firms: GPS tracking + driver mobile app for $30/mo, no hardware_

## Pain point

5,000+ small Uzbek logistics firms (5-50 trucks) lack GPS tracking. Wialon and Galileo charge $50-100/truck/month + hardware. Telegram-based check-ins are common.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/logistics/


**Target customer**: Small Uzbek logistics firms 25-200 trucks doing UZ + Middle Corridor routes; owner-operators


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $30.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $150,000


## MVR plan (landing_page)

- **Build time**: 12 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build Android-only driver app with GPS + Telegram
- [ ] Web dashboard for fleet owner
- [ ] $30/mo per truck flat
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch via TBC SME logistics + ICT Week 2026


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 3 firms + 30 trucks paying in 90 days
- **Window**: 90 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME logistics, ICT Week 2026 logistics, UZ trucker WhatsApp


**Tech stack**: Flutter Android, Supabase, Mapbox, Click subscription

**Capability required**: Russian, Flutter dev, B2B logistics sales


## Moat potential

Recurring SaaS, can extend to compliance + driver behavior; possible OEM to State Customs Committee for Middle Corridor pilot.

## Risks

- **Hardware-free reliability** — _Mitigation_: Phone-based GPS + driver compliance
- **Slow adoption** — _Mitigation_: Free 30-day trial; partner with TBC fleet financing
