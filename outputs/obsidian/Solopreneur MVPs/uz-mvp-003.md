# MyID Onboarding Coach

_5-minute video walkthrough of MyID Mobile-ID activation, with WhatsApp support, for non-technical Uzbek adults_

## Pain point

MyID Mobile ID is rolling out as the universal authentication for EPIGU + bank apps + courts. Activation requires 8 steps including biometric capture and SMS verification. Adoption is uneven outside Tashkent — Telegram help threads see 50+ daily 'MyID не работает' posts. Banks force MyID for new accounts, creating cliff demand.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/12/05/myid/


**Target customer**: Non-tech-savvy Uzbeks 40-65 forced to onboard MyID for new bank accounts, especially returning migrant workers and rural residents whose SMS gateways struggle


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 4 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Record 90-second YouTube/TikTok activation walkthrough (Russian + Uzbek voiceover)
- [ ] Set up WhatsApp Business funnel: viewers DM for 1:1 help
- [ ] Charge 25,000 UZS ($2) for guided activation via screen-share over WhatsApp
- [ ] Cross-post in spot.uz Telegram comments and IT Park UZ Telegram
- [ ] Add affiliate link to TBC Bank or Anorbank account opening (referral fee $5-10/lead)


## Validation

- **Window**: 45 days

**Tech stack**: YouTube + TikTok, WhatsApp Business, Notion CRM, Stripe Atlas + Click for tipping

**Capability required**: video editing (CapCut), Uzbek + Russian voiceover, WhatsApp customer support
