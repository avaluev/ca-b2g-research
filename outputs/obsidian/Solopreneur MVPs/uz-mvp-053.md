# MigrantWageGuard

_Wizard-of-Oz: helps Uzbek migrants in Russia/Korea recover unpaid wages via legal demand letters in 14 days_

## Pain point

Uzbek migrants in Russia/Korea are systematically underpaid. Recovery requires legal Russian-language documents. Lawyers in destination countries charge $300+. Daryo.uz publishes regular features showing the problem.

**Evidence**: https://daryo.uz/


**Target customer**: Uzbek migrant workers 25-50 in Moscow, St. Petersburg, Seoul, Istanbul who experienced 1-3 months unpaid wages


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $50.00
- **Year-1 target**: $24,000
- **Year-3 target**: $180,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Partner with one Moscow + one Seoul lawyer for template review
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: employer + amount + evidence
- [ ] Generate Russian/Korean demand letter + send by registered mail
- [ ] $50 per case via Click
- [ ] Cross-post in 'Uzbeks in Russia' Telegram + Daryo.uz comments


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid cases + 60% recovery rate in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: 'Uzbeks in Moscow' Telegram, Daryo.uz migrant chat, VK groups Uzbek diaspora


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click, Russian/Korean legal templates

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, legal-document drafting, diaspora outreach


## Moat potential

Diaspora-bridge brand; lawyer network + template library; can scale to migration support broadly.

## Risks

- **Legal complexity per destination** — _Mitigation_: Start with Russia, expand once stable
- **Recovery rate variance** — _Mitigation_: Set expectations; refund if no formal response
- **Migrants distrust digital channels** — _Mitigation_: WhatsApp + Telegram dual channel; testimonials
