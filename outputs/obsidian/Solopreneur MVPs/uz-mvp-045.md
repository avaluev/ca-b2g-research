# DiabetesCoachUZ

_Subscription Telegram coach for diabetic patients with Russian/Uzbek meal plans, glucose tracking, and dietitian Q&A_

## Pain point

Uzbekistan diabetes prevalence is rising (8%+ adults). Specialists are scarce; medication adherence is low. Patients need ongoing coaching; existing global apps lack Uzbek and Uzbek diet.

**Evidence**: https://kun.uz/ru/news/2024/06/12/zdorovie-uzbekistan


**Target customer**: Newly-diagnosed type-2 diabetics 40-65 in Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara wanting affordable structured support


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $8.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 9 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Partner with one Tashkent endocrinologist + dietitian ($400/mo retainer)
- [ ] Telegram channel + 1:1 weekly Q&A in Russian/Uzbek
- [ ] Meal plans + Uzbek-cuisine carb counts
- [ ] $8/mo subscription via Click
- [ ] Soft-launch via TBC SME health insurance partner


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 100 subscribers + 30 paying in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: TBC corporate-wellness Telegram, kun.uz health, Tashkent women's club


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Buttondown, Click, Notion (meal plan CMS)

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, endocrinologist network, content production


## Moat potential

Diabetic adherence + outcome data is gold; possible white-label to SMI Fund.

## Risks

- **Medical liability** — _Mitigation_: Educational positioning; doctor in the loop
- **Patient churn** — _Mitigation_: Annual prepay + outcome celebration
- **Content fatigue** — _Mitigation_: Cohort-style 12-week programs with graduations
