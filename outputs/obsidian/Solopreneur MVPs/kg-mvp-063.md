_Free Kyrgyz Telegram bot solving Grade 8-11 math step-by-step in Kyrgyz and Russian_

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Grade 8-11 students 14-18 in Naryn, Issyk-Kul, Talas preparing for ORT
