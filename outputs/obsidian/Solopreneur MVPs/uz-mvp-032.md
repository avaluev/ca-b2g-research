# GrainPriceUZ

_Daily Telegram digest of wheat and grain prices across 10 Uzbek bazaars and the state purchasing fund_

## Pain point

Wheat prices vary 15-25% across regions. Farmers and small mills sell or buy without market visibility. UzAgroMarket is a paid B2B service, not accessible to smallholders.

**Evidence**: https://uztrade.uz/


**Target customer**: Wheat farmers 35-60 in Jizzakh, Syrdarya, Fergana with 50-300 ha; small mill operators in oblast capitals


## Monetization

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $7.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 9 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Onboard 10 bazaar field reporters at $30/mo each via researcher contacts
- [ ] Telegram channel + email digest
- [ ] Free 7-day trial, $7/mo subscription
- [ ] Click subscription billing
- [ ] Soft-launch in 5 wheat-belt Telegram groups


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 200 free + 20 paid subscribers in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Jizzakh wheat-farmer Telegram, agro.uz forum, Uzdjm cooperative bulletins (Tashkent), spot.uz business


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Notion (reporter network), Buttondown, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, field reporter management, data hygiene


## Moat potential

Reporter network is the moat; can extend to fruit, vegetable bazaars; partner with WB Agri.

## Risks

- **Reporter accuracy** — _Mitigation_: Cross-check via 2 reporters per bazaar
- **Low willingness to pay** — _Mitigation_: Cooperative-level subscription
- **State competitor** — _Mitigation_: Lean on speed and bilingual delivery
