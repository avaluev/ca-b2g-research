# VotingDayUZ

_Squeeze page + Telegram bot reminding citizens of upcoming local mahalla elections, with candidate list and polling station map_

## Pain point

Mahalla raisi and local council elections happen on staggered schedules. Citizens often miss polling. There is no aggregated voter-info tool. UNDP-supported transparency initiatives create grant alignment.

**Evidence**: https://uza.uz/ru/posts/mahallinskie-vybory


**Target customer**: Civic-active mahalla residents 25-50 across Tashkent districts who want to vote informedly in mahalla elections


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $5,000
- **Year-3 target**: $35,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $20

**Steps:**
- [ ] Aggregate polling-station data from district hokimiyat sites
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram bot for reminders
- [ ] Add candidate list scraped from official channels
- [ ] UNDP-aligned grant pitch ($15-30K)
- [ ] Soft-launch in spot.uz civic chat


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 reminder signups by next election cycle
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz civic Telegram, Tashkent district groups


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram Bot, Notion data

**Capability required**: Russian and Uzbek-Latin, civic-tech network


## Moat potential

Civic-tech grant continuity; can extend to national elections cycle.

## Risks

- **Sensitive political topic** — _Mitigation_: Stay neutral, no candidate endorsement
- **Data collection limits** — _Mitigation_: Use only published sources
- **Low engagement** — _Mitigation_: Lean on partner NGOs for distribution
