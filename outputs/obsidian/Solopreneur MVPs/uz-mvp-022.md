# AzbukaGosUslug

_TikTok-native series: 60-second explainers of every government service Uzbeks need to know, from kindergarten enrollment to inheritance_

## Pain point

Younger Uzbek adults (Gen Z, 18-30) consume TikTok over text. Existing government communications are PDFs or static articles. No bilingual TikTok creator covers public services.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/08/15/tiktok-uzbekistan/


**Target customer**: Uzbek Gen Z (18-30), urban and small-city, encountering adult bureaucracy for the first time (apartment leases, marriages, first jobs)


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 14 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Record 30 60-second explainers in 2 weeks (founder + cousin/friend on camera)
- [ ] Post 1 video/day across TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts
- [ ] Monetize via TikTok Creator Fund + brand deals (TBC, Anorbank, Uzcard)
- [ ] Affiliate links to bank account opening + insurance
- [ ] Soft-launch via cross-promo with 3 mid-size UZ creators


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 10K TikTok followers + 2 brand deal inquiries in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days

**Capability required**: Uzbek + Russian on-camera, TikTok algo basics, video editing
