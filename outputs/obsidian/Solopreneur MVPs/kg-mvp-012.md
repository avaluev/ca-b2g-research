# GovHotlineRouterKG

_Free tool: given your problem in Russian/Kyrgyz, tells you which of the 30+ KG hotlines to call_

## Pain point

KG has 30+ thematic hotlines (1108 mayor, 112 emergency, 1304 anti-corruption). Citizens don't know which. Kaktus.media publishes annual cheat sheets.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Adult KG citizens 25-65 facing service problems


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $3,000
- **Year-3 target**: $25,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile master directory from gov.kg, kaktus.media, 24.kg
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram bot
- [ ] Voluntary tip via Mbank QR
- [ ] Soft-launch via kaktus.media + 24.kg


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 3,000 visits + 200 Telegram users in 30 days
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: kaktus.media, 24.kg, Bishkek civic Telegram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram Bot, Notion data

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, civic tech network


## Moat potential

De facto directory; sponsorship from partner SaaS.

## Risks

- **Hotline changes** — _Mitigation_: Crowdsourced corrections
- **Govt official directory** — _Mitigation_: Lean on Telegram-native
- **No revenue** — _Mitigation_: Donor-grant funding
