# UniApplyAssistantUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz: drafts foreign university application essays + recommendation letters for Uzbek students_

## Pain point

Uzbek students 17-22 applying to foreign universities lack English-language essay coaching. Private services in Tashkent charge $300-1000.

**Evidence**: https://kun.uz/ru/category/society/


**Target customer**: Uzbek students 17-22 applying to foreign universities (Korea, EU, UK, Russia); secondary: parents financing the application


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $60.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $120,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Hire 1-2 English-fluent Uzbek alums of foreign unis at $200/essay
- [ ] Telegram intake + Notion fulfillment
- [ ] $60 per essay revision; $200 for full SOP package
- [ ] Click + escrow
- [ ] Soft-launch in NUUz, INHA, Westminster student Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid essays in 60 days during application season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: NUUz Telegram, Westminster Tashkent, Chevening UZ Telegram


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Notion, Click

**Capability required**: English fluency, alumnus network, essay-coaching skills


## Moat potential

Alum network + outcome data compounds; success-stories market cycle.

## Risks

- **Quality variance** — _Mitigation_: Senior alum review for premium
- **Plagiarism concerns** — _Mitigation_: Custom essays, originality reports
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Multiple-deadline cycles + IELTS prep cross-sell
