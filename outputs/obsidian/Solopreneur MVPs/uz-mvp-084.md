# LastMileTashkent

_Wizard-of-Oz: Tashkent SMEs send 5-50 packages/day to last-mile riders via Telegram, manual dispatch_

## Pain point

Tashkent SMEs (online retailers, small wholesalers) deliver via Yandex/Maxim drivers ad-hoc — expensive and unreliable. Express24 + UzPost target enterprise. SMEs want simpler.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/logistics/


**Target customer**: Tashkent online retailers 5-30 employees doing 5-50 deliveries/day (clothing, F&B, electronics)


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $150,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 3-5 reliable Telegram-active riders
- [ ] Telegram bot intake from SMEs
- [ ] Founder dispatches via Notion
- [ ] $3 per delivery in Tashkent
- [ ] Soft-launch via TBC SME + Tashkent retail Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 5 SME clients + 200 deliveries in 30 days
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME, Tashkent retail Telegram, spot.uz commerce


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click

**Capability required**: Russian, logistics ops, rider network


## Moat potential

Rider network + SME book is the moat; possible scale to Yandex equivalent.

## Risks

- **Operational burden** — _Mitigation_: Productize after 200 deliveries/wk
- **Rider quality** — _Mitigation_: Performance bonuses + ratings
- **Yandex moves down market** — _Mitigation_: Lean on personal SME relationships
