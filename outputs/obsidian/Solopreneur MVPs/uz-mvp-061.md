# TutorMatchUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz: matches Tashkent students with verified university-prep tutors in Russian, Uzbek, English, and IT subjects_

## Pain point

DTM (Center for State Test) prep is critical for university entrance; tutors found via WhatsApp word-of-mouth. No verified marketplace exists. KOICA supports IT education skills programs creating partnership opportunity.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/06/02/dtm-tutors/


**Target customer**: Parents 38-55 of Grade 11 students applying to UZ universities or IT Park Junior; secondary: undergrads seeking IT mentorship


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $30.00
- **Year-1 target**: $16,000
- **Year-3 target**: $120,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 50 verified Tashkent tutors (DTM-prep, English, IT)
- [ ] Carrd directory + Telegram bot intake
- [ ] $30/mo featured listing for tutors; or $15/match commission
- [ ] Click payment
- [ ] Soft-launch in Tashkent parent groups + IT Park UZ


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 successful matches + 10 paid tutor listings in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent parent Telegram, DTM-prep groups, IT Park UZ


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Telegram Bot, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, tutor verification, B2B sales


## Moat potential

Tutor + family network compounds; possible scale to other oblast capitals.

## Risks

- **Tutor quality variance** — _Mitigation_: Verified credentials + parent reviews
- **Direct WhatsApp bypass** — _Mitigation_: Charge tutor not parent; ongoing value via reviews
- **DTM rule changes** — _Mitigation_: Stay close to Center for State Test
