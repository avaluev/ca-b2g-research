# GovHireUZ

_Job board listing all civil-service open positions across Uzbek ministries — first dedicated GovTech career site_

## Pain point

Civil service openings are scattered across ministry sites, vacancy.uz, gov.uz. UP-189 forces ministries to hire AI/digital specialists rapidly — most postings are buried. IT Park developers report 'I would work GovTech but can't find roles' on Telegram.

**Evidence**: https://vacancy.uz/


**Target customer**: Junior-mid IT professionals (24-35) in Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara who want stability + impact in civil service or donor PIUs
