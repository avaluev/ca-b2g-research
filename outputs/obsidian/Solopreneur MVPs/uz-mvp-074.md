# PortfolioBuilderUZ

_Web app: Uzbek IT freelancers paste their work into a polished GitHub-style English portfolio in 10 minutes_

## Pain point

Uzbek freelancers/students struggle to communicate their work in English-language portfolios. Existing tools (Notion, Webflow) require design skills. KOICA-supported youth IT employment creates partnership opportunity.

**Evidence**: https://it-park.uz/


**Target customer**: Uzbek developers + designers 22-35 in IT Park targeting foreign clients on Upwork, foreign agencies


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $5.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build templated portfolio generator (Next.js + Vercel)
- [ ] GPT-4o paraphrasing for clean English
- [ ] Free 1 portfolio, $5/mo for unlimited + custom domain
- [ ] Click + Stripe billing
- [ ] Soft-launch in IT Park UZ Telegram + uzcloud Slack


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 200 free + 30 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: IT Park UZ Telegram, uzcloud Slack, Habr UZ


**Tech stack**: Next.js + Vercel, Supabase, GPT-4o, Click + Stripe

**Capability required**: Russian + English, Next.js, design sensibility


## Moat potential

Portfolio + freelancer network; affiliate to Stripe/Wise + Upwork referral.

## Risks

- **Free competitors (Notion, Webflow)** — _Mitigation_: Lean on Uzbek-Latin onboarding + IT Park integration
- **Low conversion** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with FreelancerInvoiceUZ
- **Quality of generated portfolios** — _Mitigation_: Curated templates + GPT-4o pass
