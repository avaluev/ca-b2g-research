# EPIGUForBusiness

_Carrd landing page that lists the top 30 EPIGU services for SMEs with one-click direct deep links_

## Pain point

EPIGU has a citizen-centric layout and SME services are buried 4-5 clicks deep. SME owners (per spot.uz business reader survey) waste 20-30 min per service. No bookmarkable directory exists.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/


**Target customer**: Tashkent and Samarkand SME owners and accountants (10-50 employee firms) who use EPIGU monthly for VAT, social-tax, payroll filings


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $25.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 4 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Manually map 30 highest-value SME services on EPIGU
- [ ] Carrd directory page with deep links and Russian/Uzbek descriptions
- [ ] Add 3 sponsored slots for accounting SaaS / banks at $25/month
- [ ] SEO with kun.uz/spot.uz business keywords
- [ ] Cross-post in TBC SME Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 2,000 visits in 30 days, 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME Telegram, Buxgalter Plus community, ICT Week 2026


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Plausible analytics

**Capability required**: Russian and Uzbek-Latin, basic SEO, B2B sponsor outreach


## Moat potential

First-mover SME directory; sponsorship list compounds; can spin into a 'EPIGU power-user newsletter' premium product.

## Risks

- **EPIGU URL changes** — _Mitigation_: Monitor changes weekly
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Pivot to affiliate model with banks
- **Out-competed by accounting SaaS** — _Mitigation_: Partner with one as co-publisher
