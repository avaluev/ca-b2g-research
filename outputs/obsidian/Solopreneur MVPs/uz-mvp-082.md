# BorderWaitUZ

_Live Telegram bot showing wait times at UZ-KZ, UZ-KG, UZ-TJ, UZ-TM, UZ-AF border crossings, crowd-sourced_

## Pain point

Border-crossing wait times unpredictable, especially Uzbekistan-Kazakhstan. Truck drivers and migrants lose 6-12h. No live feed exists; informal Telegram channels do crude job.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/logistics/


**Target customer**: Truck drivers + freight forwarders 30-60; secondary: cross-border migrants returning


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $4,000
- **Year-3 target**: $30,000


## MVR plan (ad_booking)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build Telegram bot: report wait time + photo
- [ ] Aggregate + display per-crossing live
- [ ] Donation tip jar (Click)
- [ ] Soft-launch in trucker WhatsApp + customs forum
- [ ] Sponsor logistics SaaS at $80/mo


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 active reporters + 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: UZ trucker WhatsApp, Andijan migrant Telegram, logistics forum Habr


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase, Mapbox

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, trucker network outreach


## Moat potential

Crowdsourced data + B2B logistics sponsorship; possible OEM to State Customs Committee.

## Risks

- **Reporter quality** — _Mitigation_: Multi-reporter cross-check
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Tip jar baseline
- **Coverage gaps** — _Mitigation_: Focus on top 3 crossings first
