# MentalHealthHotlineUZ

_Anonymous, paid Telegram support service connecting Uzbeks with Russian/Uzbek-speaking mental health professionals_

## Pain point

Mental health is taboo + underserved. State counselors cluster in Tashkent. Rural Uzbeks have no anonymous channel. Spot.uz publishes annual mental-health features showing demand.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/10/10/mental-health/


**Target customer**: Urban professionals 22-40 dealing with anxiety/depression/burnout; secondary: returning migrants with PTSD


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $18.00
- **Year-1 target**: $14,000
- **Year-3 target**: $120,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 5 Russian/Uzbek-speaking psychologists at $300/mo retainer
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: anonymous, picks language and gender
- [ ] 30-min session at 220,000 UZS
- [ ] Click payment, anonymous escrow
- [ ] Soft-launch via TBC corporate-wellness partnership


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 sessions + NPS 8+ in 30 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: TBC corporate Telegram, Tashkent women community, kun.uz wellness chat


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Calendly, Click, Notion (psychologist roster)

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, psychologist network, anonymous-protocol design


## Moat potential

Psychologist + customer trust compounds; possible spin-out as a localized BetterHelp.

## Risks

- **Liability on crisis cases** — _Mitigation_: Crisis escalation protocol; partner with one hospital
- **Stigma** — _Mitigation_: Anonymous + safe-space messaging
- **Psychologist burnout** — _Mitigation_: Roster of 5+ + rotation
