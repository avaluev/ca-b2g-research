# VotingDayKG

_Squeeze page + Telegram bot reminding citizens of upcoming local elections with candidate list and polling station map_

## Pain point

Local council elections happen on staggered schedules. Citizens miss. UNDP supports civic engagement.

**Evidence**: https://shailoo.gov.kg/


**Target customer**: Civic-active Kyrgyz residents 25-50 across regions wanting to vote informedly in local elections


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $4,000
- **Year-3 target**: $25,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $20

**Steps:**
- [ ] Aggregate polling-station data from CEC KG
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram bot
- [ ] Add candidate list
- [ ] UNDP grant pitch ($15K)
- [ ] Soft-launch in kaktus.media community + 24.kg


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 reminder signups by next election cycle
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: kaktus.media, 24.kg, Bishkek civic Telegram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram Bot, Notion data

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, civic tech


## Moat potential

Civic-tech grant continuity.

## Risks

- **Sensitive topic** — _Mitigation_: Stay neutral; no endorsements
- **Low engagement** — _Mitigation_: NGO partner distribution
- **Data limits** — _Mitigation_: Published sources only
