# TeacherSubBoardUZ

_Directory of substitute teachers available for Tashkent schools, with daily/hourly rates and verified credentials_

## Pain point

Tashkent schools struggle to find substitute teachers when staff are sick. Phone-based scramble dominates. No central board.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/edu/


**Target customer**: Tashkent and oblast school principals + HR; secondary: substitute teachers (often retired or part-time) seeking gigs


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $20.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $40

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 50 verified substitute teachers in Tashkent
- [ ] Carrd directory + Telegram alert bot
- [ ] Free for principals; $20/mo for teachers wanting featured listing
- [ ] Click payment
- [ ] Soft-launch in 'Uchitelya Uzbekistana' Telegram + Tashkent district education


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 50 teacher signups + 5 schools using in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: 'Uchitelya Uzbekistana' Telegram, Tashkent district education


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Telegram Bot, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, credential verification


## Moat potential

Trust + network compounds.

## Risks

- **Public schools resist private intermediary** — _Mitigation_: Start with private + bilingual schools
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Charge teachers, not schools
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Year-round demand from sick days + maternity
