# VetCallUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz: photo + voice description of sick livestock → 24h vet response in Russian/Uzbek for $5_

## Pain point

Rural Uzbekistan has 1 vet per 5,000 livestock units. Farmers wait 2-5 days; livestock loss is common. Local vet WhatsApp groups exist but charge ad-hoc.

**Evidence**: https://agro.uz/


**Target customer**: Smallholder livestock farmers 35-60 in Khorezm, Karakalpakstan, Surxondaryo with 5-50 cattle / sheep


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $5.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $150

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 3 part-time vets (vet college graduates) at $200/mo retainer
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: photo + voice + species
- [ ] Vets respond within 24h
- [ ] Click payment, 60,000 UZS per consult
- [ ] Soft-launch in livestock farmer Telegram groups


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid consults + 5-star rating in 30 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: livestock farmer Telegram, agro.uz, Surxondaryo cattle association


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click, Google Drive (vet handbook)

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, vet network management


## Moat potential

Vet network is the moat; can extend to medication delivery via local pharmacy partner.

## Risks

- **Vet liability** — _Mitigation_: Disclaimer + emergency-call routing for severe cases
- **Vet retention** — _Mitigation_: Performance bonuses + reputation tiers
- **Regulatory** — _Mitigation_: Position as 'preliminary advice' + refer to in-person vet
