# GovHotlineRouter

_Free tool that, given your problem in Uzbek/Russian, tells you exactly which of the 50+ Uzbek government hotlines to call_

## Pain point

Uzbekistan has 50+ thematic hotlines (1198 mahalla, 1142 Anti-corruption, 1105 Tax, 1006 Health, etc.). Citizens don't know which to call; many wrong-number complaints. Spot.uz publishes annual 'hotline cheat sheet' that goes viral every January.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/01/15/uzbekistan-hotlines/


**Target customer**: Adult Uzbek citizens 25-65 facing a service problem (utility, social, legal, tax) and unsure which authority to call


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $4,000
- **Year-3 target**: $30,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile master directory of all hotlines from gov.uz, spot.uz, kun.uz
- [ ] Build a Carrd page with a 1-line problem input -> top 3 hotlines
- [ ] Telegram bot for mobile-first usage
- [ ] Voluntary donation via Click
- [ ] Soft-launch with spot.uz cross-post


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 5,000 page visits + 200 Telegram users in 30 days
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz, kun.uz Telegram, Tashkent civic Telegram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram Bot, Notion (data source), Click donation

**Capability required**: Uzbek + Russian, civic-tech network


## Moat potential

Becomes the de facto hotline directory; partnership with Public Services Agency for affiliated badge; ad slots for partner SaaS.

## Risks

- **Hotline changes break tool** — _Mitigation_: Crowdsourced corrections + quarterly audit
- **Government launches official directory** — _Mitigation_: Lean into UX speed and Telegram-native delivery
- **No revenue** — _Mitigation_: Pivot to grant-funded civic tool
