# PermitFinderKG

_Squeeze page: 3 questions and email back the exact list of permits you need to open a small business in Bishkek_

## Pain point

First-time entrepreneurs face 5-10 permits in KG. EBRD SME digital initiative supports this gap. Kaktus.media publishes annual guides.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Aspiring Bishkek and Osh small-business owners 22-40, especially women returning to workforce + migrants opening shops


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $9.00
- **Year-1 target**: $10,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile permit checklist from State Tax Service + SAII
- [ ] Carrd squeeze page
- [ ] Auto-email Russian + Kyrgyz PDF
- [ ] $9 paid 30-min call upsell
- [ ] $50 Instagram ad targeting Bishkek 25-40 'biznes ochuu'


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 100 conversions + 10 paid calls in 30 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: High Tech Park Telegram, kaktus.media business, Bishkek women business community, Instagram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Mailerlite, Notion + Zapier, Optima/Mbank

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, permit research, email funnel


## Moat potential

Email list + bank affiliate referrals.

## Risks

- **Permit changes** — _Mitigation_: Monthly review + law firm partner
- **Govt competitor via SAII** — _Mitigation_: Lean on personalization
- **Low ad conversion** — _Mitigation_: Pivot to organic Telegram
