# FreightTrackerKG

_SaaS for small Kyrgyz logistics firms: GPS tracking + driver mobile app for $25/mo, no hardware_

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Small KG logistics firms 5-50 trucks doing China-Russia corridor; owner-operators

- **Model**: subscription
- **Price point**: $25.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000
