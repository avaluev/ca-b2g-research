# AyilCommunityFeed

_Free Telegram channel + bot listing every public hearing and ayil okmotu meeting in your district_

## Pain point

Ayil okmotu (rural municipality) meetings + hokimiyat receptions invisible to citizens. EU-GIZ supports local governance + decentralization. Existing notice boards underused.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Civic-active rural Kyrgyz residents 25-55 in Naryn, Issyk-Kul, Talas; secondary: ayil okmotu staff


## Monetization

- **Model**: donation
- **Price point**: $0.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $40,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Map ayil okmotu in 3 oblasts via volunteer aksakals
- [ ] Telegram channels per district
- [ ] Notion-driven publishing pipeline
- [ ] EU-GIZ grant pitch ($25K)
- [ ] Soft-launch via kaktus.media + 24.kg community


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 subscribers across 3 districts in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: kaktus.media community, 24.kg, ayil okmotu WhatsApp via researcher contacts


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Notion, Buttondown

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, civic outreach, content cadence


## Moat potential

Hyperlocal subscriber loyalty; donor-grant continuity.

## Risks

- **Information drying up** — _Mitigation_: Build network of 30+ aksakals
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Lean on EU-GIZ grant
- **Politically sensitive in some regions** — _Mitigation_: Stay neutral; partner with established NGOs
