# EventCalendarUZ

_Free Telegram channel listing every Tashkent professional event — meetups, conferences, donor events — with RSVP_

## Pain point

Tashkent has growing IT/business event scene (ICT Week, IT Park meetups, donor launches) but no aggregator. Founders/employees miss key networking. Spot.uz publishes events but reactively.

**Evidence**: https://it-park.uz/


**Target customer**: Tashkent professionals 22-50 in IT, business, NGO, donor — networking-active


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $100.00
- **Year-1 target**: $7,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Aggregate events from IT Park, ICT Week, Buyuk Kelajak, donor calendars
- [ ] Telegram channel + email digest
- [ ] $100/event for sponsored event boost
- [ ] Soft-launch via IT Park UZ + ICT Week 2026


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 subscribers + 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: IT Park UZ, ICT Week 2026, spot.uz business


**Tech stack**: Telegram, Buttondown

**Capability required**: Russian + English, event-curation


## Moat potential

Event-curator brand; sponsorship from event organizers.

## Risks

- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Charge organizers for promo
- **Competition from Eventbrite** — _Mitigation_: Lean on local-language curation
