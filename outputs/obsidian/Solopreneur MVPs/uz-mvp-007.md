# PermitPathfinder

_Squeeze page that asks 3 business questions and emails back the exact list of permits you need to open a bakery, salon or shop in Tashkent_

## Pain point

Aspiring entrepreneurs face 8-15 permits to start a small business in Tashkent. The official 'business desk' (бизнес-маслахат) is overloaded. Spot.uz business section runs weekly explainers because demand is constant.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/02/11/biznes-permits/


**Target customer**: Aspiring small business owners 22-40 in Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara — including women returning to workforce and migrant workers using savings to open shops


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $9.00
- **Year-1 target**: $14,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000


## MVR plan (squeeze_page)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile permit checklist for top 12 small business types from soliq.uz, hokimiyat.uz, EPIGU
- [ ] Carrd squeeze page with 3-question intake (business type / city / ownership form)
- [ ] Auto-email PDF checklist using Zapier + Notion + Mailerlite
- [ ] Upsell: paid 30-min call ($9) for live walkthrough
- [ ] Run $50 Instagram ad targeting Tashkent women 25-40 'biznes ochish'


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 150 squeeze-page conversions in 30 days, 10 paid calls by day 45
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz Telegram, Instagram Tashkent women business community, TBC Bank entrepreneur Telegram, ICT Week 2026 startup track


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion + Zapier, Mailerlite, Click for paid call upsell

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek written content, permit research, Mailerlite operations


## Moat potential

Email list of intent-verified entrepreneurs becomes lead source for partner banks (acquiring referral $30-50/customer) and accounting service partners.

## Risks

- **Permit rules change frequently** — _Mitigation_: Monthly review, partner with one Tashkent law firm for accuracy
- **Officially-backed competitor launches** — _Mitigation_: Lean on personalization and bilingual delivery; integrate Octobank acquiring referrals to differentiate
- **Low ad-budget conversion** — _Mitigation_: Pivot to organic Telegram channel + content marketing
