# MedReminderKG

_Free Telegram bot reminding patients to take meds, with Kyrgyz/Russian voice prompts and family caregiver loop_

## Pain point

Chronic-disease patients miss doses. Adult children manage parents remotely. Existing apps lack Kyrgyz.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Adult children 30-50 caring for elderly parents (60-85)


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $2.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] Telegram bot intake meds + schedule + caregiver phone
- [ ] OpenAI TTS Kyrgyz + Russian
- [ ] Family-loop SMS via Twilio
- [ ] Free 2 patients, $2/mo unlimited
- [ ] Soft-launch via Mbank family Telegram + 24.kg health


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 200 reminders + 25 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Mbank family, 24.kg health, Bishkek caregiver


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, OpenAI TTS, Twilio, Supabase

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, TTS


## Moat potential

Adherence data; pharma + insurance partnerships.

- **Adoption friction** — _Mitigation_: Caregiver-driven
