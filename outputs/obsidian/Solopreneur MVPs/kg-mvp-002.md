# FormFillerKG

_Wizard-of-Oz: send photo of Kyrgyz government form via Telegram, get it back filled and ready for Tunduk submission in 4h_

## Pain point

Rural Kyrgyz citizens (Osh, Naryn, Issyk-Kul) struggle with Russian-language government forms. They pay neighborhood notaries 200-500 KGS per form. No online alternative exists.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Rural adults 35-65 in Osh, Naryn, Issyk-Kul applying for pensions, land cadastre, marriage certificates


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: photo of form + voice description
- [ ] Notion-based fulfillment, hire 1 part-time AUCA student at $250/mo
- [ ] $3/form, $5/full submission
- [ ] Optima/Mbank acquiring
- [ ] Run $80 Instagram ad targeting Osh women 30-55


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid orders in 21 days at $3-5 ACV
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: Osh Telegram parent groups, Naryn community Telegram, Bishkek expat WhatsApp, kaktus.media


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Optima Bank acquiring, Google Drive for templates

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, manage 1-2 part-time fulfillment, Optima/Mbank acquiring setup


## Moat potential

Library of 200+ pre-filled form templates becomes productized SaaS; brand recognition with rural customers; donor partner via WB Identity project.

## Risks

- **Notary lobby pushback** — _Mitigation_: Position as 'digital assistant' not legal service
- **Tunduk API closes off** — _Mitigation_: Pivot to form-only + customer self-upload
- **Personal data handling** — _Mitigation_: Auto-delete forms after 7 days
