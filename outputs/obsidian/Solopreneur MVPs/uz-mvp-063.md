# MathTutorUZ

_Free Uzbek-language Telegram bot solving Grade 8-11 math problems step-by-step in Uzbek-Latin and Russian_

## Pain point

Uzbek schools have weak math instruction in rural areas. Parents can't help. Photomath/Mathway are English. Spot.uz education section reports demand.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/edu/


**Target customer**: Grade 8-11 students 14-18 in Andijan, Bukhara, Khorezm preparing for university entrance


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] GPT-4o vision for math photo + Uzbek/Russian step-by-step
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: photo of problem
- [ ] Free 5/day, $3/mo unlimited + DTM-prep mode
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch in DTM-prep + parent Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 500 free + 30 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: DTM-prep Telegram, Andijan parent groups, kun.uz education


**Tech stack**: GPT-4o vision, Telegram Bot, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, math-domain familiarity


## Moat potential

Uzbek-localized step-by-step library compounds; donor-grant viable (IsDB SmartEd).

## Risks

- **AI accuracy** — _Mitigation_: Math-validator + user feedback
- **Low conversion** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with TutorMatchUZ
- **Competition from English apps** — _Mitigation_: Uzbek + Russian first-class
