# SamarkandRestoFinder

_Carrd directory of vetted Samarkand and Bukhara restaurants with English menus, halal/vegetarian filters, and direct WhatsApp_

## Pain point

Samarkand has 200+ restaurants but only 20-30 have Google Maps presence with English menus. Tourists rely on hotel concierge or random walk-ins. No vetted directory exists.

**Evidence**: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g293964-Samarkand_Samarkand_Province.html


**Target customer**: Tourists 25-60 in Samarkand and Bukhara for 1-3 nights wanting authentic + reliable dining


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $25.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 10 days
- **Build cost**: $150

**Steps:**
- [ ] Visit + photograph 30 Samarkand and 20 Bukhara restaurants
- [ ] Carrd directory with English menus + price tier + halal/veg
- [ ] $25/mo for featured listing + reservations link
- [ ] Click + Stripe
- [ ] Soft-launch via Lonely Planet + TripAdvisor forum


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 3,000 visits + 5 paid restaurants in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Lonely Planet UZ, TripAdvisor Samarkand, Bookmundi, Tashkent expat WhatsApp


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Plausible, Click

**Capability required**: English + Russian + Uzbek-Latin, field visits, B2B sales to restaurant owners


## Moat potential

Quality curation + sponsor network; can extend to other Silk Road cities.

## Risks

- **Initial outreach time** — _Mitigation_: Allocate 10 days field visit
- **Low restaurant payment** — _Mitigation_: Volume pricing, 6-month deals
- **Outdated menus** — _Mitigation_: Quarterly photo refresh
