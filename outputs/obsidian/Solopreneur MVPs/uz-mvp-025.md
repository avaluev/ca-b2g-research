# OneTouchPensionUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz service helping retired Uzbeks check, transfer, and dispute pension entitlements via Pension Fund e-services_

## Pain point

Pension Fund Uzbekistan moved to e-services but most retirees (3M+ in active receipt) struggle with the portal. Their adult children call the call center on their behalf, often during work hours. Spot.uz forum 'pension digital' threads run constantly.

**Evidence**: https://pf.uz/ru


**Target customer**: Adult children (35-55) in Tashkent and Andijan supporting parents (60-80) with pension matters, especially after a parent's residency move or spouse death


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $12.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $110,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile top 10 pension-related tasks (transfer, dispute, certificate)
- [ ] Telegram bot intake + power-of-attorney template
- [ ] Founder + 1 student handle backend within 24h
- [ ] Click payment, 150,000 UZS per task
- [ ] Cross-post in Andijan parent groups + spot.uz seniors community


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 15 paid tasks in 30 days
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: Andijan parent Telegram groups, spot.uz seniors, Mahalla raisi referrals


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click, Google Drive for PoA templates

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, pension domain familiarity, patience with elderly callers


## Moat potential

Productize into a $30/year pension-management subscription service for adult children of retirees; align with WB social protection program.

## Risks

- **Power-of-attorney legal complexity** — _Mitigation_: Partner with one Tashkent notary for template review
- **Low margins given operational time** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with FormFiller UZ to share fulfillment ops
- **Sensitive customer base — fraud risk** — _Mitigation_: Limit to verified family-relation submissions
