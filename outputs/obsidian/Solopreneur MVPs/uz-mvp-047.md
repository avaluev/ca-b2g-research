# PharmacyDirectoryUZ

_Searchable directory of 12,000+ Uzbek pharmacies with stock-availability search for hard-to-find drugs_

## Pain point

Patients call 5-10 pharmacies hunting rare drugs. No central availability tool. Forum threads on spot.uz routinely ask 'who has X drug?'

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/health/


**Target customer**: Tashkent patients 30-65 hunting rare meds, especially imported drugs and chronic-disease specialty meds


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $15.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 500 Tashkent pharmacies (Apteka Plus, Med Plus, etc.) with daily-stock submission
- [ ] Carrd searchable directory + Telegram bot
- [ ] Free for patients, $15/mo for pharmacies wanting featured + bulk listings
- [ ] Soft-launch via spot.uz + pharmacy distributor Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 3,000 visits + 5 paid pharmacies in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz health, pharmacy distributor Telegram, Tashkent senior Telegram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS + Airtable, Telegram Bot

**Capability required**: Russian, field outreach to pharmacies


## Moat potential

Becomes the 'Yandex Maps of pharmacies'; sponsorship from PharmacyShelfUZ user base.

## Risks

- **Stock data goes stale** — _Mitigation_: Daily check-in mechanism; commission for accurate updates
- **Pharmacies don't pay** — _Mitigation_: Lean on volume/free tier; B2B distributor sponsorship
- **Coverage gaps in oblasts** — _Mitigation_: Regional rollout, prove model first in Tashkent
