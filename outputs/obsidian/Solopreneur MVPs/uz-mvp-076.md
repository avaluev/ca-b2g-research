# SilkRoadGuideUZ

_AI-powered Telegram bot delivering UNESCO Samarkand/Bukhara/Khiva tour itineraries in 6 languages with audio narration_

## Pain point

Tourist arrivals 8M+ in 2024. Multilingual audio guides exist for top 5 sites but not for broader Silk Road. Tour guides charge $50-200/day. AI tools fill the gap.

**Evidence**: https://uzbektourism.uz/


**Target customer**: Independent tourists 25-65 from EU, Korea, China, Russia visiting UZ for 5-14 days; secondary: backpackers and digital nomads


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $9.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $150,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 12 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile UNESCO + cultural-heritage content for top 30 sites
- [ ] GPT-4o + ElevenLabs TTS for 6 languages (EN, RU, KO, ZH, FR, DE)
- [ ] Telegram mini-app + audio download offline
- [ ] Free 1 city, $9 for full country
- [ ] Soft-launch via Bookmundi UZ + Lonely Planet UZ FB groups


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 500 free + 50 paid in 60 days during shoulder season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Lonely Planet UZ, TripAdvisor Tashkent forum, Tashkent expat WhatsApp, ICT Week 2026 tourism track


**Tech stack**: Telegram Mini App, GPT-4o, ElevenLabs TTS, Supabase, Stripe

**Capability required**: English + Russian, tourism content awareness, TTS engineering


## Moat potential

Multilingual content library compounds; partnership with state tourism agency Uzbektourism for branding; can extend to Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan.

## Risks

- **Misinformation on cultural details** — _Mitigation_: Historian advisor + UNESCO source review
- **Free competitors (Google Maps audio)** — _Mitigation_: Lean on offline + 6 languages
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: B2B partnership with hotels for in-room subscription
