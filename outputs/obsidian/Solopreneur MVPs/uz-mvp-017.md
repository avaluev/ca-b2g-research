# OneStopComplaint

_Wizard-of-Oz human-in-loop service that turns your phone-photo complaint (utility, road, mahalla) into a properly-formatted petition to the right authority_

## Pain point

Citizen complaints are routinely rejected for wrong format or wrong authority. Legal-aid centers exist but require visit + 2-week wait. Spot.uz publishes annual 'how to write a жалоба' guide because demand is constant.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2025/05/14/zhaloba-guide/


**Target customer**: Working-class Tashkent and Andijan adults aged 30-60 with a specific grievance (broken elevator, refused refund, unpaid wages)


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $8.00
- **Year-1 target**: $16,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: photo + voice description + recipient
- [ ] Notion-based fulfillment by founder + 1 paralegal student
- [ ] Click payment, 100,000 UZS per complaint
- [ ] Soft-launch with Tashkent district groups and Telegram civic chat
- [ ] Pitch UNDP for $30K legal-aid grant alignment


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid complaints in 30 days, 60% resolution rate
- **Window**: 45 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz civic Telegram, Tashkent district groups, Andijan Telegram parents


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click acquiring, Uzbek-Latin and Russian templates

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, legal writing basics, managing 1-2 part-time paralegals


## Moat potential

Library of complaint templates by issue type; can productize into self-serve later.

## Risks

- **Legal liability on wrongly-filed petitions** — _Mitigation_: Disclaimer + review by partner law firm
- **Notary lobby pushback** — _Mitigation_: Position as 'document-prep service' not legal advice
- **Low margins** — _Mitigation_: Premium tier with follow-up phone calls
