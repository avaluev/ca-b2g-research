# SymptomCheckerKG

_Free Kyrgyz-language symptom checker delivering triage levels (self-care / GP / ER) and nearest clinic_

## Pain point

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Adult Kyrgyz 25-55 with symptoms but no GP access
