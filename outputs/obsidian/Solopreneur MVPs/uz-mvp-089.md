# EcoTourUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz curator: hand-picked eco-tours of Aral, Chimgan, Zaamin, with 1:1 trip planning in English/Russian_

## Pain point

Foreign tourists 30-55 want eco-experiences (Aral basin, Chimgan, Zaamin) but can't find vetted operators. Big tour ops focus on Samarkand. Lonely Planet UZ has minimal eco-tour content.

**Evidence**: https://uzbektourism.uz/


**Target customer**: Eco-conscious tourists 30-55 from EU/Korea/Japan/Russia spending $1K-3K for 5-10 days in Uzbekistan


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $200.00
- **Year-1 target**: $16,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Partner with 3 local eco-guides (Aral, Chimgan, Zaamin) on revenue split
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram intake
- [ ] $200 trip-planning fee + 10% commission on trip cost
- [ ] Click + bank wire for foreign payment
- [ ] Soft-launch via Lonely Planet + Tashkent expat WhatsApp


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 5 paid trips in 90 days
- **Window**: 90 days
- **Channels**: Lonely Planet UZ, TripAdvisor Tashkent, Tashkent expat WhatsApp, ICT Week 2026 tourism track


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion, Click + Stripe Atlas + Wise

**Capability required**: English + Russian + Uzbek-Latin, guide network, trip planning


## Moat potential

Eco-guide network + foreign-tourist book; can scale to Kyrgyzstan + Tajikistan.
