# SchoolEnrollerUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz service: helps Tashkent parents enroll their child in a desired public school via maktab.uz_

## Pain point

School enrollment moves online (maktab.uz) but slot system is opaque, popular schools fill in hours. Parents miss windows. Spot.uz publishes annual 'how to enroll' guides.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/06/12/maktab-uz/


**Target customer**: Tashkent parents 28-45 enrolling kids in Grade 1 or transferring schools; secondary: parents in Samarkand, Bukhara


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $12.00
- **Year-1 target**: $14,000
- **Year-3 target**: $90,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Map maktab.uz process for Tashkent districts
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: child + preferred schools + documents
- [ ] Founder + 1 part-time fulfillment monitors slots, books fast
- [ ] Click 150,000 UZS per enrollment
- [ ] Soft-launch in Tashkent parent groups before April enrollment cycle


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid enrollments in 60 days during peak season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent parent Telegram, Mamamylar parenting, spot.uz education


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click, Playwright for slot monitoring

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, maktab.uz process awareness


## Moat potential

Seasonal cash flow; can extend to kindergarten + university enrollment.

## Risks

- **System changes** — _Mitigation_: Monthly process check
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Diversify with PassportAppointmentBuddy
- **Liability** — _Mitigation_: Refund if no slot secured
