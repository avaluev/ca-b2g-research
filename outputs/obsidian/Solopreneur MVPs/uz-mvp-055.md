# BankRateUZ

_Daily UZ Telegram digest: best deposit and consumer-loan rates across 12 Uzbek banks_

## Pain point

Uzbek savers compare deposit rates manually across bank sites. Rates change weekly. Spot.uz finance publishes monthly 'best deposits' articles. No daily aggregator exists.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/finance/


**Target customer**: Middle-class Uzbeks 30-60 with $5K-50K savings; secondary: HR managers comparing payroll-card programs


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $80.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (manifesto)

- **Build time**: 5 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Scrape bank deposit-rate pages daily
- [ ] Telegram channel + email digest
- [ ] Sponsored slot for one bank/week ($80)
- [ ] Affiliate to bank account opening (Anorbank, TBC)
- [ ] Soft-launch in TBC SME finance Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,500 subscribers + 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME, spot.uz finance, ICT Week 2026 fintech track


**Tech stack**: Python scraping, Telegram, Buttondown

**Capability required**: Russian, scraping, B2B sponsor sales


## Moat potential

Brand authority; affiliate revenue compounds; partner with one bank for white-label.

## Risks

- **Banks block scraping** — _Mitigation_: Manual sourcing + bank-relations agreements
- **Low sponsor demand** — _Mitigation_: Lean on affiliate
- **Rate-watcher fatigue** — _Mitigation_: Add deposit-rate forecast for premium tier
