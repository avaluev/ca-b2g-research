# UzbekHistoryGameUZ

_Free Telegram quiz bot teaching Uzbek history and culture to Grade 5-8 students with daily 10-question challenges_

## Pain point

School curriculum has weak Uzbek-history retention. Children prefer mobile games. ISDB SmartEd supports edutainment. Tashkent and oblast parents want supplementary cultural learning.

**Evidence**: https://www.isdb.org/


**Target customer**: Grade 5-8 students 10-14 across Uzbekistan; parents using as educational supplement


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $2.00
- **Year-1 target**: $6,000
- **Year-3 target**: $50,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 500-question Uzbek history bank in Russian + Uzbek
- [ ] Telegram bot daily quiz + leaderboard
- [ ] Free with leaderboard, $2/mo for parent dashboard + topic-level tracking
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch via parent Telegram + IsDB SmartEd partner schools


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 500 daily-active + 50 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent parent Telegram, Mamamylar parenting, kun.uz education


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, history domain familiarity


## Moat potential

Question bank + community compounds; donor-grant viable.

## Risks

- **Content accuracy** — _Mitigation_: Historian advisor review
- **Low retention** — _Mitigation_: Leaderboard + parent dashboard
- **Government competitor** — _Mitigation_: Lean on game design
