# DTMQuestionBank

_Free Telegram bot serving daily Uzbek-language DTM-style practice questions with explanations_

## Pain point

DTM (Center for State Test) is the bottleneck for university entrance. Question banks exist on paper or in expensive private platforms ($30/mo+). Free, accessible, Telegram-native is missing.

**Evidence**: https://dtm.uz/


**Target customer**: Grade 11 students 17-18 across Uzbekistan preparing for DTM in May; secondary: Grade 10 students starting prep


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $4.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 500 sample DTM questions across 6 subjects
- [ ] Telegram bot daily-question feature
- [ ] Free 3/day, $4/mo unlimited + tracking
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch in DTM-prep + parent Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 users + 50 paid in 60 days during prep season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: DTM-prep Telegram, Andijan/Fergana parent groups, MathTutorUZ user base


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, DTM domain familiarity


## Moat potential

Question bank is the moat; cross-promo with TutorMatchUZ, MathTutorUZ.

## Risks

- **Copyright on DTM questions** — _Mitigation_: Generate original DTM-style questions, not copies
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Year-round Grade 9-11 with cohort
- **Free competitors** — _Mitigation_: Lean on UX + tracking
