_Carrd directory of vetted Bishkek restaurants with English menus, halal/vegetarian filters, direct WhatsApp_

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Tourists 25-60 in Bishkek for 1-3 nights wanting authentic + reliable
