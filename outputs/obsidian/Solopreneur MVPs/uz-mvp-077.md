# TashkentDayPlanner

_Free Telegram tool: tells you 6 best things to do today in Tashkent based on weather, day of week, and your interests_

## Pain point

Tashkent visitors and weekend Uzbeks struggle to plan. TripAdvisor is generic, English-only. Spot.uz publishes weekly 'what to do' but static.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/lifestyle/


**Target customer**: Tashkent visitors 25-65 + locals 25-45 looking for spontaneous weekend plans


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $60.00
- **Year-1 target**: $8,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 200 Tashkent activities with metadata
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: budget + interests
- [ ] Daily curated 6-item list
- [ ] Sponsored slot for restaurants/venues at $60/mo
- [ ] Soft-launch in spot.uz + Tashkent expat WhatsApp


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 1,000 weekly users + 1 sponsor in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz lifestyle, Tashkent expat WhatsApp, Anorbank lifestyle Telegram


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion CMS, Plausible

**Capability required**: Russian + English, Tashkent local knowledge


## Moat potential

Local content + sponsor relationships compound.

## Risks

- **Content stale** — _Mitigation_: Crowdsourced updates
- **Low ad demand** — _Mitigation_: Charge venues for featured listings
- **Competition from spot.uz** — _Mitigation_: Lean on personalized + Telegram-native
