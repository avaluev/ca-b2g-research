# FertilityTrackerUZ

_Privacy-first Telegram cycle tracker with Uzbek/Russian content for women's health in conservative regions_

## Pain point

Women's health apps (Flo, Clue) lack Uzbek and respect for Uzbek conservative norms. Women want private tracking, especially in religious households where US-led apps feel inappropriate.

**Evidence**: https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/list/health/


**Target customer**: Women 18-45 in Tashkent, Andijan, Bukhara wanting Uzbek-language private cycle and pregnancy tracking


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $3.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $70,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Build private Telegram bot with E2E option (libsodium)
- [ ] Add Russian + Uzbek-Latin cycle log + 7-day prediction
- [ ] $3/mo for fertility coaching layer
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch via Tashkent women Telegram and 'Mamamylar' parenting group


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 users + 30 paid in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent women Telegram, Mamamylar Uzbek parenting, TBC family


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Supabase E2E, OpenAI for content

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, respectful health content, privacy-first dev


## Moat potential
