# MountainTelemed

_Wizard-of-Oz: connects Naryn/Issyk-Kul villagers with Bishkek doctors via Telegram for $5 consults_

## Pain point

Naryn, Issyk-Kul villages have 1 doctor per 5K people. Patients travel 4-8h to Bishkek. AKDN supports rural connectivity.

**Evidence**: https://www.akdn.org/


**Target customer**: Naryn, Issyk-Kul villagers 25-65 with chronic conditions or non-acute symptoms


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $5.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $150

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 3 Bishkek GPs at $300/mo retainer
- [ ] Telegram bot intake symptoms + photo
- [ ] Doctors respond 24h
- [ ] Mbank 450 KGS per consult
- [ ] Soft-launch via AKDN partner network + Naryn Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 30 paid consults + NPS 8+ in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: AKDN partner network, Naryn community, Issyk-Kul Telegram


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Mbank

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, doctor network, fulfillment ops


## Moat potential

Doctor network + rural patient book; donor-grant.

## Risks

- **Medical liability** — _Mitigation_: Disclaimer + emergency escalation
- **Doctor retention** — _Mitigation_: Performance bonuses
- **Connectivity** — _Mitigation_: SMS fallback
