# AcademicTranslatorUZ

_Free Telegram tool that translates academic Uzbek/Russian texts to research-quality English with citations preserved_

## Pain point

Uzbek graduate students publishing in English struggle with academic translation. DeepL doesn't cover Uzbek-Latin well. Existing translation services charge $0.10/word.

**Evidence**: https://kun.uz/ru/news/2024/12/15/uzbek-academia


**Target customer**: Uzbek graduate students 25-35 at NUUz, INHA, MDIS, Westminster Tashkent applying for international PhD programs or publishing


## Monetization

- **Model**: freemium
- **Price point**: $12.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $80,000


## MVR plan (demo_video)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] GPT-4o + custom Uzbek prompt for academic register
- [ ] Telegram bot + web app
- [ ] Free 1,000 words/mo, $12/mo for unlimited
- [ ] Click subscription
- [ ] Soft-launch in NUUz + INHA student Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 200 users + 25 paying in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: NUUz Telegram, INHA Tashkent student chat, Westminster Tashkent


**Tech stack**: GPT-4o, Telegram Bot, Next.js, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + English + Uzbek-Latin, academic-translation domain


## Moat potential

Academic-corpus library compounds; possible OEM to research universities.

## Risks

- **Free competitors (DeepL, GPT direct)** — _Mitigation_: Lean on Uzbek-Latin quality + citation handling
- **Niche market size** — _Mitigation_: Add CV/SOP writing
- **Quality variance** — _Mitigation_: Human-in-loop review for premium
