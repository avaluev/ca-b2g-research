# CodingClubUZ

_Wizard-of-Oz weekend coding club for Tashkent kids 10-14, manual fulfillment via 1 instructor, Click-paid_

## Pain point

Parents want kids in coding clubs early. State Ministry of School (Maktab.uz) clubs have 1-month waitlists. Private clubs charge $80-200/mo. Aspirational middle class wants affordable + structured.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/09/05/itpark-junior/


**Target customer**: Tashkent middle-class parents 30-45 with kids 10-14 wanting Scratch/Python after school


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $25.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $120,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $200

**Steps:**
- [ ] Recruit 1-2 university CS students at $100/Saturday
- [ ] Rent Tashkent co-working space hourly ($20)
- [ ] $25/Saturday session, group of 8 kids
- [ ] Click for booking
- [ ] Soft-launch in Tashkent parent Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: Cohort of 8 paying weekly for 4 weeks
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: Tashkent parent Telegram, Mamamylar parenting, IT Park UZ


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Click, Notion (curriculum)

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, CS basics, operations


## Moat potential

Community + curriculum compounds; can scale to multiple Tashkent districts.

## Risks

- **Instructor reliability** — _Mitigation_: Roster of 3 + co-teaching
- **Venue cost** — _Mitigation_: Partner with one IT Park hub
- **Operational burden** — _Mitigation_: Limit to 2 cohorts, prove model
