# PastureBookerKG

_Wizard-of-Oz: helps Kyrgyz herders book seasonal pasture (jailoo) slots via local jailoo committees, in Russian/Kyrgyz_

## Pain point

Pasture (jailoo) allocation runs through ayil okmotu committees. Bookings are paper-based, prone to favoritism. Herders lose access. EU-GIZ supports.

**Evidence**: https://kaktus.media/


**Target customer**: Smallholder herders 35-60 in Naryn, Issyk-Kul wanting transparent jailoo bookings


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $8.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $100

**Steps:**
- [ ] Partner with 3 ayil okmotu committees in Naryn
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: jailoo + herd size + dates
- [ ] Founder coordinates booking
- [ ] $8 per booking via Mbank
- [ ] EU-GIZ grant pitch


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 15 paid bookings in 30 days during season
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: Naryn farmer Telegram, EU-GIZ partner network, kaktus.media


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Mbank

**Capability required**: Russian + Kyrgyz, ayil okmotu network, fulfillment ops


## Moat potential

Committee + herder network compounds; donor-grant.

## Risks

- **Committee resistance** — _Mitigation_: Pre-brief; small honorarium
- **Seasonality** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with LivestockTrackKG
- **Politically sensitive** — _Mitigation_: Partner with EU-GIZ for cover
