# BiometricCheckUZ

_Demo video and Wizard-of-Oz service: helps citizens verify their MyID biometric data is correctly registered before they need it_

## Pain point

MyID biometric records have known data-entry errors (mistyped DOBs, swapped photos). Citizens find out at the airport or notary. There is no preventive verification service. Spot.uz reports rising complaints.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/2024/12/05/myid/


**Target customer**: Tashkent professionals planning international travel within 60 days; returning migrants whose records were re-entered from old paper passports


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $6.00
- **Year-1 target**: $10,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 4 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Record demo video showing the verification process
- [ ] Telegram bot intake (photo of MyID screen + passport)
- [ ] Founder reviews and reports errors in 2h
- [ ] Click payment, 75,000 UZS per check
- [ ] Soft-launch in TBC SME and travel agency Telegram


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid checks in 21 days
- **Window**: 30 days
- **Channels**: TBC SME Telegram, Tashkent travel agencies, spot.uz Telegram


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, MyID familiarity


## Moat potential

Productize into a self-serve $4 wizard once volume crosses 200/mo.

## Risks

- **Government tightens MyID API access** — _Mitigation_: Customer-driven model — they show their own screen
- **Low volume outside travel season** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with passport-issuance prep service
- **Refund disputes** — _Mitigation_: Refund if no error found
