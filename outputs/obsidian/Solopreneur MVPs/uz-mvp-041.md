# InsuranceClaimHelper

_Wizard-of-Oz service: photo your medical bills, get a State Medical Insurance Fund reimbursement claim filed within 48h_

## Pain point

State Medical Insurance launching from PP-311. Citizens unfamiliar with claim formats; rejection rates expected high. No private claim-helpers exist yet.

**Evidence**: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7203456


**Target customer**: Tashkent professionals 35-55 with private + state coverage, especially after major treatments (surgery, hospitalization)


## Monetization

- **Model**: services
- **Price point**: $10.00
- **Year-1 target**: $18,000
- **Year-3 target**: $120,000


## MVR plan (wizard_of_oz)

- **Build time**: 7 days
- **Build cost**: $80

**Steps:**
- [ ] Map State Medical Insurance Fund claim process (will publish 2026 details)
- [ ] Telegram bot intake: bills + diagnosis + member ID
- [ ] Founder + 1 paralegal file claim within 48h
- [ ] Click 120,000 UZS per claim
- [ ] Soft-launch with TBC Bank private-insurance customers


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 20 paid claims + 80% acceptance rate in 60 days
- **Window**: 90 days
- **Channels**: TBC Bank private insurance Telegram, Anorbank health Telegram, Akfa Clinic patient WhatsApp


**Tech stack**: Telegram Bot, Notion, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek, insurance claim domain expertise


## Moat potential

Claim-template library + insurance relationships; possible OEM to State Medical Insurance Fund.

## Risks

- **Insurance Fund rules unclear** — _Mitigation_: Wait for PP-311 implementing regs Q3 2026; soft-launch as advisory until then
- **Liability on rejected claims** — _Mitigation_: Refund if rejected for service-side error; T&Cs
- **Volume seasonal** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with InsuranceClaimHelper to family-care advisory
