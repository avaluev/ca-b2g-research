# DairyMarketUZ

_Telegram-based directory of regional dairy buyers and prices for smallholder dairy farmers_

## Pain point

Smallholder dairy farmers (5-30 cows) often sell at below-market prices because they don't know regional buyer prices. Local creameries vary 20-30% in price. No transparent marketplace exists.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/agriculture/


**Target customer**: Dairy farmers 35-55 in Tashkent oblast, Samarkand, Fergana valley with 5-30 cows producing 50-300L/day


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $8.00
- **Year-1 target**: $9,000
- **Year-3 target**: $60,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $30

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 50 dairy buyers with weekly price quotes
- [ ] Telegram channel + Carrd directory page in Uzbek + Russian
- [ ] Free for farmers, $8/week for buyers wanting featured listing
- [ ] Click micro-payment
- [ ] Soft-launch in 5 oblast farmer Telegram groups


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 300 farmer subscribers + 5 paid buyers in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: agro.uz, regional farmer Telegram, Uzdjm cooperative network


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Telegram, Notion CMS

**Capability required**: Russian + Uzbek-Latin, field outreach to creameries


## Moat potential

Trust + buyer list compounds; can extend to other livestock products.

## Risks

- **Buyer pushback on transparency** — _Mitigation_: Offer first month free + data anonymization
- **Low payments collected** — _Mitigation_: Bundle with input-dealer ads
- **Regional fragmentation** — _Mitigation_: Start with one oblast, prove model
