# DocLookupUZ

_Searchable directory of Tashkent specialist doctors with verified credentials, English ratings, and direct WhatsApp_

## Pain point

Tashkent expats and middle-class Uzbeks struggle to find verified specialists. Booking.com-style sites don't exist for healthcare. WhatsApp word-of-mouth dominates. Listing fee model is untapped.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/health/


**Target customer**: Tashkent middle/upper-middle class 30-55 seeking specialists; expats; medical tourists from rural Uzbekistan


## Monetization

- **Model**: listing_fee
- **Price point**: $40.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (directory)

- **Build time**: 8 days
- **Build cost**: $60

**Steps:**
- [ ] Compile 200 specialists from clinic websites + verify credentials with Ministry of Health
- [ ] Carrd directory + sector filter + reviews integration
- [ ] Free for patients, $40/mo for featured listing
- [ ] Click micro-payment for paid listing
- [ ] Soft-launch via Tashkent expat WhatsApp + spot.uz


## Validation

- **Signal target**: 5,000 visits + 5 paid listings in 60 days
- **Window**: 60 days
- **Channels**: spot.uz, Tashkent expat groups, Anorbank health-insurance partner Telegram


**Tech stack**: Carrd, Notion CMS, Plausible, Click

**Capability required**: Russian + English, credential verification process, B2B sales


## Moat potential

Trusted directory + reviews; possible OEM to insurance companies; doctor listings compound.

## Risks

- **Liability for misinformation** — _Mitigation_: Verify credentials; ToU disclaimer
- **Doctors slow to pay** — _Mitigation_: First 30 days free + commission on first patient
- **Government compete with official directory** — _Mitigation_: Lean on UX and English-language coverage
