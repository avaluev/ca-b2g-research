# RemittanceCalcUZ

_Free tool: best USD/RUB-to-UZS rate across Money Transfer apps for Uzbek migrant families receiving remittances_

## Pain point

$15B+ annual remittances from Russia/Korea/Turkey. Family back home loses 1-3% to suboptimal exchange rate or fees. No live aggregator covers Uzcard, Humo, ASAKA, Anorbank, Korona Pay, Zolotaya Korona. Spot.uz finance section publishes weekly 'best rate' articles.

**Evidence**: https://www.spot.uz/ru/category/biznes/finance/


**Target customer**: Spouses/parents 30-55 in Andijan, Fergana, Namangan receiving remittances from Russia (60% of remittances)


## Monetization

- **Model**: ad
- **Price point**: $100.00
- **Year-1 target**: $12,000
- **Year-3 target**: $100,000


## MVR plan (free_tool)

- **Build time**: 6 days
- **Build cost**: $50

**Steps:**
- [ ] Aggregate rates from 6 services (Korona, Zolotaya Korona, Profi Credit, Humo Pay, Click, Anorbank)
- [ ] Carrd page + Telegram bot in Russian + Uzbek
- [ ] Sponsored 'best rate' slot for one bank ($100/mo)
- [ ] Soft-launch in Andijan/Fergana migrant family Telegram
- [ ] Affiliate to Anorbank account opening for receiving
