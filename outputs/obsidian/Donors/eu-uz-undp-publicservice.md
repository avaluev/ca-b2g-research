# Further Improvement of Public Service Delivery in Uzbekistan (EU-UNDP)

**Donor**: EU  •  **Country**: UZ  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

Digitize at least 35 public services; upskill 1,000 civil servants including AI/data science training for ICT specialists; establish 34 new public service delivery zones; digital user-centric transformation of back-office functions across 11 institutions. AI and data science training specifically for government ICT staff (5-day seminars, Tashkent region Oct 2025). Implementing: UNDP + Ministry of Justice + Agency for Strategic Reforms + Agency for Development of Public Service + MoDT.
