# Uzbekistan Digital Inclusion Project

**Donor**: WorldBank  •  **Country**: UZ  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

Establishes up to 11 regional IT-enabled services (ITES) centers; trains ~6,200 youths in AI, programming, digital skills; attracts ~240 new ITES companies; funds employment incentives for ~9,000 youth in digital economy. Direct AI/digital workforce pipeline. Supports IT Park under Ministry of Digital Technologies.
