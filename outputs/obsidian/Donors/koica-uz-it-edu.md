# Enhancing Youth Employment and Entrepreneurship through Improving IT Education Ecosystem in Uzbekistan

**Donor**: KOICA  •  **Country**: UZ  •  **Status**: pipeline

## AI / digital relevance

Grant funding to develop UZ IT education ecosystem: 2 new IT hubs (Nukus and Karshi), 14 IT centers across 7 regions, training in AI, prompt engineering, programming, IoT, online marketing for 500+ specialists and educators. Startup incubation with Korean experts. Directly aligns with УП-189 AI decree targets and WB P179108 digital inclusion pipeline.
