# Connectivity for Central Asia (C4CA)

**Donor**: EU  •  **Country**: BOTH  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

Regional digital connectivity initiative covering KZ, KG, TJ, TM, UZ. Four components: (1) high-capacity internet network development, (2) satellite constellation enabling environment, (3) ground infrastructure deployment, (4) capacity building for cybersecurity. Implementing partners: Expertise France, e-Governance Academy (Estonia), Finnish Institute of Public Management (HAUS), Lithuanian CPMA, Latvian CFCA. EGA involvement means X-Road/GovStack expertise available to KG Tunduk modernization.
