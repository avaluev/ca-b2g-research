# Huawei — Kyrgyzstan Unified Government Database and Data Center Initiative

**Donor**: China_DSR  •  **Country**: KG  •  **Status**: pipeline

## AI / digital relevance

Huawei proposal: unified database of state agencies and local governments in KG (currently fragmented separate ISs). MoDD expressed interest in cooperation with Huawei Technologies Bishkek Co., LTD. Pre-MoU stage as of 2024. Potentially competing with Tunduk/UDP digital modernization funded by WB and EU. Context: Digital Silk Road expansion.
