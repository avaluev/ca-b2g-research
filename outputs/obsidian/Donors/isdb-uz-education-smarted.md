# IsDB-GPE SmartEd Education Transformation Project (Uzbekistan)

**Donor**: IsDB  •  **Country**: UZ  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

Part of IsDB SmartEd $350M regional program (UZ, KG, Cameroon). In UZ: constructs 80+ schools with digital infrastructure; improves EMIS (Education Management Information System) for data-driven decision-making; implements competency-based curriculum with digital learning tools; teacher professional development digital platforms. Total: IsDB $160.25M + GPE grant $40M + Government $20M. UNICEF tripartite partner. EMIS upgrade directly relevant for education AI analytics.
