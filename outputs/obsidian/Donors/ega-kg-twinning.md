# e-Governance Academy (EGA) Twinning — Support to Digitalisation Agenda in Kyrgyzstan

**Donor**: EU  •  **Country**: KG  •  **Status**: closed

## AI / digital relevance

EGA (Estonia) + HAUS (Finland) + CSI-Piemonte (Italy) twinning for KG digital agenda: e-governance systems, e-learning, data protection (GDPR-modeled), cybersecurity strategy, Innovation Centre of Digital Competencies. Project Manager: Tõnis Mäe (EGA). Completed Oct 2023 but EGA now C4CA implementing partner — ongoing relationship. KG-Estonia digital bridge is active and strategic.
