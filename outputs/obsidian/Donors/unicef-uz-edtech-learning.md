# Uzbekistan Learning Innovation Hub — AI-Driven EdTech (Eduten / Learning Pioneers Programme)

**Donor**: UNICEF  •  **Country**: UZ  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

Uzbekistan selected as one of 6 global 'Learning Pioneers' (2024). Eduten platform — AI-driven math education tool with 200,000+ tasks, learning analytics, automated assessment. Finnish-government-funded. Pilot in UZ schools with UNICEF Learning Innovation Hub. Direct AI education application. MoSHE (Ministry of School and Higher Education) counterpart.
