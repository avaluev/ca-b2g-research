# EDB Fund for Digital Initiatives — Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan Digital Integration

**Donor**: EDB  •  **Country**: BOTH  •  **Status**: active

## AI / digital relevance

EDB Fund for Digital Initiatives (FDI) presents projects at Digital Almaty 2025: (1) National Digital Investment Platform (800 investment projects, 150,000 jobs potential); (2) BEE-ONLINE light industry ecosystem connecting UZ, KG, Russia, Armenia, Belarus, KZ. Uzbekistan joined EDB 2025 (10% stake, 3rd largest shareholder). EDB FDI specifically focuses on AI and digital transformation across Eurasian Economic Union and associated states. Regional digital integration play.
