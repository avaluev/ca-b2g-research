# On Approval of Regulations on the Procedure for Assessing Cybersecurity Levels of Critical Information Infrastructure Objects

_Об утверждении Положения о порядке оценки уровня обеспечения кибербезопасности объектов важной информационной инфраструктуры_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Cybersecurity assessment methodology for critical infrastructure. Banks and financial institutions must undergo periodic cybersecurity audits. Creates recurring procurement for cybersecurity audit and SOC services.
