# On Measures to Further Accelerate the Implementation of Digital Technologies in the Regions, Sectors and Industries of the Republic

_О мерах по дальнейшему ускорению внедрения цифровых технологий в регионах, сферах и отраслях республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

41 priority digital projects for 2025-2026. Target: electronic government services to 70%. 30+ types of government services in mahallas within 'Digital Government' program. Extends ПП-162 ambitions across all regions and sectors. Sets baseline for AI integration in regional digital government.
