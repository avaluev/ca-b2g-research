# On Measures to Radically Improve the Personnel Training System for Economic Sectors

_О мерах по коренному совершенствованию системы подготовки кадров для сфер экономики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Comprehensive workforce training reform. Aligns training programs with digital/AI economy needs. Creates pipeline for AI talent from education to government service. Supports 15 AI labs mandate under УП-189.
