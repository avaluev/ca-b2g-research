# On Additional Measures for Further Development of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

_О дополнительных мерах по дальнейшему развитию технологий искусственного интеллекта_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Four primary goals: (1) minimum 100 AI projects by end-2026; (2) over $1B foreign investment by 2030; (3) AI education in curricula from 2026/2027; (4) 15 AI laboratories in higher institutions by end-2026. Key deadlines: Jan 1, 2026: AI procurement procedures; Mar 1, 2026: Weather forecasting AI + AI Cluster New Uzbekistan; Jun 1, 2026: Public procurement AI systems; End-2026: Language corpus + all 15 AI labs. From $100M FRR: $90M interest-free credit, $10M grants. Establishes Alliance for AI Development.
