# On Additional Measures to Accelerate Digitization of the Healthcare System and Introduction of Advanced Digital Technologies

_О дополнительных мерах по ускорению цифровизации системы здравоохранения и внедрению передовых цифровых технологий_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Liquidates IT-Med institution; transfers functions to UZINFOCOM as single healthcare IT integrator. Approves Program for Effective Organization of National Digital Healthcare System. Sets up 'Electronic Healthcare' platform infrastructure. Lays ground for AI-assisted clinical decision support and e-prescription.
