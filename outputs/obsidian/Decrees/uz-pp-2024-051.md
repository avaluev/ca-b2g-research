# On Additional Support Measures for Training Qualified Specialists in Digitalization

_О дополнительных мерах поддержки деятельности по подготовке квалифицированных специалистов в сфере цифровизации_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Supports training of IT/digital workforce. Includes access to international online courses (IT export direction), supports non-state digital education organizations. Links to 'One Million Programmers' project. Subsidies for IT Park resident organizations.
