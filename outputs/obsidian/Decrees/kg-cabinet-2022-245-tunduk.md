# On Creating State Institution 'Tunduk' under the Ministry of Digital Development of the Kyrgyz Republic

_О создании Государственного учреждения «Тундук» при Министерстве цифрового развития Кыргызской Республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Restructures Tunduk from GP (state enterprise) to GU (state institution) under Ministry of Digital Development. Establishes governance, mandate, funding. Now superseded by April 2026 reorganization — Tunduk transferred to GP Infocom under UDP. Key reference for understanding institutional migration.
