# On Amendments to Certain Legislative Acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan in Connection with Regulating Relations Arising from the Application of Artificial Intelligence

_О внесении дополнений и изменений в некоторые законодательные акты Республики Узбекистан в связи с регулированием отношений, возникающих при применении искусственного интеллекта_

## Provisions on AI / digital

FIRST AI-SPECIFIC LAW IN UZBEKISTAN. Defines AI as 'complex technological solutions allowing imitation of human cognitive functions' including self-learning. Core principle: AI systems must not harm human life, health, freedom, honor, dignity or other inalienable rights. Legally significant decisions affecting individual rights cannot rely exclusively on AI conclusions. Establishes administrative liability for illegal data processing using AI. Directly implements principles from ПП-358 and УП-189.
