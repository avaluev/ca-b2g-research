# On Priority Measures for Creating the International Center for Digital Technologies

_О первоочередных мерах по созданию Международного центра цифровых технологий_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Creates International Digital Technologies Center ('Enterprise Uzbekistan') with special legal regime for foreign IT companies. Establishes Coordination Council for center activities. Aims to attract foreign IT companies and develop software export. Expanded by УП-233 of 26.11.2025.
