# On Amendments to the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan 'On State Procurement'

_О внесении дополнений и изменений в Закон Республики Узбекистан «О государственных закупках»_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Further digitalization of state procurement system. Amendments aimed at streamlining electronic procurement, reducing corruption opportunities, increasing transparency. Aligns with ПП-320 provisions for AI project procurement by government bodies.
