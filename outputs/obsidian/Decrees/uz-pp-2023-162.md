# On Measures to Expand Coverage and Improve Quality of Digital Services, and Digital Transformation of Sectors, Industries and Regions

_О мерах по расширению охвата и повышению качества цифровых услуг, а также цифровой трансформации сфер, отраслей и регионов_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Comprehensive sectoral digitalization push: digital government in all regions, expanded electronic services. Target: 70% of government services in electronic form. Established framework for 'Digital Government' program delivering 30+ services at mahalla level.
