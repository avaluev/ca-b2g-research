# On Approval of the 'Digital Uzbekistan-2030' Strategy and Measures for its Effective Implementation

_Об утверждении Стратегии «Цифровой Узбекистан-2030» и мерах по ее эффективной реализации_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Foundational digital strategy: 400+ information systems and e-services, internet coverage from 78% to 95%, training 587,000 citizens in programming, Coordination Commission established, regional digital transformation programs for 13 pilot districts. Parent decree for all subsequent digital initiatives.
