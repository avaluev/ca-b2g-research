# On State Procurement

_О государственных закупках_

## Provisions on AI / digital

State procurement law requiring electronic procurement. Sets framework for zakupki.gov.kg portal. Being upgraded via ADB project 55109-001 (e-Procurement digitalization). Digital procurement is core for AI/tech vendor access to government contracts.
