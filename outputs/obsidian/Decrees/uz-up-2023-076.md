# On Measures for Effective Organization of State Administration in Digital Technologies within Administrative Reforms

_О мерах по эффективной организации государственного управления в сфере цифровых технологий в рамках административных реформ_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Reorganizes Ministry of Digital Technologies structure. Creates clearer mandate separation between MoDT, UZINFOCOM, and IT Park. Establishes administrative framework for implementing УП-6079 and subsequent AI decrees.
