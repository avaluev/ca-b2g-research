# On Additional Measures to Implement Healthcare Reforms

_О дополнительных мерах по реализации реформ в системе здравоохранения_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Approves ICT equipment list for medical facilities under pilot project. Mandates population registration in 'Electronic Healthcare' system by June 15, 2025 (pilot areas) and end-2025 (all regions). Implements '90-day changes in medicine' digital program. Prepares for nationwide digital health coverage.
