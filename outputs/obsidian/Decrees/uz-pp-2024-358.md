# On Approval of the AI Technology Development Strategy until 2030

_Об утверждении Стратегии развития технологий искусственного интеллекта до 2030 года_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Approves comprehensive AI Strategy 2030. Target metrics: AI software/services revenue $1.5B by 2030; 10% of e-government services via AI; 10 scientific AI labs; Government AI Readiness Index Top 50. Sectors: banking/finance fraud prevention, tax/customs shadow economy reduction, healthcare diagnostics, agriculture yield forecasting, energy optimization. Budget: $50M interest-free credit (5-year, 2-year grace) from Reconstruction Fund to MoDT. Creates Center for AI and Digital Economy Development. Implemented by УП-189 and ПП-320 of Oct 2025.
