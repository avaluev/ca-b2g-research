# On Electronic Governance

_Об электронном управлении_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Defines electronic governance as activities of state bodies, local government, and citizens in making legally significant decisions using electronic documents. Establishes legal framework for digital government services. Basis for Tunduk operations and digital services delivery.
