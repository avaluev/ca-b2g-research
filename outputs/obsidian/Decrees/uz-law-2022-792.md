# On Electronic Commerce

_Об электронной коммерции_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Comprehensive electronic commerce law replacing 2004 original. Defines electronic commerce as entrepreneurial activity using information systems. Ministry of Digital Technologies designated as authorized body. Covers digital contracts, electronic offers, digital marketplace operators.
