# On Personal Information

_Об информации персонального характера_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Foundational personal data protection law. Defines personal information, establishes rights of data subjects, regulates processing by government and private entities. Still in force — Цифровой кодекс (2025) expands rather than replaces it. Relevant for AI data processing compliance.
