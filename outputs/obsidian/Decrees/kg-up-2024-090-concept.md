# On Approval of the Digital Transformation Concept of the Kyrgyz Republic for 2024-2028

_Об утверждении Концепции Цифровой трансформации Кыргызской Республики на 2024-2028 годы_

## Provisions on AI / digital

200+ implementation measures, 89 in 2024. Covers digital government in all regions, digital healthcare, digital education, national digital infrastructure, digital content. Budget: 600M KGS (~$7.1M) from state budget for FY2024 (up 100M from prior years). Framework for: e-services expansion, Tunduk modernization, AI integration pilot projects, digital identity strengthening. Parent document for all subsequent KG digital decrees.
