# On State Procurement (New Law)

_О государственных закупках_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Replaces 2015 procurement law. Requires electronic procurement format as standard. Establishes authorized state body functions for electronic procurement development and administration. ADB project 55109-001 aligns with this law's implementation.
