# On Virtual Assets

_О виртуальных активах_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Comprehensive virtual assets law. Establishes licensing framework for virtual asset exchange operators. Basis for KGST stablecoin and broader crypto ecosystem. Amended by Law №81 of 05.08.2022 and Law №3 of 19.01.2026. Foundational for USDKG and digital som infrastructure.
