# On Measures to Implement Advanced Digital Technologies in Agriculture

_О мерах по внедрению передовых цифровых технологий в сферу сельского хозяйства_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Establishes 'Agroplatforma' integration with 'Digital Agriculture' unified platform from September 1, 2023. Covers inter-agency information systems for crop monitoring, digital allocation of preferential loans for cotton/grain/vegetables. Remote sensing and satellite imagery integration for land monitoring.
