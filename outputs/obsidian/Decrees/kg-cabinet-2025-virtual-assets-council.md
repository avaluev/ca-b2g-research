# On Creation of the National Council on Virtual Assets and Blockchain Technologies under the President of the Kyrgyz Republic

_О создании Национального совета по виртуальным активам и блокчейн-технологиям при Президенте Кыргызской Республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Creates National Council on Virtual Assets and Blockchain Technologies under the President. Positions KG as crypto-friendly jurisdiction. Links to USDKG stablecoin (launched Oct 2025) and digital som infrastructure. Oversight body for virtual asset ecosystem including AI-driven compliance tools.
