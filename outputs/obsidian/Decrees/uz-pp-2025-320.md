# On Additional Measures to Support Projects Based on Artificial Intelligence Technologies

_О дополнительных мерах по поддержке проектов, основанных на технологиях искусственного интеллекта_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Distributes $100M from Reconstruction & Development Fund: $50M grants for AI implementation in state bodies (2026-2027), $50M equity split equally between 'Fund of Funds' and 'IT Park Ventures'. Coordination Commission distributes grants and determines criteria. Key dates: Dec 1, 2025: agencies submit AI proposals; Jan 1, 2026: working body presents selections to Coordination Commission; 2026-2027: fund distribution. This decree creates the single highest-leverage policy advocacy window — distribution criteria not yet fully published as of May 2026.
