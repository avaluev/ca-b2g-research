# On Measures to Implement Tasks Defined in the Fourth Open Dialogue of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan with Entrepreneurs

_О мерах по реализации задач, определенных в четвертом открытом диалоге Президента Республики Узбекистан с предпринимателями_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Parent decree for AI strategy development. Approves Program with 5 directions including AI-related reforms. Contains digital economy simplification measures: reduces mandatory requirements, simplifies licensing, accelerates digital service delivery. Sets stage for ПП-358 AI Strategy.
