# On Additional Measures to Improve the Cybersecurity System for Critical Information Infrastructure Facilities of the Republic of Uzbekistan

_О дополнительных мерах по совершенствованию системы обеспечения кибербезопасности объектов критической информационной инфраструктуры Республики Узбекистан_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Mandates cybersecurity assessments for critical information infrastructure. Establishes vulnerability management requirements. Assigns UNICON as state cybersecurity center. Requires periodic penetration testing of government systems.
