# On Personal Data

_О персональных данных_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Foundational personal data law. Defines personal data as information on electronic/paper/material media relating to or allowing identification of an individual. Covers collection, systematization, storage, modification. Cross-border transfer may be restricted to protect constitutional order, morality, health, rights of citizens, defense. Amended by ЗРУ-711 (2021), ЗРУ-964 (2024), ЗРУ-1125 (2026). Critical for AI-in-government compliance (ЗРУ-1115).
