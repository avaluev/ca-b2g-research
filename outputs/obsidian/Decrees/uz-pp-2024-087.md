# On Additional Measures to Develop Digital Services Export in the Country

_О дополнительных мерах по развитию в стране экспорта услуг в сфере цифровизации_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Expands IT export incentives. Includes target indicators for digital services export by region. Subsidy coverage for IT Park residents. Platform for training in export-direction IT professions. Links to 'One Million Programmers'. Followed by УП-157 of 14.10.2024 for enterprises.
