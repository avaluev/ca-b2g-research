# On Measures to Introduce Aerospace Technologies in Economic Sectors in 2024-2026

_О мерах по внедрению аэрокосмических технологий в отраслях экономики в 2024-2026 годах_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Roadmap for space industry development 2024-2026. Creates Agency for Space Research and Technologies under Ministry of Digital Technologies. Satellite remote sensing for agriculture, urban planning, disaster monitoring. AI-assisted satellite imagery analysis component. Direct link to geospatial NSDI procurement window (CW-009).
