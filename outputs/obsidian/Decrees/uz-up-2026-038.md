# On Determining the Cybersecurity Strategy of the Republic of Uzbekistan and Improving the System for Preventing Cybercrime

_Об определении Стратегии кибербезопасности Республики Узбекистан и совершенствовании системы предупреждения киберпреступности_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Cybersecurity Strategy 2026-2030. Five objectives: (1) Strengthen national cyber resilience and protect critical infrastructure; (2) Systematically reduce cyber risks; (3) Counter cybercrime; (4) Develop digital innovations and technological independence; (5) International cooperation. Establishes National Coordination Council on Cybersecurity chaired by Security Council Secretary. Major milestones: Apr 1, 2026: Cybersecurity departments at agencies; Jul 1, 2026: Assessment reports; Aug 1, 2026: Outsourcing regulations; Oct 1, 2026: Digital vulnerability testing platform. Funding from April 1, 2026.
