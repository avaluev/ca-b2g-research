# On Additional Measures to Accelerate Reforms in Agricultural and Food Sectors

_О дополнительных мерах по ускорению реформ в сельскохозяйственной и продовольственной сфере_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Agricultural reform acceleration with digital components. Extends digital monitoring of crop production, land use, and food supply chains. Links to Agroplatforma system and precision agriculture tools.
