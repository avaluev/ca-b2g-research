# On Approval of the Automated Information System 'State Electronic Document Management System'

_Об утверждении Положения об автоматизированной информационной системе «Государственная система электронного документооборота»_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Establishes state electronic document management system (EDMS). Automates internal government document circulation. Prerequisite for AI-assisted document processing, routing, and classification. Links to Digital Code chapter on trusted services.
