# On Protection from False Information

_О защите от недостоверной информации_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Law targeting false information in digital media. Requires platforms to remove false information within specified timeframes. Creates regulatory context where AI content moderation tools are necessary for compliance. Intersection with AI systems regulation in Digital Code.
