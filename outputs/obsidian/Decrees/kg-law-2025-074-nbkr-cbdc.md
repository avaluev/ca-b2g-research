# On Amendments to the Constitutional Law of the Kyrgyz Republic 'On the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic' (Introduction of Digital Som)

_О внесении изменений в Конституционный закон Кыргызской Республики «О Национальном банке Кыргызской Республики» (введение цифрового сома)_

## Provisions on AI / digital

CBDC FRAMEWORK LAW. Establishes digital som (CBDC) as legal tender. National Bank (NBKR) has exclusive emission rights and operates the digital som platform. Defines cryptographic protection principles. Key provisions entered into force January 1, 2027. Prior to that date: pilot project of national digital currency prototype. Creates procurement need for: wallet infrastructure, AML/KYC AI, fraud detection, transaction monitoring (CW-005 highest priority).
