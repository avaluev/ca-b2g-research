# On Approval of Requirements for Creating Cybersecurity Systems for Critical Information Infrastructure Objects

_Об утверждении Требований к созданию систем кибербезопасности объектов критической информационной инфраструктуры_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Mandatory cybersecurity requirements for critical information infrastructure operators. Defines technical standards, incident response requirements, personnel qualifications. Creates procurement demand for cybersecurity systems, SOC services, and security monitoring tools.
