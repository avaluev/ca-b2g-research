# On Enacting the Digital Code of the Kyrgyz Republic

_О введении в действие Цифрового Кодекса Кыргызской Республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Enactment law for the Digital Code. Sets February 6, 2026 as date when Digital Code enters into force. Establishes transition provisions. Cabinet required to bring all existing legislation into conformity with Digital Code within specified periods.
