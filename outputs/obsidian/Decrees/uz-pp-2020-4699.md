# On Measures for the Widespread Introduction of Digital Economy and Electronic Government

_О мерах по широкому внедрению цифровой экономики и электронного правительства_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Early comprehensive e-government acceleration. Expands EPIGU services, automates government processes. Lays infrastructure for subsequent AI integration. Sets digital signatures as mandatory for government transactions.
