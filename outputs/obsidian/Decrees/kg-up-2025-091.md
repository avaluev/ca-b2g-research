# On Measures to Create the National Council on Artificial Intelligence (probable subject)

_О мерах по созданию Национального совета по искусственному интеллекту (предположительно)_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Decree УП-91 of March 17, 2025. Likely creates or restructures the National Council on Artificial Intelligence — consistent with January 2025 announcement that KG will establish an AI council (EADaily source). Kabmin Chairman Kasymaliiev signed companion resolution approving Council composition and charter. Council tasks: develop AI projects, increase scientific/educational potential, strengthen KG's international AI positioning. Proposed Regional AI Hub within EAEU.
