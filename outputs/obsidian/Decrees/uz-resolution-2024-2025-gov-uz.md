# On Migration of State Agency Websites to the Unified gov.uz Platform from March 1, 2025

_О переводе сайтов государственных органов на единую платформу gov.uz с 1 марта 2025 года_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Mandates all government agency websites transition to unified gov.uz platform from March 1, 2025. Centralizes government digital presence. Key enabler for future AI chatbot deployment across all government services (CW-007). Decree number to be confirmed on lex.uz.
