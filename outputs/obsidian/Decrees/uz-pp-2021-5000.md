# On Measures for the Effective Organization of Digitization in the Healthcare Sector

_О мерах по эффективной организации цифровизации в сфере здравоохранения_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Established foundational digital health architecture. Assigned UZINFOCOM as integrator. Created 'Electronic Healthcare' information system framework. Superseded by ПП-415 of 28.12.2023 but foundational infrastructure provisions remain in force.
