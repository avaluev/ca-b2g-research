# On Questions of Organization and Management of State Enterprises of the Kyrgyz Republic in the Field of Digitalization

_О вопросах организации и управления государственными предприятиями Кыргызской Республики в сфере цифровизации_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Establishes governance framework for state enterprises in digitalization sector (GP Infocom, GP Tunduk predecessors). Sets accountability, reporting, and performance standards for digital SOEs.
