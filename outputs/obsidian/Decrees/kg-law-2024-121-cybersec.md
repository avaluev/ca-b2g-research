# On Cybersecurity of the Kyrgyz Republic

_О кибербезопасности Кыргызской Республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

First standalone cybersecurity law of Kyrgyzstan (preceding the Digital Code which subsumed many provisions). Sets legal framework for cybersecurity governance, critical infrastructure protection, incident response, CERT operations. Creates mandatory compliance for state information systems. Directly creates procurement demand for cybersecurity solutions.
