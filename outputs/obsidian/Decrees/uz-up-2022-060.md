# On the Development Strategy of New Uzbekistan for 2022-2026

_О Стратегии развития Нового Узбекистана на 2022-2026 годы_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Overarching development strategy including seven priority directions. Digital transformation is Priority Direction 5. Sets baseline for all sectoral digitalization including AI implementation. Parent strategic document for ПП-358, УП-189, and УП-140.
