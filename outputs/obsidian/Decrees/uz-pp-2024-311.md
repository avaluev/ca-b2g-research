# On Measures to Implement State Medical Insurance Mechanisms

_О мерах по внедрению механизмов государственного медицинского страхования_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Implements state medical insurance with digital components. Electronic queue and referral systems from Oct 2022 (transitioning Jan 2025). Integrates with Electronic Healthcare platform. Creates digital claims processing. Insurance digitalization requires AI-fraud detection integration (CW-006 procurement window).
