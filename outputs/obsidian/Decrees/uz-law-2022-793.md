# On Electronic Digital Signature

_Об электронной цифровой подписи_

## Provisions on AI / digital

New comprehensive law replacing 2003 original (in force from Jan 14, 2023). Regulates electronic digital signature use across government and private sectors. Foundational for MyID, EPIGU, and legal validity of digital transactions.
