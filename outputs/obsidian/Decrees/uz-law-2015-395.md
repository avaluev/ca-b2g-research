# On Electronic Government

_Об электронном правительстве_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Foundational e-government law. Defines electronic government as system of organizational-legal measures and technical means for state bodies to provide services using IT. MoDT designated as authorized e-government body. Enables legal basis for EPIGU and all subsequent digital service delivery. Amended multiple times through 2021.
