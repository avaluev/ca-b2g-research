# Digital Code of the Kyrgyz Republic

_Цифровой Кодекс Кыргызской Республики_

## Provisions on AI / digital

COMPREHENSIVE DIGITAL CODE — MOST SIGNIFICANT LEGISLATION IN KG DIGITAL HISTORY. Entry into force: February 6, 2026 (6 months after publication August 8, 2025). Structure: Part I General Provisions (Chapters 1-9): digital environment foundations, state regulation, digital identification, rights protection. Part II Special Provisions — Section III Data Processing: personal data (Chapter 11, 100+ definitions), spatial data, digital registries. Section IV Digital Services: wellness, telecom, trusted services, government services, national ecosystem. Section V Digital Technology Systems: AI systems regulation (Chapter 23, high-risk AI requires danger assessment + compliance confirmation), telecommunications networks. KEY AI PROVISION: High-risk AI systems require danger assessment and compliance confirmation. Establishes sectoral regulators for personal data and telecom. Secondary legislation (bylaws implementing the code) being drafted throughout 2026 — LEVERAGE WINDOW for AI auditability standards, model registries, testing requirements (CW-010).
