# On Organizing the Activities of the International Center for Digital Technologies

_Об организации деятельности Международного центра цифровых технологий_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Expands and formalizes 'Enterprise Uzbekistan' Digital Technology Center. Establishes Management Board composition and territorial boundaries. Special legal regime for foreign IT companies. Aims to develop software export and attract foreign IT investment. Supersedes parts of УП-25 of 01.02.2024.
