# On Measures to Implement Identification ID-Cards in the Republic of Uzbekistan

_О мерах по внедрению идентификационных ID-карт в Республике Узбекистан_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Establishes biometric ID card rollout (fingerprints + digital photo on microchip). Voluntary exchange Jan 2021–Dec 2022; mandatory from Jan 2023–Dec 2030. Old documents invalid from Jan 2031. Integrates with e-government portal for online services. Direct precursor to MyID digital identity layer.
