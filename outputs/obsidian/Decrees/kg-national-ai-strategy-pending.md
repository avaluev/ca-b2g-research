# National Strategy for AI Development of the Kyrgyz Republic (Not yet signed as of May 2026)

_Национальная стратегия развития искусственного интеллекта Кыргызской Республики (не подписана на май 2026 года)_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Announced but not yet signed as of May 2026. National AI Strategy referenced by Cabinet officials and Kyrgyzstan's proposal for EAEU Regional AI Hub. The Национальный совет по ИИ (created March 2025) is drafting this. Expected to be signed in H2 2026. Key strategic questions: (1) Will it mandate National AI Center (24.kg reported plans); (2) Will it establish AI procurement requirements for state bodies; (3) Will it fund AI education infrastructure.
