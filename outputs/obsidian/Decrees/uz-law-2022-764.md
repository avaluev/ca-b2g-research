# On Cybersecurity

_О кибербезопасности_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Foundational cybersecurity law. Establishes principles: legality, protection of personal/societal/state interests in cyberspace, unified regulation approach, priority of domestic producers in cybersecurity systems, international cooperation. Regulates cybersecurity for critical information infrastructure. Amended by ЗРУ-964 of 20.09.2024.
