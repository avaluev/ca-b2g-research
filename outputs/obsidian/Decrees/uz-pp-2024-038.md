# On Additional Measures to Deepen Healthcare Reforms

_О дополнительных мерах по углублению реформ в сфере здравоохранения_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Deepens healthcare reforms with digital health components. Establishes electronic referral systems. Integrates population registration in 'Electronic Healthcare' information system. Prepares infrastructure for telemedicine and remote care services.
