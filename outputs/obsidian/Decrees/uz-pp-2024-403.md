# On Additional Measures to Increase Private Sector Participation in Education

_О дополнительных мерах по повышению участия частного сектора в сфере образования_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Opens education to private sector including EdTech companies. Creates market for AI-powered tutoring and learning management platforms. Enables international EdTech market entry for AI tutoring products (trend intersection with AI Cluster education mandate).
