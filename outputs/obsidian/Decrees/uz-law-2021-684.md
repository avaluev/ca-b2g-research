# On State Procurement

_О государственных закупках_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Main public procurement law replacing ЗРУ-472 (2018). Establishes electronic procurement as standard form using ICT. Authorized body develops regulations for electronic procurement procedures. Amended by ЗРУ-1005 of 27.11.2024 for further digitalization. Critical for AI/digital vendor contracting with government.
