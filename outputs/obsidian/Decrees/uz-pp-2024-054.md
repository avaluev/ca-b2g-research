# On Additional Measures to Accelerate Education Reforms

_О дополнительных мерах по ускорению реформ в сфере образования_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Accelerates education reforms with digital components. Integration of digital learning management systems. Framework for AI tutoring deployment in schools (2026/2027 curriculum mandate under УП-189).
