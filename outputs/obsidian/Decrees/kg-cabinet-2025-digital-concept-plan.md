# On the Implementation Plan for the Digital Transformation Concept of the Kyrgyz Republic for 2024-2028

_О плане мероприятий по реализации Концепции Цифровой трансформации Кыргызской Республики на 2024-2028 годы_

## Provisions on AI / digital

Implementation plan for Концепция УП-90. Detailed 200+ measure roadmap with assigned responsible bodies and timelines. Procurement guide for digital projects 2024-2028. Covers e-services, healthcare digitalization, education digitalization, AI pilots, Tunduk expansion.
