# Singapore Project Ubin CBDC / Digital SGD

**Origin**: Singapore  •  **Year**: 2016  •  **Sector**: Finance & Fiscal

## Problem solved

Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) needed to explore distributed ledger technology for interbank settlement and cross-border payments, reducing settlement costs and times from T+2 days to near-real-time while maintaining security and regulatory oversight.

## Architecture

5-phase research programme: Phase 1 (tokenised SGD on Ethereum); Phase 2 (DvP delivery vs payment for securities on Quorum/Hyperledger); Phase 3 (multi-currency payments network); Phase 4 (CBDC prototype with privacy features — unlinkability); Phase 5 (cross-border connectivity with Bank of Canada, Bank of England, Bank of Thailand). Key technical innovations: privacy-preserving CBDC using zero-knowledge proofs; programmable payment conditions; multi-bank DvP settlement. MAS published full technical reports for each phase.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

UZ CBU is not yet planning a CBDC — current focus is on payment system modernisation (UPI-equivalent). Ubin's cross-border CBDC design is the forward reference for when UZ eventually explores CBDC. Score 6: useful architecture reference but not actionable in 2-3 year horizon. More immediately relevant: Ubin's privacy-preserving payment design for UZ's AML compliance obligations.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG digital som (in force January 2027) is an ACTIVE CBDC implementation. Ubin's design work is directly relevant to NBKR's architecture decisions. Specifically: wholesale CBDC for bank settlement, privacy-preserving design, programmable payments for government transfer disbursement. IMF is providing advisory to NBKR on digital som design — Ubin is likely part of the reference stack.
