# Saudi Arabia SDAIA National AI Capability Building Programme

**Origin**: Saudi Arabia  •  **Year**: 2020  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 required 20,000+ AI professionals by 2030 to staff both public sector AI programmes and the emerging AI industry. The country lacked university AI programmes with sufficient scale, and government agencies had no internal AI capabilities to specify or evaluate AI procurements. SDAIA (Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority) created a national AI talent development programme across schools, universities, and government agencies.

## Architecture

Layered talent programme: (1) Schools: AI basics embedded in K-12 curriculum via Mawhiba gifted programme; (2) Universities: AI concentration tracks at 15 universities with SDAIA accreditation framework, Arabic-language AI curriculum developed with KAUST and Tuwaiq Academy; (3) Government: SDAIA AI Readiness Assessment tool for all ministries + mandatory AI training for data officers; (4) Bootcamps: Tuwaiq Academy (SDAIA-operated) running 6-week AI bootcamps for government staff, free for Saudi nationals. Arabic-language curriculum: 80% of content in Arabic avoiding Western AI curriculum gap. National AI strategy: published as public document with annual tracking report.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

УП-189 (2025) mandates AI education across schools and universities in UZ — SDAIA is the direct reference for programme design. Key elements to replicate: (1) government AI readiness assessment tool for UZ agencies; (2) mandatory AI training for civil servants; (3) university AI curriculum accreditation framework. Uzbek-language AI curriculum development (cf. Saudi Arabic-language investment) is the bottleneck. Budget for UZ AI capability programme: $20-40M over 5 years. Donor pathway: UNDP, World Bank, IsDB.

## KG transferability (6/10)

SDAIA model is aspirational for KG — budget ($180M) is 10x KG annual education budget. But the STRUCTURE is replicable at 1/20 the cost: (1) government AI readiness self-assessment; (2) 3-6 month bootcamp for 500 government staff; (3) 2 university AI concentrations. Total: $3-5M as a donor-funded programme. IsDB and UNDP are natural funders for KG.
