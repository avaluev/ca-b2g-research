# Bilim Land EdTech Platform (Kazakhstan)

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2012  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan needed to supplement school education with digital content aligned to the national curriculum for 2M+ school students. Rural schools lacked qualified teachers in STEM subjects. National curriculum reform (shift from Soviet-era to competency-based) required new content that the state education system couldn't produce fast enough. Bilim Land was built as a public-private partnership to create Kazakhstan-specific digital curriculum content.

## Architecture

Web and mobile LMS built by Kazakhstani company: gamified learning platform with 100,000+ exercises, video lessons aligned to KZ school curriculum, and teacher monitoring dashboards. AI features: adaptive learning paths based on assessment performance (skill gap identification per student), AI-based recommendations of next exercises. Integration with NIS (Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools) curriculum. School administration panel for teacher assignment tracking. Monetisation: freemium (basic content free, premium subscription $12/year). 2021: Ministry of Education partnership — free access for all Kazakhstani students via bilateral agreement.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

Bilim Land is the single most directly deployable EdTech platform for UZ. Russian-language content exists. UZ has 5M+ school students — Bilim Land partnership (vs building from scratch) makes financial sense at $2-5M state procurement. KOICA is already active in UZ education and explicitly references Bilim Land. Key: negotiate Ministry of Education contract for free access for all UZ students as KZ did in 2021.

## KG transferability (8/10)

Bilim Land Russian/Kazakh content partially usable for Russian-medium schools in KG. Kyrgyz-language adaptation required for Kyrgyz-medium schools (50%+ of KG schools). KOICA KG education project could fund KG localisation. 400,000 KG students: subscription at $5/student/year = $2M/year revenue model or state subsidy. Deployment timeline: 12 months.
