# India ISRO Bhuvan Geoportal & Environment Monitoring

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2009  •  **Sector**: Environment & Climate

## Problem solved

India needed a national geospatial platform for environment monitoring, forest cover change detection, land degradation assessment, and disaster management that used India's sovereign satellite data (Resourcesat, Cartosat) rather than relying on international commercial providers. Bhuvan provided a Google Earth equivalent operated by ISRO with Indian satellite imagery.

## Architecture

National web GIS platform: Bhuvan portal (bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in) with Indian satellite imagery archive (15+ years), vector datasets, and analysis tools. Environment modules: forest cover change detection (bi-annual), land degradation assessment, wetland mapping, coastal change monitoring, Aral Sea equivalent (Chilika Lake seasonal mapping). AI layer: automated change detection between time-series imagery, forest encroachment alert system, crop yield estimation from satellite. API: open access for government agencies, researchers. Disaster Management Support (DMS): real-time flood and cyclone mapping during events.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ World Bank NSDI project ($35M, 2025-2030) is building exactly this — a national spatial data infrastructure. Bhuvan architecture + ESA Copernicus data (free) is the technical foundation. The AI change detection modules for cotton field monitoring, Aral Sea monitoring, and land degradation are directly applicable. Budget is covered by WB project — additional AI analytics layer is $2-3M incremental cost.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG needs environment monitoring for glacier retreat, deforestation in Fergana range, and pasture degradation. Bhuvan architecture using free Copernicus data is feasible for KG at $3-5M. GEF and World Bank environment projects are funding sources. Key application: pasture degradation monitoring for KG's 9M cattle/sheep.
