# Kazakhstan AI in Schools National Programme (NIS)

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan recognized that AI literacy was becoming foundational for economic participation, yet the existing computer science curriculum was based on 1990s programming skills. The National Action Plan for AI Development 2022-2025 included AI curriculum integration across 7,700+ schools. Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) piloted the first AI elective courses before rolling out to mass secondary education.

## Architecture

Two-track approach: (1) NIS Elite Track — full AI/ML course using Python, ML frameworks, national AI competition (BrainCup); (2) Mass Track — AI literacy module embedded in existing informatics curriculum at grades 7-9, covering AI concepts, ethics, basic neural network concepts without coding. Digital delivery: Bilim Land + local LMS platforms. Teacher training: NIS teachers as master trainers in summer intensives. Hardware: GPU lab grants to 500 pilot schools. AI competition: winners attend national Science Olympiad. KOICA advisory support for curriculum design.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

УП-189 explicitly mandates AI in schools by 2026-2027. Kazakhstan NIS model is the 18-month-ahead reference. NIS curriculum materials in Russian are immediately usable in UZ before Uzbek translation. GPU lab grant model: UZ government could target 200 schools with GPU labs in 2026 for $5-8M. Teacher training: NIS master trainers available for UZ secondments. KOICA is already in UZ education and can fund programme continuity.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG school system has 2,100 schools. NIS AI curriculum (Russian-language) is directly usable. GPU labs: 50-100 pilot schools at $15-25k/lab = $1-2.5M. Key challenge: KG physics/math teacher pipeline is weaker than KZ, making AI curriculum more demanding. Target: 10 GPU labs in Bishkek/Osh + distance learning platform for rural schools.
