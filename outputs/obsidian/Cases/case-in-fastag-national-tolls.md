# India FASTag National Electronic Toll Collection

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2017  •  **Sector**: Transport & Urban

## Problem solved

India's 600+ national highway toll booths caused 2-4 hour queues, consumed 8-10 litres of fuel per truck during waiting, and generated $500M+/year in toll leakage (manual collection corruption). FASTag deployed RFID-based electronic toll collection on all national highways, mandating electronic payment and eliminating cash.

## Architecture

RFID sticker (FASTag) affixed to vehicle windscreen, toll plaza RFID readers, national settlement platform (NETC — National Electronic Toll Collection) operated by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). Backend: prepaid wallet or bank account linked to FASTag. Real-time toll deduction without stopping. Interoperability: any bank-issued FASTag works at any NHAI toll plaza. ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) camera layer added 2022 for enforcement of non-FASTag vehicles. Integration with VAHAN (national vehicle registry) for ownership verification.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ is expanding national highway network with ADB/World Bank support. Toll collection infrastructure needs to be built from scratch on new roads — this is the RIGHT TIME to deploy electronic tolling. FASTag architecture is replicable with Russian or Korean vendor RFID systems. UZ Central Bank payment integration via UZCARD/HUMO provides local payment rail. Budget: $15-25M for UZ national electronic toll system. ADB transport project includes tolling component.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG Bishkek-Osh highway (ADB-funded) has planned toll gates. Electronic tolling is more efficient for both operator and user. ADB project includes toll management system component. Budget: $5-10M for KG highway electronic tolling. Key: integration with Elsom payment system and MobilBanking for top-up.
