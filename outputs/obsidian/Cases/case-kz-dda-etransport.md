# Kazakhstan Digital Driver's Licence & e-Inspection

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Transport & Urban

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan required digital format for driver's licenses, vehicle registration, and technical inspection certificates — allowing citizens to show documents via eGov mobile app instead of physical cards. Additionally, the annual vehicle technical inspection (ТО) was plagued by corruption — inspection centres issuing certificates without actual inspection.

## Architecture

eGov mobile app integration: digital driver's licence, vehicle registration, insurance policy stored as QR-verified documents on eGov app. Traffic police can verify via QR scan. Technical inspection reform: certified inspection centres required to upload photo evidence of each inspection point via mobile app with GPS tagging (proving vehicle at inspection centre). AI verification: photo analysis checks that images show a vehicle (not a static background). Violation history linked to digital licence. Cross-border: KZ digital documents accepted in EAEU countries for road travel.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ has explicitly announced a digital driving licence programme and connected vehicle initiative. Kazakhstan's implementation is the direct reference. MyID is the identity anchor for UZ digital documents (as eGov is for KZ). The vehicle inspection anti-corruption architecture (GPS-tagged photos) is high political priority in UZ where vehicle inspection bribery is widely documented. Budget: $5-10M for complete UZ digital transport document system.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG has Tunduk + GRS digital ID as foundation. Digital driving licence via Tunduk service app is technically feasible in 12-18 months for $2-3M. Vehicle inspection anti-corruption module is high priority for KG Interior Ministry. EAEU membership means KZ digital documents already accepted in KG — replicating KZ architecture ensures automatic EAEU compatibility.
