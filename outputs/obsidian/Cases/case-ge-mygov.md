# Georgia Public Service Hall & my.gov.ge

**Origin**: Georgia  •  **Year**: 2011  •  **Sector**: Public Administration & e-Gov

## Problem solved

Post-Soviet Georgia had endemic bureaucratic corruption in government service delivery. Citizens faced Soviet-era queuing, informal payments, and multi-day waits for routine services. The Rose Revolution reforms created political will for radical service delivery overhaul. Public Service Hall ('Public Service Bureau') created a branded, corruption-free multi-service centre network alongside digital channels for the same services.

## Architecture

Two-track approach: physical Public Service Halls (modern, branded, electronic queue management, no-cash rules) plus digital portal my.gov.ge with 300+ services. Backend integration via the Georgian Government Gateway. Digital ID: digital signature certificates issued by LEPL Digital Governance Agency. MoCA (Ministry of Communications and Information Technologies) drives strategy; LEPL Digital Governance Agency (formerly Data Exchange Agency, DEA) operates infrastructure. Key: strong civil society pressure and international donor support (USAID, EU) created accountability mechanisms.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

Georgia's Public Service Hall model is the DIRECT reference for UZ's DXA ЦГУ network. UZ has explicitly benchmarked against Georgia. The 'branded, anti-corruption service centre' concept is politically powerful in UZ context where informal payments at government windows remain a public concern. Digital integration lessons for my.gov.ge mirror UZ EPIGU architecture choices. Donor pathway: World Bank GovTech, EU, UNDP — all active in both Georgia and UZ.

## KG transferability (7/10)

Georgia is a smaller country (3.7M vs KG 6.5M) making the Public Service Hall cost structure comparable. KG has been piloting similar physical service centre models in Bishkek. Donor ecosystem overlap: USAID, EU, World Bank all fund both KG and Georgia. Key adaptation: Kyrgyz language requirement and higher rural dispersion in KG than Georgia. my.gov.ge digital architecture is directly replicable for KG at lower cost.
