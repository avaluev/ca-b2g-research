# Rwanda Irembo Digital Government Platform

**Origin**: Rwanda  •  **Year**: 2014  •  **Sector**: Agriculture & Water

## Problem solved

Rwanda needed to digitise government services rapidly with very limited IT budget. A unique public-private partnership was created: Irembo (private company) built and operates the digital government services portal under a concession agreement, generating revenue from transaction fees. The agriculture module specifically digitised: land registration, crop insurance registration, farmer cooperative registration, and agricultural export permits.

## Architecture

Private-sector-operated government platform: Irembo Ltd holds exclusive concession from GoR for 10 years. Revenue model: 7% transaction fee on payments processed. Platform: 100+ government services, SMS + web + mobile channels, integrated with Rwanda national ID (Nida). Agriculture-specific: smart subsidies module cross-referencing farmer IDs with land registry; coffee and tea export permit automation; agricultural cooperative digital registration; crop insurance claims processing via mobile with satellite damage verification. Backend: AWS, PostgreSQL, React frontend.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

Agriculture digital registry + export permit automation is directly applicable to UZ cotton/wheat export ecosystem. Private concession model: controversial but UZ IT Park ecosystem might produce a local Irembo equivalent. Key lesson: the agriculture module (farmer ID + land + subsidy + export permit in one digital workflow) is more important than the governance model for UZ adaptation. Budget: $5-8M for standalone UZ agricultural services digitisation.

## KG transferability (6/10)

Rwanda (13.5M pop) is closest comparable to KG's service delivery challenge. Irembo private concession model is high-risk for KG's politically sensitive government services. Agriculture module: KG can replicate farmer digital registry + export permit automation for $2-3M via government-built platform (not private concession). Coffee/tea export permits in Rwanda → livestock + potato + fruit export permits in KG.
