# India Bhashini Multilingual AI Platform

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

India's 22 official languages and 780+ dialects created a profound digital divide: most government and educational digital content was in English or Hindi, excluding 600M+ citizens speaking other languages. Bhashini created a national language technology platform providing free AI translation, speech recognition, and text-to-speech APIs for all 22 official languages, enabling developers to build multilingual apps without language expertise.

## Architecture

Open API platform operated by MeitY: REST APIs for (1) automatic speech recognition (ASR) for all 22 languages, (2) neural machine translation (NMT) between all language pairs, (3) text-to-speech (TTS), (4) OCR for Indian scripts, (5) transliteration. Training data crowdsourced through Bhasha Daan citizen participation programme (6M+ contributions). Open-source models: IndicASR, IndicNMT, IndicTTS published on AI4Bharat. Integration with DIKSHA, eSanjeevani, MyGov citizen portal. Rate limit: free tier for government and NGOs, commercial tier for private sector.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

This is the HIGHEST PRIORITY reference case for UZ's sovereign language AI strategy (УП-189 mandates AI in Uzbek language). Bhashini architecture is directly applicable: Uzbek ASR + NMT + TTS. AI4Bharat open-source models (IndicASR) can be forked for Uzbek with modest training data investment. UZ has a clear AI language mandate and university-based NLP capacity (Tashkent IT university). Budget: $3-6M for Uzbek language AI platform using AI4Bharat as foundation. This becomes the language infrastructure layer for all government services.

## KG transferability (9/10)

Kyrgyz language AI is even more underdeveloped than Uzbek — there is essentially no production-grade Kyrgyz ASR or NMT as of 2026. Bhashini architecture provides the free open-source foundation. A joint UZ+KG language AI initiative (Uzbek + Kyrgyz + Russian trilingual platform) could be deployed for $5-10M total, serving both countries. UNDP, EU, and Korean NIPA language AI programmes are donor pathways.
