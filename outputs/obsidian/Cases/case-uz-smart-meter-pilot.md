# Uzbekistan Smart Meter Pilot (Toshkentenergo) — ACTIVE

**Origin**: Uzbekistan  •  **Year**: 2022  •  **Sector**: Energy

## Problem solved

Uzbekistan has 11M+ electricity consumers with 30-40% of residential meters producing inaccurate readings, enabling energy theft and creating $300M+ annual revenue losses for Uzbekenergo. Manual meter reading requires 15,000+ meter readers with high error rates and corruption opportunities. Smart meters eliminate reading errors, enable time-of-use pricing, and provide real-time load data for grid management.

## Architecture

World Bank-supported pilot: 100,000 Toshkentenergo customer smart meters installed 2022-2024 as proof-of-concept for national 3M rollout. Metering technology: DLMS/COSEM standard, AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure), PLC (Power Line Communication) for data transport in urban areas, GPRS backup for problematic connections. Head-end system: ACS (Automatic Control System) for meter data management. Consumer portal: web + app for self-service balance and consumption tracking. National rollout planned: 3M meters by 2028, estimated cost $450M (hardware). AI data analytics planned as Phase 2.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

This is an active UZ case — transferability to UZ = this IS the reference. Key next phase: procuring the AI analytics layer on top of the head-end system. The Phase 2 opportunity is precisely an AI platform for energy theft detection, load forecasting, and grid optimisation using the smart meter data being collected now. Market entry window: 2026-2027 as Phase 2 scoping begins.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG has similar energy theft problem (estimated 25-35% losses in rural distribution). UZ pilot architecture (Iskraemeco meters + AMI) is directly applicable to KG. Lesson from UZ pilot: PLC reliability in Soviet-era wiring is poor — KG should evaluate GPRS-only or NB-IoT for last-mile communication. KG scale: 1.2M meters, $150-200M hardware investment.
