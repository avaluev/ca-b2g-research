# Singapore SPF i-Witness Crime Reporting & Analytics

**Origin**: Singapore  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Security & Public Order

## Problem solved

Singapore Police Force (SPF) needed to modernise evidence submission (citizens submitting photos/videos of incidents), reduce police workload from trivial reports, and improve crime pattern analysis. Digital evidence from citizens was arriving via email and WhatsApp in unstructured formats that overwhelmed analyst capacity.

## Architecture

i-Witness digital platform: citizen-facing web/app for crime reporting with structured input forms, geolocation tagging, and digital evidence upload (photos, video, screenshots). AI backend: image and video content analysis (detecting relevant vs irrelevant evidence uploads), report classification (crime type tagger), duplicate report detection, crime heat map generation for patrol resource allocation. SPF Command Centre: real-time dashboard showing incident clusters, resource allocation recommendations. Integration with SPF case management system. NLP: automated routing of written reports to correct investigative unit.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

UZ MIA (Ministry of Internal Affairs) has citizen-reporting portals but lacks AI analytics layer. Crime pattern analysis and patrol optimisation have government support. Key concern: AI analytics must not embed racial or socioeconomic profiling. Budget: $3-6M for UZ crime reporting AI upgrade. Sensitive politically — avoid predictive policing features.

## KG transferability (6/10)

KG police have a citizens' complaint portal. AI triage to reduce officer workload is valuable in a police force facing budget constraints. The patrol optimisation module is highest-value for Bishkek. Budget: $2-4M. Donor pathway: OSCE, UNODC, UNDP rule-of-law programmes in KG.
