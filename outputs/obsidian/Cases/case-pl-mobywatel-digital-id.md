# Poland mObywatel App (Soviet-Bloc Evolution Reference)

**Origin**: Poland  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

Poland, like UZ and KG, has a post-Soviet administrative legacy. Citizens needed to carry physical documents for employment verification, benefits access, vehicle registration, and border crossing. mObywatel (my citizen) app digitised all personal documents into a single mobile app — most relevantly for labour migration: digital employment authorization for foreign workers in Poland.

## Architecture

Ministry of Digital Affairs Poland: mObywatel mobile app providing: digital ID document (legally equivalent to physical ID), digital driving licence, vehicle registration card, vaccination certificate, employment authorization for foreign workers (Ukrainians, Belarusians). NFC chip reading from biometric card. Backend: national eID infrastructure (profil zaufany + eID). Work authorization: digital visa and work permit replacement, employer verification API. COVID additions: digital quarantine notice, isolation certificate. Deep Poland-Ukraine cooperation: Ukrainian Diia documents accepted by Polish mObywatel verification.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

mObywatel employment authorization module is directly applicable to managing UZ workers in KZ/Russia (bilateral digital permit system). UZ MyID + digital employment permit is achievable by extending existing mobile app. Key lesson: start with high-demand documents (ID + driver's licence + vehicle registration) before adding all documents. Budget: $3-5M within existing MyID programme.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG workers in Russia (800,000) would benefit enormously from digital work permit that Russian employers can verify via API. EAEU has provision for digital labour documents — KG should leverage mObywatel architecture to build compliant digital employment document for EAEU market. Budget: $2-4M.
