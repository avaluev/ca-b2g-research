# UAE MoHRE AI Labour Inspection System

**Origin**: UAE  •  **Year**: 2020  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

UAE Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) needed to modernise labour inspection for 3.8M+ migrant workers. Manual inspection visited 10% of worksites annually — inadequate for detecting wage theft, unsafe conditions, and contract fraud. AI risk scoring of workplaces could target inspections on highest-risk employers while reducing compliance burden on compliant employers.

## Architecture

AI Labour Inspection System: risk scoring model for 300,000+ registered employers using: wage payment patterns (via WPS — Wages Protection System electronic payroll monitoring), worker complaint data, inspection history, industry risk profile, nationality composition, turnover rate. High-risk employers flagged for inspection; low-risk employers extended to biennial inspection cycle. AI chatbot for worker complaints: 5 languages (Arabic, English, Hindi, Urdu, Filipino), available 24/7. Predictive analytics: heat-related illness risk scoring by outdoor employer type during summer months.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

UZ has large construction, agriculture, and textile sectors with documented labour violations. MoHRE-equivalent AI labour inspection targeting would improve enforcement efficiency. UZ has a payroll reporting system (GNK) that could serve as WPS-equivalent. Budget: $5-8M for AI labour inspection platform. ILO, World Bank social protection projects as donors. Key lesson: worker complaint multilingual chatbot must work in Uzbek, Russian, and relevant migrant worker languages.

## KG transferability (6/10)

KG labour inspection has very limited resources. AI risk scoring to focus limited inspectors on highest-risk employers is high-value. KG's construction and mining sectors have documented safety violations. Budget: $2-4M. ILO KG programme is the donor pathway.
