# India e-Shram National Database of Unorganised Workers

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

India has 400M+ unorganised/informal workers (construction, agriculture, domestic work, gig economy) without any formal employment registration, social security, or government data visibility. COVID exposed this gap — government couldn't identify or reach informal workers for relief payments. e-Shram created a universal registration system for informal workers, providing a digital identity for the unorganised sector.

## Architecture

National portal + Aadhaar-linked registration: informal worker self-registers with phone number + Aadhaar + trade/occupation. Universal Account Number (UAN) issued. Database linked to: PMSBY (accident insurance, ₹2 lakh auto-enrolled), PMJJBY (life insurance), construction worker welfare fund portals, state-level welfare schemes. AI analytics: occupation demand forecasting, migration pattern analysis (workers registering from different states), skill gap identification for government training programmes. Integration with EPFO for formalization tracking.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ has 5M+ informal workers (agriculture, construction, trade). No government data on this workforce. Mahalla system provides the PHYSICAL REGISTRATION NETWORK — e-Shram registration at mahalla offices is feasible. UZ digital social protection reform (World Bank DRM TA) includes informal worker tracking. Budget: $4-7M for UZ informal worker registry (using MyID as identity anchor). ILO and World Bank social protection projects as donors.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG has massive informal sector (50-60% of employment). Circular migration to Russia creates data gaps. e-Shram equivalent for KG would enable: social transfer targeting, disaster relief distribution, and labour market analytics. GRS biometric ID as identity anchor. Budget: $2-4M. ILO KG programme, World Bank social protection project.
