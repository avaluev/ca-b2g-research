# New Zealand MfE AI Emissions Reporting

**Origin**: New Zealand  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Environment & Climate

## Problem solved

New Zealand's Ministry for the Environment (MfE) needed to improve greenhouse gas inventory accuracy, especially for agricultural emissions (methane from livestock — 50% of NZ's GHG). AI-assisted satellite and sensor data analysis could improve agricultural emission estimates beyond traditional activity-based calculations.

## Architecture

Multi-source emissions monitoring: agricultural CH4 estimates using satellite methane column measurements (TROPOMI Sentinel-5P), livestock density maps (from aerial survey + ML), land use change detection. Urban emissions: traffic emissions AI using vehicle counts × emission factors × congestion multipliers. Industrial: automated reconciliation between industry-reported data and satellite NO2/SO2 measurements for anomaly flagging. All integrated into NZ Greenhouse Gas Inventory system. Public dashboard: real-time emissions tracking by sector/region.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

UZ has livestock and cotton agriculture emissions that are poorly quantified. Paris Agreement reporting requires improved GHG inventory. NZ architecture using free TROPOMI data is applicable for $3-5M. Donor pathway: GEF, ADB climate finance. Key: UZ GHG reporting accuracy is needed for accessing climate finance (carbon credit markets, green bonds).

## KG transferability (6/10)

KG has significant livestock methane emissions (9M livestock) and hydro power sector (which has its own reservoir methane emissions). Paris Agreement reporting improvement. Donor pathway: GEF, World Bank. Budget: $2-3M.
