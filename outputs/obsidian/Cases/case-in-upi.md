# India UPI Unified Payments Interface

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2016  •  **Sector**: Finance & Fiscal

## Problem solved

India needed a real-time payment infrastructure that would enable bank-to-bank transfers without cash, card machines, or expensive clearing infrastructure. Existing payment systems (NEFT, RTGS) were batch-based, slow, and inaccessible to small merchants. UPI created an interoperable, instant, zero-cost layer on top of existing bank accounts accessible via QR codes and phone numbers.

## Architecture

NPCI-operated interoperability layer: UPI ID (user@bank_name) abstracts bank account details. Payment rails: IMPS (Immediate Payment Service) backend for settlement. Mobile SDK + UPI QR standard: any Android/iOS app can send/receive UPI payments. Merchant integration: static QR on any surface enables payment without hardware. TPAP (Third Party Application Providers): PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM all run on same rails. Zero merchant discount rate (MDR) mandated by government for person-to-merchant transactions. API: 50+ banks connected to UPI switch.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ payment ecosystem has UZCARD and HUMO cards but lacks a true interoperable real-time payment layer with zero merchant fees. UPI architecture — NPCI-equivalent operated by Central Bank of Uzbekistan, UZCARD + HUMO + bank ecosystem connected — would transform UZ digital commerce. Presidential priority on cashless economy. Budget: $15-25M for UZ payment interoperability layer. NPCI has offered bilateral assistance. India-UZ bilateral digital cooperation MOU is in place.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG digital som (CBDC, in force January 2027 per law) could incorporate UPI-style interoperability from inception. NBKR (National Bank KR) is already studying payment rail design. UPI architecture provides the real-time settlement layer on which digital som wallets would operate. KG's smaller scale (6.5M population) makes this more manageable. NBKR has engaged with NPCI in international payment forums.
