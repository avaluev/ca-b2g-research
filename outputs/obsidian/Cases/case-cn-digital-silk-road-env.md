# China BeiDou Navigation + Environmental Monitoring in Central Asia

**Origin**: China  •  **Year**: 2020  •  **Sector**: Environment & Climate

## Problem solved

China promoted BeiDou satellite navigation as a GPS alternative across BRI partner countries. In Central Asia, BeiDou-based applications were deployed for agricultural monitoring, flood early warning in mountain areas, and infrastructure monitoring along the Karakoram Highway and China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan rail corridors.

## Architecture

BeiDou III constellation (2020 fully operational): precision navigation + SMS capability + short-message emergency service for remote areas without cellular coverage. CA applications: (1) Agricultural IoT: BeiDou-connected soil sensors for precision farming along BRI agricultural zones; (2) Slope stability monitoring: BeiDou GNSS displacement sensors on mountain slopes along CKU (China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan) planned rail route; (3) Fleet management: BeiDou tracking for cross-border transport. No cellular coverage required — BeiDou provides direct satellite communication link.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

BeiDou is ALREADY in use in UZ. The monitoring applications (agriculture IoT, slope stability) are relevant. However, Western donor projects will not procure BeiDou-primary systems. Score 6: medium transferability with geopolitical caveat. UZ can use BeiDou for applications funded without Western donor restrictions (national budget, IsDB, AIIB, EDB).

## KG transferability (6/10)

Same caveat. KG remote mountain areas where GPS accuracy is poor and cellular is absent — BeiDou emergency messaging is the only option. Slope monitoring along CKU rail corridor is a Chinese-funded project that naturally uses BeiDou. Score 6 with same geopolitical asterisk.
