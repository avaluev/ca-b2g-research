# Finland Aurora AI — National AI Strategy for Public Services

**Origin**: Finland  •  **Year**: 2017  •  **Sector**: Environment & Climate

## Problem solved

Finland needed to apply AI systematically across government services, particularly in areas where Finland has competitive advantage: forestry management, cold-climate agriculture, and environmental data analytics. The Aurora AI programme identified 150+ government AI use cases and piloted the highest-priority ones across 10 government agencies.

## Architecture

Government AI coordination programme: Aurora AI task force under Prime Minister's Office identifying AI opportunities across all ministries. Key environmental implementations: (1) MAVI satellite-based agricultural subsidy verification (replacing field inspections); (2) Forestry AI: drone + satellite analysis of forest health, illegal logging detection; (3) Helsinki environmental twin (building permit environmental impact AI). Technical platform: Azure Government for most AI deployments. Open AI register: Finland publishes list of all government AI systems (algorithmic transparency). AI ethics board reviews all citizen-facing AI.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

Finland's MAVI agricultural subsidy satellite verification is the MOST DIRECTLY APPLICABLE component for UZ: $35M/year savings from eliminating field inspections maps proportionally to $100M+/year potential for UZ cotton subsidies. The forestry AI is applicable to UZ's Uzbekistan afforestation programme (Yashil Makon). Budget: $3-5M for UZ satellite subsidy verification (agriculture component). Finland's open AI register concept is a governance good practice UZ could adopt for trust-building.

## KG transferability (6/10)

Finland's forestry drone monitoring is applicable to KG's deforestation crisis in Fergana range and Chui Valley. The open AI register transparency model is directly applicable — KG Цифровой Кодекс (2025) includes provisions for AI accountability. Budget: $2-4M for KG environmental AI monitoring. Nordic-Baltic cooperation channel provides diplomatic pathway.
