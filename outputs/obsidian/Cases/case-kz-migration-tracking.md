# Kazakhstan Digital Labour Migration Monitoring System

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan receives 500,000+ labour migrants annually (primarily from Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan) and has 1M+ Kazakhstani workers in Russia. Manual migration tracking through border control stamps and employer work permit applications was inefficient, allowed illegal employment, and produced 6-month-delayed labour market statistics. Digital tracking would enable real-time labour migration analytics and compliant work permit management.

## Architecture

Integrated migration information system: border crossing data (CAMS) linked to work permit registry (Ministry of Labour), employer digital notification of migrant hiring (obligatory within 48 hours via eGov), social insurance contribution tracking for migrants (ENBEK + ENOF integration). AI module: migration flow prediction (seasonal patterns from border data + economic indicators), fake document detection (comparison of submitted work documents against national databases). Mobile app for migrants: digital work permit check + employer rating + complaint mechanism.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ has 2M+ citizens working abroad (Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkey, UAE). Digital work permit system + bilateral data sharing with KZ reduces exploitation of UZ workers. For inbound migration: UZ receives Kyrgyz and Afghan agricultural workers (informal). Digital employment registration for foreign workers in UZ improves tax collection and labour rights. Budget: $5-8M. IOM and ILO are active UZ partners.

## KG transferability (9/10)

KG has the most critical need: 800,000 KG citizens (12% of population) work in Russia. Bilateral KZ-KG data sharing on KG workers in KZ is already partially established. KG digital migration tracking from the source country side would enable: remittance tracking, skills mapping of returning migrants, social transfer continuity during migration. Budget: $3-5M. IOM KG programme active. This is the MOST IMPORTANT Labor case for KG.
