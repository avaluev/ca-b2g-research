# Kazakhstan Water Canal AI Monitoring (Syr Darya Basin)

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Agriculture & Water

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan's share of the Syr Darya river (feeding from Kyrgyz mountains through Uzbekistan to Kazakhstan) involves complex water allocation negotiations across 5 countries. KZ needed real-time monitoring of water flow at canal gates, automated reporting to the Interstate Commission for Water Coordination (ICWC), and predictive modelling for seasonal water allocation planning.

## Architecture

IoT sensor network on 1,200+ canal gates and pumping stations in South Kazakhstan and Kyzylorda regions: ultrasonic flow meters + water level sensors, GPRS/4G telemetry, central SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) system managed by KazVodKhoz. AI modules: flow anomaly detection (unauthorized water intake detection), seasonal water availability forecasting (using upstream hydrology data from Kyrgyz measurement points), irrigation demand prediction for downstream farmers. ICWC reporting: automated daily flow data submission. Platform: Wonderware (Aveva) SCADA + local AI analytics layer.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ operates the most extensive canal irrigation network in CA (4.5M ha irrigated). Canal gate monitoring for real-time water accounting is a GOVERNMENT PRIORITY aligned with Aral Sea restoration commitments. World Bank NSDI project includes water infrastructure components. Key: UZ can build on KZ SCADA architecture using Chinese IoT sensors (cheaper for scale) + Russian SCADA systems already familiar to UZ engineers. Budget: $15-30M for national coverage. World Bank + ADB joint funding pathway.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG is the headwater country for Syr Darya and Naryn rivers — upstream water monitoring in Naryn and Toktogul reservoir catchment is strategically critical for KG's negotiating position in ICWC. A KG water monitoring system would provide BOTH domestic benefit AND diplomatic leverage with downstream Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Budget: $5-8M for Naryn basin monitoring. Donor pathway: UNDP, World Bank, GEF water projects.
