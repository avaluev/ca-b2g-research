# Mongolia E-Mongolia Digital Education Platform

**Origin**: Mongolia  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

Mongolia's harsh climate and nomadic population create unique educational delivery challenges — children in herder families travel to boarding schools seasonally, while rural schools have severe teacher shortages. E-Mongolia's education component (built with KOICA support) created a digital platform delivering curriculum content, teacher professional development, and student assessment for a population scattered over a country twice the size of France.

## Architecture

KOICA-funded national LMS: Khan Academy-style video content in Mongolian language, offline download mode for satellite and slow connectivity areas, biometric student attendance system at boarding schools, AI-based learning gap analysis per student cohort. Integration with national student registry (E-Mongolia citizen app). KOICA provided: platform development funding + Korean curriculum experts as content advisors + technical training for 2,000 teachers. Hosted on Mongolian government cloud. Language: Mongolian (Cyrillic script) + Russian interface for some content.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

UZ has a much larger base (3M+ school students) making Mongolia ($8.5M / 550k) roughly proportionate to a $45M UZ investment. More relevant as PROCESS reference: KOICA delivers EdTech using this methodology and is already in UZ. The offline-mode architecture for rural students is the key technical lesson. Key lesson: ensure government maintenance budget is locked in before KOICA exits.

## KG transferability (9/10)

Mongolia (3.3M population, nomadic) is the closest cultural-structural analog to KG (6.5M, rural-dispersed). KOICA Mongolia EdTech → KOICA KG EdTech is a natural programme extension. Mongolian Cyrillic platform is technically closest to KG Cyrillic-script requirements. Budget match: $4-6M KOICA KG programme would deliver KG equivalent. This is the most immediately actionable EdTech case for KG.
