# Brazil gov.br Digital Government Portal

**Origin**: Brazil  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Public Administration & e-Gov

## Problem solved

Brazil's 26 states and federal government each had separate portals (1,400+ .gov.br domains) with different authentication, UX, and service quality. Citizens needed separate logins for federal social security, driving licence, tax filing, and vaccine certificates. gov.br unified all federal services behind a single authenticated portal.

## Architecture

Single Sign-On platform using Login.gov.br: CPF (national ID) + password or biometric authentication. Progressive trust levels: Bronze (self-declaration), Silver (bank-verified), Gold (biometric via Serpro ID). Backend: 1,800+ connected federal services. API: gov.br Digital Certificate API used by 300+ private sector companies for KYC. COVID: digital certificate on gov.br saved programme months of time. AI: personalized service recommendations based on citizen profile and life events (birth, death, employment change). Analytics: SEGES dashboard tracking service delivery times across all agencies.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

Brazil's progressive trust level model (Bronze/Silver/Gold) is the right evolution path for UZ MyID — enabling partial access for citizens without full biometric verification while providing full access to those enrolled. The life events personalisation ('proactive government') is the EPIGU evolution UZ officials have stated publicly. Budget: $5-10M on existing EPIGU. UNDP South-South digital cooperation could fund Brazilian-UZ advisory exchange.

## KG transferability (7/10)

Brazil's progressive trust level model is highly applicable to KG where rural biometric enrollment coverage is incomplete. Bronze/Silver/Gold layers allow Tunduk services to be accessible even to citizens without GRS biometric card enrollment. Budget: $3-5M.
