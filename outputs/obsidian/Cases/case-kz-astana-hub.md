# Astana Hub Tech Innovation Centre

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Public Administration & e-Gov

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan lacked a critical mass of technology startups, and government innovation was siloed within individual ministries with no cross-cutting R&D-to-procurement pipeline. Astana Hub was created to aggregate tech companies under a regulatory sandbox, connect them to government procurement opportunities, provide startup support infrastructure, and attract diaspora and international tech talent back to Kazakhstan.

## Architecture

Special Economic Zone structure under Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) jurisdiction, allowing foreign ownership, English common-law contracts, and preferential tax treatment. 1,000+ resident companies by 2024. Core services: regulatory sandbox (fintech, healthtech, govtech), procurement matchmaking with government agencies, venture capital connections, accelerator programmes. Digital platform for company registration, reporting, and government tender notifications. Operates under Presidential decree with dedicated regulator (Astana Hub Management). Key programmes: Visa-free access for tech workers, fast-track company registration in 3 days.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

IT Park Uzbekistan (established 2019) is already an operational Astana Hub analog with tax incentives, visa-free access for tech workers, and 800+ resident companies. Key lesson from Astana Hub: the procurement pipeline from Hub residents to government agencies is the most important metric — IT Park must develop the same. Direct institutional parallel enables KZ-to-UZ advisory exchanges at low cost.

## KG transferability (5/10)

KG has the High-Tech Park (HTP) but at much smaller scale. Astana Hub's $120M investment level is beyond KG's budget. Key lesson: KG can implement the SOFT infrastructure (procurement sandbox, regulatory fast-track, startup-to-government matchmaking) without the heavy physical infrastructure. Digital-first Astana Hub equivalent in KG would cost $5-15M. HTP leadership has visited Astana Hub per public reports.
