# UAE Louvre Abu Dhabi AI Visitor Experience

**Origin**: UAE  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Tourism & Culture

## Problem solved

Louvre Abu Dhabi (opened 2017) needed to create a world-class multilingual visitor experience for a culturally diverse visitor base (Arabic, French, English, Russian, Chinese, South Asian languages) while positioning Abu Dhabi as a global cultural destination. AI-powered multilingual guide and personalized artwork recommendation system replaced traditional audio guide rentals.

## Architecture

Mobile app with AI guide: NLP-based artwork query system (visitor photographs artwork → AI identifies + provides multilingual description + related works). Personalized tour recommendation: ML model based on visitor's time available, interests stated at entry, previous artwork interactions. Crowd management: beacon-based visitor tracking (anonymous) feeds density model for staff deployment. Partnership with Artlens AI (Cleveland Museum model). Multilingual NLP: 9 languages including Arabic (MSA + dialects). Backend: Azure + AWS hybrid.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

UZ Samarkand/Bukhara heritage institutions could deploy equivalent: visitor photographs Islamic tilework → AI explains Timurid architectural motifs in Russian/Uzbek/English/Chinese/Arabic. App download penetration at major sites (Registan, Kalon Minaret) could easily reach 50%+. Budget: $2-4M for AI heritage guide (Artlens-equivalent or custom). Tourism Committee has budget authority.

## KG transferability (5/10)

KG heritage institutions are smaller and less digitised than UZ. Simpler audio guide with AI multilingual translation is achievable first ($500k-1M) before investing in full AR heritage guide. World Nomad Games venue guide app would be the pilot application.
