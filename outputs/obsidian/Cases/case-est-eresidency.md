# Estonia e-Residency — Digital Governance Export Programme

**Origin**: Estonia  •  **Year**: 2014  •  **Sector**: Cross-cutting

## Problem solved

Estonia discovered its digital government infrastructure had global commercial value: entrepreneurs worldwide wanted access to Estonia's business-friendly, EU-compliant digital legal environment without physically relocating. e-Residency gives non-citizens a government-issued digital identity that allows running an EU-compliant company, signing contracts, and accessing EU banking without visa requirements.

## Architecture

Programme: e-Resident digital ID card (government-issued, different from citizen ID but same cryptographic infrastructure). Usage: company registration in Estonia online (3 hours, €200), access to EU banking for e-Resident companies, digital contract signing enforceable under EU law, access to Estonia's digital services as a business. X-Road integration: e-Residents can query Estonian company registry, tax filings. EGA and Enterprise Estonia operate jointly. 1,000+ countries represented. Diaspora-focused: Ukrainians, Russians, Georgians are top applicants.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

Full e-Residency model not transferable (UZ is not EU). BUT: the DIASPORA BRIDGE concept — a digital identity for Uzbek diaspora abroad that allows investment facilitation, remote company registration in UZ IT Park, and cultural engagement — is directly applicable. IT Park already has a similar concept in early stages. Budget: $1-3M for UZ Digital Diaspora programme. This is the Lens 5 (Diaspora Bridge) application case.

## KG transferability (6/10)

KG has 800,000+ workers abroad — diaspora digital bridge could enable remittance reduction (direct to KG government investment platforms vs expensive Western Union), HTP investment facilitation, and skills mapping. Budget: $500k-1.5M. Cultural resonance: 'Digital Kyrgyz' status for diaspora is politically appealing.
