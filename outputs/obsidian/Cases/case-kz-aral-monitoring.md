# Kazakhstan Aral Sea Restoration AI Monitoring

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Environment & Climate

## Problem solved

The Aral Sea (shared by KZ and UZ) shrank to 10% of its original size due to Soviet-era irrigation diversion. The North Aral Sea (KZ side) partially recovered after the Kokaral Dam (2005) but required continuous environmental monitoring: water level, salinity, biodiversity, and dust storm frequency. Manual monitoring was infrequent and expensive. AI satellite analysis enabled continuous environmental change detection.

## Architecture

Satellite change detection platform: Sentinel-1 (SAR) + Sentinel-2 (optical) + Landsat time series for water surface monitoring. Change detection algorithm: NDWI (Normalized Difference Water Index) time series analysis with anomaly detection. Air quality monitoring: MODIS aerosol optical depth for dust storm detection from dried seabed. Biodiversity: drone survey AI for vegetation and bird population counting. Data portal: public-access dashboard showing water level trend, salinity estimates, dust storm frequency. World Bank co-funded component for UZ Southern Aral.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ is the most directly affected country by Aral Sea disaster (Southern Aral dried completely). Satellite monitoring of Southern Aral dust storms is a PUBLIC HEALTH PRIORITY (Karakalpakstan respiratory disease rates are 3x national average from dust exposure). World Bank NSDI project includes environment monitoring component. IFAS provides multilateral framework. Budget: included in existing World Bank projects — incremental cost is $1-2M for AI analytics layer.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG's environmental monitoring priorities are different (mountain glacier retreat, lake level changes, forest degradation) but the satellite monitoring architecture is identical. Issyk-Kul lake monitoring, Tien Shan glacier retreat tracking, and Toktogul reservoir level monitoring are all achievable with the same AI satellite analysis pipeline. Budget: $2-4M for KG environmental AI monitoring. UNDP, GEF, ADB environment projects.
