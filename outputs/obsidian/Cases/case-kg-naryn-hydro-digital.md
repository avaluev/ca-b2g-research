# Kyrgyzstan Toktogul Reservoir Digital Management (Pilot)

**Origin**: Kyrgyzstan  •  **Year**: 2020  •  **Sector**: Energy

## Problem solved

Toktogul Reservoir (capacity: 19.5 billion m³) is Central Asia's largest hydroelectric facility and controls water supply for KG, KZ, and UZ downstream. Manual reservoir management led to suboptimal water release decisions — discharging too much water in winter (electricity generation priority) at the expense of summer irrigation downstream. Automated predictive management could balance energy generation and water supply needs across seasons.

## Architecture

World Bank-funded pilot: IoT sensor network on dam gates and tributaries, weather integration (KG Hydromet), seasonal inflow forecasting model (regression + neural network). SCADA upgrade from Soviet-era relay system to modern DCS (Distributed Control System). AI module: multi-objective optimisation for water release scheduling balancing energy generation revenue vs downstream agricultural water delivery commitments. Dashboard for Energo engineers and inter-agency reporting to ICWC.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ is DIRECTLY AFFECTED by Toktogul management decisions as downstream water user. The AI scheduling model used in KG pilot is applicable to UZ's own reservoirs (Andijan, Charvak). More importantly: this case demonstrates that the KG-UZ water conflict is manageable through transparent AI-based scheduling with joint monitoring — a diplomatic use case as much as a technical one. Budget: $5-8M for UZ reservoir digital management with ADB/World Bank water project funding.

## KG transferability (9/10)

This IS a KG case — included as reference. KG Phase 2 expansion (all 8 Naryn cascade dams) is the ACTIVE procurement opportunity. The AI scheduling model would be the consulting/software component of the expansion. Budget: $20-35M for Phase 2 (hardware + software + consulting). World Bank Energy Sector project is the funding vehicle. UDP digital department post-reorganisation may have oversight on this strategic infrastructure.
