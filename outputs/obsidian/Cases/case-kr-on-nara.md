# Korea On-nara / Government 24 Public Service Portal

**Origin**: Korea  •  **Year**: 2005  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

Korea's government services were fragmented across 200+ agency websites with different authentication systems, document requirements, and processing times. Citizens needing certificates (birth, family relations, property) visited physical offices. Ministry of the Interior launched Government 24 (민원24/정부24) as a single digital portal for all government services, and On-nara BPS as the government-wide business process system for internal government workflows.

## Architecture

Two complementary systems: (1) Government 24 (gov.kr) — citizen-facing: 4,000+ government services behind single SSO (KR인증, GPKI), real-time processing for simple certificates, bulk document ordering for businesses. Connects 4,500+ central and local government agencies; (2) On-nara BPS — internal-facing: government-wide document management and workflow system used by 200,000+ civil servants. Integration layer: National Information Network connecting all agencies. AI added 2021: AI chatbot for service navigation, AI document classification, predictive analytics for peak load management.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

KOICA is one of the most active donors in UZ digital space ($14M IT Education project). Korea-UZ government digital cooperation is formalized. Government 24 is the architectural reference for EPIGU 1000+ services. On-nara BPS is the reference for UZ internal government document management (still largely paper-based at ministry level). Budget: KOICA-funded advisory + partial system deployment within existing ODA envelope. Key lesson: internal workflow digitisation (On-nara equivalent) is essential companion to citizen portal.

## KG transferability (6/10)

KOICA KG programme is smaller than UZ but active. Government 24 architecture is over-sized for KG standalone. Key: KOICA could fund a KG version of On-nara BPS for internal government workflow as KG ministerial reform tool — cost $3-5M. This would be the most immediate quick-win within the KOICA ODA pipeline.
