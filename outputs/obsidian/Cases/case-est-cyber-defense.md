# Estonia National Cyber Defence Centre (NCSC & CERT-EE)

**Origin**: Estonia  •  **Year**: 2008  •  **Sector**: Security & Public Order

## Problem solved

Estonia's 2007 cyberattack (DDoS on Estonian government and banking systems — attributed to Russian state actors) exposed critical infrastructure vulnerabilities and demonstrated that e-government is only as strong as its cybersecurity. Estonia built a comprehensive national cyber defence architecture to protect digital government services.

## Architecture

Three-pillar architecture: (1) CERT-EE (Computer Emergency Response Team) — 24/7 incident response, threat intelligence sharing with EU/NATO partners, national vulnerability disclosure programme; (2) NCSC-EE (National Cyber Security Centre) — policy, standards, cybersecurity requirements for critical infrastructure; (3) Cyber Command (military) — national defence cyber capabilities. Key technical innovations: government network monitoring (ISKE baseline security framework for all government systems), national backup data embassies (copies of X-Road + land registry stored in Luxembourg and UK), cybersecurity exercise programme (Cyber Range at TalTech). Annual CyCon (Cyber Conference) hosted by NATO CCDCOE in Tallinn.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ cybersecurity law (2022) and UNICON.UZ (national cybersecurity SOC) provide the institutional foundation. CERT-EE architecture is the direct reference for UZ CERT enhancement. Data Embassy concept — backing up EPIGU and critical registries in a neutral foreign data centre — is politically appealing as sovereignty protection. EU bilateral cybersecurity cooperation programme (DigiConnect4CA) provides donor pathway. Budget: $5-10M for UZ CERT enhancement with Estonian advisory.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG's Цифровой Кодекс (2025) includes cybersecurity requirements. KG needs a functional CERT (currently exists on paper, limited capacity). Estonian CERT model — small team, automation-first, international threat intel sharing — is directly applicable at KG scale. Budget: $3-5M. Donor pathway: EU DigiConnect4CA, NATO Partners across the Globe programme (KG is a NATO partner country).
