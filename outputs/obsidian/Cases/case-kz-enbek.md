# Kazakhstan Enbek Labour Market Digital Platform

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan needed a unified labour market information system that would provide real-time employment analytics, skills gap identification, and AI-assisted job matching. Existing employment centres were paper-based with significant backlogs, and unemployment benefits were distributed without fraud detection or reemployment monitoring.

## Architecture

Enbek.kz platform (Ministry of Labour and Social Protection): job portal (employer posts + jobseeker profiles), ML job-skills matching algorithm (collaborative filtering on 500k+ job-seeker profiles), digital social contract for unemployed (digital sign-up for training vouchers + unemployment benefit + job placement commitment). Integration with GNK (tax committee) for payroll verification and benefit fraud detection. AI component: skills gap analytics by region and sector, predicting labour demand 12 months ahead. Mobile app for employers: instant hire with digital employment contract generation.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ Ministry of Labour is actively developing a digital employment platform. Enbek is the direct reference. UZ has 5M+ working-age unemployed/underemployed — the AI job matching and training voucher system is a PRESIDENTIAL PRIORITY (youth employment is a key political stability concern). Budget: $8-15M for UZ Enbek-equivalent. KOICA is already co-funding UZ labour market digital advisory. This is one of the strongest cases in the Labor batch.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG has 600,000 registered unemployed + significant seasonal labour migration to Russia/Kazakhstan. Enbek at KG scale ($4-6M) would enable digital social contract for unemployed, training voucher management, and remittance tracking. Integration with MFSD (Ministry of Finance and Social Development) for social payment integration. KOICA KG labour advisory is active.
