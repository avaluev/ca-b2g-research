# Bangladesh BOESL Digital Labour Export Management

**Origin**: Bangladesh  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Labor & Migration

## Problem solved

Bangladesh sends 700,000+ workers/year to Gulf states, Malaysia, and South Korea — but the process was dominated by unregulated manpower agents who charged illegal fees, falsified contracts, and abandoned workers. BOESL (Bureau of Overseas Employment and Training) needed digital tools to verify contracts, enable direct government-to-government worker placement, and track worker welfare post-arrival.

## Architecture

BOESL online platform: employer verification (foreign employers register with Bangladeshi missions abroad, verified via bilateral G2G agreements), worker application system linked to National ID (NID), digital employment contract with QR verification (prevents alteration), pre-departure briefing system (online modules), post-arrival welfare tracking (worker check-ins via app), complaint portal with embassy routing. AI component: contract fraud detection (ML on contract clauses identifying illegal terms like excessive work hours or unpaid overtime clauses), employer blacklist scoring. Smart Card for workers: NFC card with all employment details readable by Bangladesh missions.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

UZ has 2M+ migrant workers (Russia, KZ, Turkey, UAE) with documented exploitation. UZ BOESL-equivalent (Agentstvo po vneshnei trudovoi migratsii) needs digital platform for contract verification and worker welfare tracking. The KOICA-UZ Korea EPS labour programme is the immediate deployment context. Budget: $4-6M. IOM, ILO, KOICA are donors. Korea EPS integration is highest ROI application.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG BOESL-equivalent: DODF (Department of Overseas Development and Foreign Investment). 800,000 KG workers in Russia — digital contract verification and post-arrival welfare check would address documented exploitation. G2G model with Russia (via EAEU labour protocol) and South Korea (KG-Korea EPS exists). Budget: $2-4M. IOM, KOICA KG programme.
