# South Korea Sejong City AI Smart City Platform

**Origin**: Korea  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Transport & Urban

## Problem solved

Sejong was Korea's purpose-built administrative capital (pop 400,000) designed as a testbed for smart city technologies. The government needed to demonstrate commercial-scale deployment of AI urban management systems — autonomous vehicles, AI traffic management, smart energy grid, digital citizen services — to develop exportable Korean smart city products.

## Architecture

Sejong Smart City National Pilot Zone: (1) Autonomous Vehicle Corridor — 7.7km fully AV-ready road with 5G + V2X communications; (2) AI Traffic Management — 300+ adaptive signal intersections, incident detection; (3) Energy Management — AI-optimised community energy storage + building management; (4) City Data Hub — all city data on shared platform, API-accessible; (5) 60-minute delivery service (logistics drone pilot). Government investment: $2.5B in infrastructure over 5 years. KOICA exports Sejong model to developing countries as 'K-Smart City'.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

UZ announced Nurafshan Innovation Centre near Tashkent — a pilot smart city zone. Sejong is the direct reference. Modular adoption: AI traffic ($10-20M) + city data hub ($5-8M) for Tashkent without the $2.5B total investment. KOICA K-Smart City programme is the procurement pathway.

## KG transferability (4/10)

Sejong's scale is totally incompatible with KG. Only the AI traffic module ($3-5M for Bishkek) and city data hub concept are applicable. Score 4: aspirational reference, not practical at KG scale.
