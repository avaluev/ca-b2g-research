# Kazakhstan National AI Strategy 2024-2029

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2024  •  **Sector**: Cross-cutting

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan needed a comprehensive national AI governance framework — covering procurement standards, ethical guidelines, compute infrastructure, talent development, and regulatory sandbox — to guide $1B+ in planned AI investments through 2029 and position Kazakhstan as the regional AI hub.

## Architecture

Comprehensive strategy document adopted by Cabinet Resolution 2024: six pillars — (1) AI-powered public services; (2) AI regulation and ethics (first CIS AI ethics law); (3) AI talent (50,000 specialists target); (4) AI compute (national sovereign AI data centre); (5) AI ecosystem (Astana Hub AI vertical); (6) AI in priority sectors (healthcare, agriculture, finance). Operational: Digital Kazakhstan 2026 programme included $300M+ AI investment component. KAZNIIISA (National Institute for AI Standards) created as standards body. Annual National AI Report mandated.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ УП-189 AI strategy is 12-18 months behind KZ on implementation. KZ strategy execution details — specifically the KAZNIIISA standards work, the government AI reskilling programme design, and the Nvidia GPU infrastructure deal — are directly applicable to UZ AI Cluster design. UZ should negotiate similar Nvidia MoU for AI Cluster. Budget: KZ $1.2B is 3x UZ expected; UZ should target $300-400M with similar structure. KAZNIIISA advisory for UZ is available.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG National AI Strategy is in drafting stage (announced but not signed as of May 2026). KZ strategy structure is the reference. KG should adapt six-pillar framework to KG scale: scaled-down compute ($10-20M vs KZ $300M), 3,000 specialists target (vs KZ 50,000), 3-sector focus (finance, agriculture, health). UNDP and ADB can fund KG strategy implementation.
