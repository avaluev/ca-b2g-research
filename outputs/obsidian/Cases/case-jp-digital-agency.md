# Japan Digital Agency — Post-COVID Government Digitalisation Sprint

**Origin**: Japan  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Public Administration & e-Gov

## Problem solved

Japan's COVID response exposed the consequences of digital neglect: relief payments took 3+ months to reach citizens due to paper-based procedures, fax machines still used between ministries, and My Number (national ID) had 35% enrollment with zero digital utility. Digital Agency was created as a whole-of-government digital transformation unit reporting directly to Cabinet.

## Architecture

Japan Digital Agency (JDA): Cabinet-level agency with authority over IT standards across all ministries. My Number + My Number Card (physical + digital wallet on iPhone/Android) integration with health insurance, bank accounts, tax, and driver's licence. Digital Common Platform: API-first government system standards (no more legacy custom procurement — all systems must use standard APIs). Government cloud: Government System Standardisation Programme moving 1,700 government systems to cloud by 2025. AI: government-wide LLM policy (civils servants allowed Bing AI/Copilot with government tenant isolation).

## UZ transferability (6/10)

Japan's Digital Agency model — Cabinet-level authority over IT standards, cloud mandate, API standardisation — is the governance model UZ MoDT should study. UZ currently lacks the cross-government authority to enforce API standards. The LESSON not the technology: create a Cabinet-level digital authority with real power to mandate standards. Budget: governance change, not technology spend.

## KG transferability (5/10)

Japan's model is too large and too expensive for direct KG replication. Key lesson: the UDP digital department (post-April 2026 reorganisation) IS the equivalent of JDA — Presidential Administration-level authority over digital standards. KG should formalize UDP's authority explicitly in the new Положение (regulation) being drafted.
