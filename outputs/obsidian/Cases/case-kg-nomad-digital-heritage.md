# Kyrgyzstan Nomadic Heritage Digital Archive & Tourism AI

**Origin**: Kyrgyzstan  •  **Year**: 2021  •  **Sector**: Tourism & Culture

## Problem solved

Kyrgyzstan's intangible cultural heritage — Manas epic, komuz music, eagle hunting, nomadic yurt traditions — was underdocumented and inaccessible to international tourists and researchers. Digital archiving and AI-assisted multilingual presentation of nomadic culture could differentiate KG tourism from mass-market alternatives and support UNESCO nomination processes.

## Architecture

Emerging government-NGO programme: National Library KG + UNDP digital archive project — 3D scanning of artifacts, audio/video archive of Manas oral recitations, AI-generated multilingual audio guides (Russian, English, Chinese, Korean). AR application for displaying 3D yurt construction and eagle hunting techniques at World Nomad Games venues. AI content: NLP-based query-answering system trained on Kyrgyz oral literature corpus. Platform: local hosting at KG GIIN (State Institute of Intellectual Property). UNDP and UNESCO co-funded.

## UZ transferability (6/10)

UZ has rich tangible heritage (architecture) vs KG's intangible heritage (oral tradition). UZ can learn from KG's experience: don't over-invest in AR before content is properly digitised. UZ's priority is digital guide/chatbot for Samarkand and Bukhara using existing digitised content.

## KG transferability (8/10)

This IS the KG case and represents an active gap/opportunity. Phase 2 of the nomadic heritage digital programme — building on UNDP's 50,000 artifacts with AI-generated multilingual tour guidance — is the procurement opportunity. Budget: $3-5M. UNESCO and UNDP would co-fund. Tourism revenue potential from differentiated 'nomadic AI guide' product is $20-30M/year in tourist spending.
