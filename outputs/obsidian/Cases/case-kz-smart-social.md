# Kazakhstan Digital Social Protection — e-Otinish Social Registry

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2019  •  **Sector**: Cross-cutting

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan's social protection system distributed benefits to 3M+ recipients through paper applications, physical window visits, and manually verified income declarations. Targeting errors and fraud cost $300M+/year. e-Otinish (e-Application) digitised social benefit applications and created an AI means-testing system that cross-referenced income from 12 government databases.

## Architecture

e-Otinish platform (Ministry of Labour, ENOF pension fund): online and mobile application for all social benefits (housing, childcare, disability, pension supplements). AI means-testing: income cross-check across tax GNK, pension ENOF, property registry, vehicle registry, banking KQIB data. Application decision: instant pre-decision in 72% of cases; complex cases to officer review. Digital payments: benefits disbursed directly to citizen bank account (IBAN). Fraud analytics: lifestyle-based anomaly detection (declared low income + luxury purchase + overseas travel pattern flagging). Integration with X-Road equivalent (Smart Bridge).

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ social protection reform (World Bank TA) is actively digitising benefits administration. e-Otinish is the direct reference for UZ unified social benefit application (ASA — Agency for Social Assistance). KZ income cross-check architecture maps to UZ MyID + GNK + pension fund integration. Budget: $10-15M. World Bank social protection project is the donor pathway. This is the most directly actionable social protection case in the dataset.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG social protection (UISE — Unified Social Information System) is partially digital but lacks AI means-testing. e-Otinish at KG scale ($5-8M) would dramatically improve targeting and reduce fraud. KG social protection fund reform is an active World Bank project component.
