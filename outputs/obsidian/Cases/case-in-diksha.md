# DIKSHA National Digital Infrastructure for School Education (India)

**Origin**: India  •  **Year**: 2017  •  **Sector**: Education

## Problem solved

India's 1.5M government schools used 22 different state-level textbook and LMS platforms with no interoperability, making national curriculum alignment, teacher training delivery, and student progress tracking impossible. DIKSHA (Digital Infrastructure for Knowledge Sharing) created an open, federated national education platform aggregating content from all states while allowing each state to maintain its own branding and curriculum.

## Architecture

Sunbird open-source platform (MIT licence) as the technical foundation: content management system (ECML format for interactive content), learner registry, teacher registry, assessment engine, and analytics. Federated data model: states maintain sovereign data, national layer provides interoperability APIs. QR code-based textbook augmentation (energy: scan textbook QR code → access digital content). Mobile-first: works on 2G with compressed content. WhatsApp delivery channel added for regions with no apps. AI features: automated content tagging, learning outcome gap analysis, teacher performance analytics.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ is building AI in Schools initiative (15 AI labs in universities + school AI curriculum by 2026 per УП-189). DIKSHA Sunbird platform is the free open-source foundation for UZ's digital education infrastructure. The QR-code textbook model is immediately deployable: UZ already prints national textbooks centrally — adding QR codes in next print cycle costs <$500k and links 5M+ students to digital content instantly. Sunbird can run in Uzbek language. Donor pathway: KOICA (already in UZ education), UNICEF, World Bank.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG has 400,000 school students across 2,100 schools. Sunbird deployment for KG would be the smallest national Sunbird implementation globally — achievable in 18 months with a 5-person team. Kyrgyz-language content creation can be bootstrapped with KG's active teachers. KOICA KG education programme provides donor pathway. Key: KG Ministry of Education and Science has active digital education agenda.
