# Kazakhstan Nur-Sultan (Astana) Smart City Platform

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Transport & Urban

## Problem solved

Nur-Sultan (population 1.4M), a purpose-built capital, needed to deploy smart city infrastructure from scratch — no legacy systems to integrate. The government wanted a comprehensive urban operating system covering transport, utilities, safety, and city services in a single platform.

## Architecture

Nur-Sultan Smart City Centre: consolidated operations centre monitoring 5,000+ cameras (Hikvision), 500+ environmental sensors, public transport GPS fleet (800 buses), utility smart meters, and 911 emergency dispatch. Huawei IdeaHub as the command centre integration platform. AI modules: traffic optimisation (adaptive signals on 120 intersections), environmental monitoring (air + noise + temperature), utility leak detection, predictive crime analysis (sensitive — not public-facing). Digital twin of city built on Esri CityEngine. Mobile app for citizens: bus tracking, utility payments, city service requests, NFC-enabled payments at parking meters.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

Tashkent (3.3M pop) is larger and more complex than Nur-Sultan. The operational centre concept + traffic AI + utility monitoring modules are directly applicable. Recommendation: avoid Huawei platform (sovereignty risk) and use Russian or neutral vendor. Budget for Tashkent smart city pilot: $15-25M. Presidential priority makes budget allocation feasible. KOICA smart city ODA offers pathway.

## KG transferability (5/10)

Bishkek (1.1M pop) is comparable to Nur-Sultan population-wise. But budget is 10-15x what KG can deploy. Targeted adoption: traffic operations centre for Bishkek's 50 most congested intersections + public bus GPS tracking would cost $5-8M and deliver 60% of the value. UNDP urban development and ADB transport projects are donor pathways.
