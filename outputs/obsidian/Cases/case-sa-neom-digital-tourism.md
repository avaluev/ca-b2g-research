# Saudi Arabia Absher Tourism App & Digital Heritage

**Origin**: Saudi Arabia  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Tourism & Culture

## Problem solved

Saudi Arabia under Vision 2030 needed to open tourism to international visitors (previously restricted), issue tourist visas digitally, and digitise heritage site access (AlUla, Diriyah, Hegra) to attract 100M visitors/year by 2030. Cultural heritage was poorly digitised and visitor data uncollected.

## Architecture

Absher multi-purpose super-app (originally an ID/government services app) extended to tourism: e-Visa for 49 countries online, digital entry tickets for heritage sites with QR verification, AI multilingual tourist information chatbot (Arabic/English/Chinese/French), IoT-managed visitor flow control at heritage sites (density sensors triggering timed entry). AlUla app: AR overlays on Nabataean rock sculptures with AI object recognition to identify carvings. Heritage management AI: drone surveillance of restricted archaeological zones detecting unauthorized access.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

UZ has 7 UNESCO World Heritage Sites (Samarkand, Bukhara, Shakhrisabz, Itchan Kala, Shah-i-Zinda). Saudi AI visitor flow management is directly applicable to managing tourism at sensitive archaeological sites. UZ AI chatbot for tourism (Russian/Uzbek/English/Chinese/Arabic) is the key product opportunity. Budget: $5-8M for AI heritage tourism platform. Tourism Committee has budget and political priority.

## KG transferability (5/10)

KG has no UNESCO World Heritage Sites but has strong ecotourism potential. Saudi model is over-engineered for KG's tourism scale. Key applicable element: AI multilingual tourism chatbot (Russian/Kyrgyz/English) and digital ticketing for Issyk-Kul resort zone management. Budget: $2-3M. ADB tourism project as pathway.
