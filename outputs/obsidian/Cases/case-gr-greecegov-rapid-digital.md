# Greece Gov.gr Rapid Digital Transformation (COVID Era)

**Origin**: Greece  •  **Year**: 2020  •  **Sector**: Tourism & Culture

## Problem solved

Greece's tourism sector was threatened by COVID-19 — 30% of GDP dependent on tourism. The government needed to rapidly digitise government services AND create a tourism certification infrastructure (vaccination certificate + test result + COVID-free border crossing) to allow tourists to arrive safely. Gov.gr became the platform for both citizen services and COVID tourism management in 18 months.

## Architecture

Gov.gr platform (accelerated COVID build): citizen authentication via TaxisNet + Gsis identity provider, digital COVID certificate (QR-verified, aligned with EU COVID passport standard), tourism reopening portal, vaccination status API for airlines and hotels. AI element: tourist inflow modelling to predict island healthcare capacity demand. Key speed achievement: built in 120 days by 250-person team, launched May 2020 before summer tourism season. Post-COVID: evolved into national citizen services portal with 500+ services. Architecture: microservices on AWS Greece region.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

Greece's tourism COVID certification is less relevant now but the SPEED OF BUILD lesson (120-day MVP with 250-person team) is directly applicable to UZ EPIGU expansion. UZ government has stated intent to rapidly expand digital services — Greece demonstrates the execution model. The specific tourism module (AI inflow modelling for heritage site capacity) is applicable to UZ Samarkand tourism management.

## KG transferability (6/10)

Speed-of-build lesson applicable. KG tourism COVID certification (post-COVID health certificate for visitors) architecture is the reference. AI tourist inflow modelling for Issyk-Kul resort zone capacity management is the specific application.
