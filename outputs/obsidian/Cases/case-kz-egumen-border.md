# Kazakhstan eGümrük Digital Customs & Border AI

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2016  •  **Sector**: Security & Public Order

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan's border checkpoints processed 1.5M+ cargo entries annually with manual customs declaration review that averaged 2-3 day clearance times, 30% of cargo physically inspected (unsustainable), and significant corruption at customs windows. The State Revenue Committee needed AI risk-scoring to focus inspections on high-risk cargo while fast-tracking compliant operators.

## Architecture

eGümrük platform (Committee of State Revenue, KRS): digital customs declaration (e-CD) submitted online; AI risk scoring engine evaluating 50+ parameters (commodity, origin, importer history, route, price anomaly); Green/Yellow/Red channel assignment; automatic fast-track for trusted traders (Green channel: no inspection, 2-hour clearance). ASTANA-1 electronic scanner integration: X-ray scanners feed images to AI image analysis for contraband detection. Integration with EAEU Common Customs Information Space (shared data with Russia, Belarus, Armenia). Mobile app for customs declarations at small border crossings.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

UZ GTK has deployed e-customs declaration but lacks the AI risk scoring layer. This is one of the highest-ROI applications in the entire tournament: UZ customs revenue leakage estimated at $500M-1B/year. Kazakhstan's $45M investment returned $180M+ in additional revenue in Year 1. KRS advisory available. EAEU common customs information space includes UZ aspirationally. Budget: $10-20M for UZ AI customs risk scoring. World Bank, ADB trade facilitation projects are donor pathways.

## KG transferability (8/10)

KG is a significant transit country on the Bishkek-Osh-China route. Customs AI risk scoring would address documented transit fraud (goods declared as transit but sold domestically). Budget: $5-10M. Donor pathway: World Bank trade facilitation, EU DigiConnect4CA (digital connectivity for CA). KRS advisory is available.
