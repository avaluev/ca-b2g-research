# Kazakhstan Biometric Border Control (SafePass & AutoPass)

**Origin**: Kazakhstan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Security & Public Order

## Problem solved

Kazakhstan international airports and major border crossings faced long queues (1-3 hours) for passport control. Border officers performed manual document inspection without biometric verification, relying on visual passport matching. Biometric AutoPass systems deployed AI facial recognition to verify identity and screen against watchlists in 10-15 seconds per person.

## Architecture

AutoPass kiosks at international airports (Nur-Sultan, Almaty): self-service passport scan + live facial recognition vs passport chip photo. Watchlist screening against INTERPOL SLTD (Stolen and Lost Travel Documents) + KZ national security watchlist. Integration with CAMS (Consolidated Automated Migration System) for real-time traveller risk scoring. Border Crossing System: police data exchange with EAEU common crossing database. Mobile version: Pre-registration QR code for frequent travellers (e-Mektebi frequent traveller programme). Vendor: NEC (Japan) facial recognition engine.

## UZ transferability (8/10)

Tashkent International Airport handles 5M+ passengers/year. UZ MIA has mandate for border modernisation. Biometric AutoPass is procurable under WB or EU transport project funding. NEC, Thales, and IDEMIA all have Central Asia presence. Budget: $10-15M for Tashkent + Samarkand airport biometric border. Avoid facial recognition at land borders (privacy risk, accuracy concerns) — airports only for Phase 1.

## KG transferability (7/10)

Manas International Airport (Bishkek) handles 2M+ passengers/year. KG border with China (Irkeshtam, Torugart) processes significant cargo and passenger flow. Biometric border at Manas Airport is achievable for $5-8M. Land border with China and Kazakhstan is high-priority for cargo risk scoring (not facial recognition — RFID-based commercial vehicle pre-clearance is better model here).
