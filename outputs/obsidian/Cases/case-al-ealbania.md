# e-Albania Rapid Digital Transformation

**Origin**: Albania  •  **Year**: 2016  •  **Sector**: Public Administration & e-Gov

## Problem solved

Albania's accession path to the EU required rapid digitisation of government services. Post-Communist administrative structures with endemic corruption and physical-only service delivery needed overhaul. A small country (2.8M people) with limited IT budget needed a way to rapidly deploy digital services without building separate systems for each agency.

## Architecture

NAIS (National Agency for Information Society) built a platform-based approach: National e-Government Portal (e-albania.al) as single entry point, APIs-first integration layer behind the portal, electronic document management system shared across agencies, national electronic registry inter-connection. Key enabling law: Law on Digital Document (2017) gave e-documents full legal validity. EU-funded SIGMA programme provided governance assistance. Unique element: all civil servants mandated to use digital workflow for internal processes, creating supply-side push for integration.

## UZ transferability (7/10)

Albania's 700-services-in-4-years trajectory is the most useful planning benchmark for UZ EPIGU expansion. UZ currently has ~350 digital services; target 1000+ by 2028. Albanian civil-servant-side digital mandate (internal document management) is the key lesson UZ MoDT can replicate via internal digitisation push. Budget: $25M over 4 years is within reach of a single World Bank loan component.

## KG transferability (8/10)

Albania (2.8M pop) is the closest population-comparable case to KG (6.5M). The small-country-fast-platform approach is highly transferable. Albania's timeline from 200 to 700 services in 4 years with EU donor support is exactly what KG's UDP digital department should target with EU DigiConnect4CA funding. Donor pathway alignment: EU active in both Albania and KG.
