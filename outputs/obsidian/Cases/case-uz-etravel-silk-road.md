# Uzbekistan eTravelUzbekistan Digital Tourism Platform

**Origin**: Uzbekistan  •  **Year**: 2018  •  **Sector**: Tourism & Culture

## Problem solved

Uzbekistan's tourism sector was severely underdeveloped relative to the Silk Road heritage (Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva — UNESCO World Heritage Sites). Visa processes were opaque, ticketing for heritage sites was paper-only, and tourist data was uncollected. The State Committee for Tourism and Tourism Development needed digital infrastructure to grow tourism from 1.7M (2016) to 7.5M visitors (2025 target).

## Architecture

e-Visa system (e.visa.gov.uz) — online application replacing embassy visits for 90+ countries; digital heritage site ticketing (e-ticket for all major sites with QR code entry); eTravelUzbekistan.com — integrated booking platform for hotels, guides, and tours with UZ Tax Committee e-receipt integration; AI recommendation engine for itinerary planning. Automated tourist statistics collection from e-ticket data. Mobile app: UzRoad navigator integrated with cultural site AR overlays. Integration with MyID for foreign visitor identity verification.

## UZ transferability (9/10)

This IS the UZ case. Key next phase: AI-powered tourist itinerary assistant (multilingual — Russian, English, Chinese, Arabic) leveraging heritage site ticketing data for personalised recommendations. AI chatbot for tourist inquiries accessible via Telegram. Budget: $3-5M for AI tourism assistant on top of existing digital infrastructure.

## KG transferability (7/10)

KG has significant tourism potential (Issyk-Kul, Tien Shan ecotourism, nomadic culture). UZ e-Visa architecture is directly replicable. KG needs: online visa + digital heritage ticketing + booking aggregator. Budget: $3-5M. Donor pathway: ADB tourism development project, World Bank.
