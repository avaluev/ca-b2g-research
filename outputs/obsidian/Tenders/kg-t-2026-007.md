# Virtual Asset Registry and Compliance Monitoring System for USDKG Stablecoin

**Issuer**: [[Institutions/kg-fsa|KG-FSA]]  •  **Deadline**: 2026-08-31  •  **Value**: $1,200,000

## Scope

Financial Services Authority procurement for USDKG stablecoin (launched on BNB Chain October 2025) compliance infrastructure. Scope: virtual asset registry, real-time transaction monitoring AI (AML), KYC platform, integration with National Bank digital som system, reporting to the National Council on Virtual Assets. Regulatory tech platform.

**Win probability (high)**: No incumbent — brand new regulatory domain. Crypto/virtual asset compliance tech (Chainalysis, Elliptic, Refinitiv) firms are natural candidates. KG is explicitly positioning as a crypto-friendly jurisdiction. FSA procurement is relatively accessible. CIS-based blockchain compliance firms (with Kyrgyz regulatory knowledge) are strongly competitive.
