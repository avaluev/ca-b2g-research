# Youth Digital Skills and Employment in Digital Economy — UNDP Programme

**Issuer**: [[Institutions/kg-mineco|KG-MINECO]]  •  **Deadline**: 2026-06-30  •  **Value**: $1,500,000

## Scope

UNDP Kyrgyzstan programme: digital skills training for youth employment in digital economy. Scope: platform development (Russian/Kyrgyz), AI literacy courses, coding bootcamps, HTP resident company job placement pipeline, women in tech component, rural digital access module. Targets: 5,000 youth trained in 2026.

HTP ecosystem (500+ resident IT companies) provides ready placement pipeline. Russian-language curriculum providers and Korean (KOICA-style) EdTech have strong references.
