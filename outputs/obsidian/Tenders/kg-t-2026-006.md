# Digital Health Implementation — Electronic Health Records and Telemedicine (UN Joint Programme)

**Issuer**: [[Institutions/kg-minzdrav|KG-MINZDRAV]]  •  **Deadline**: 2026-06-30  •  **Value**: $4,030,000

## Scope

UN Joint Programme (WHO+UNDP+UNFPA+UNICEF) funded by EU SDG Fund, Spain, Sweden. $4.03M total, 2024-2027. Scope: 60 health facilities EHR integration, 3M vaccination records, disability certificates system (120K users), sick leave digitization (280K workers), telemedicine expansion to rural/mountain areas serving 700K children. FHIR/HL7 standards required.

**Win probability (medium)**: UN procurement rules (CQS) are transparent. EU-funded mandate favors EU-standard technical approach (FHIR/HL7). Some subcomponents already underway (mid-implementation). Best opportunity is sub-contract for telemedicine platform, AI clinical decision support, or mobile health app. WHO/UNDP national office relationships are key.
