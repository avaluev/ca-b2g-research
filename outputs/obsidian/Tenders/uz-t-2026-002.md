**Issuer**: [[Institutions/uz-supreme-court|UZ-SUPREME-COURT]]  •  **Deadline**: 2026-07-01  •  **Value**: $8,000,000

## Scope

Full digital court case management system. Scope: electronic case filing (my.sud.uz portal), AI-powered legal assistant module (launched Jan 2026), automated court session audio transcription, biometric identification in courtrooms, integration with EPIGU and MyID, e-notification for parties. Initial pilot in Tashkent courtrooms, national rollout 2026-2027.

Joint venture requirement opens door for foreign AI/NLP component supplier. Turkish Havelsan (digital judiciary reference) and CIS vendors are positioned. Window exists for AI module subcontract.
