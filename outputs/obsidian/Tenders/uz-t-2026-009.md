# Development and Implementation of State Medical Insurance IT Platform (OSMS)

**Issuer**: [[Institutions/uz-minzdrav|UZ-MINZDRAV]]  •  **Deadline**: 2026-05-15  •  **Value**: $12,000,000

## Scope

National State Medical Insurance (OSMS) IT platform under ПП-311. Universal healthcare launched 2026. Platform: insurance registry, claims processing, fraud detection AI, provider network management, citizen self-service (EPIGU integration), payment gateway. Massive scale: entire population of 37M.

**Win probability (medium)**: New system with no incumbent. Government may prefer UZINFOCOM-led consortium. However, health insurance platform complexity and scale may require specialized international partner. KOICA (Korean health IT) or EU EBRD funding possible. JV requirement is achievable.
