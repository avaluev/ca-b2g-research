# AI customs risk profiling for Kyrgyzstan border crossings on China-Russia trade corridor

> [!important] Killer app
> Smart border management platform for KG customs: AI risk scoring of all inbound and transit goods at Dostuk/Torugart/Irkeshtam crossings; automated EAEU documentation verification; re-export routing analytics tracking goods from Chinese border entry to Russian exit; NII (non-intrusive inspection) image analysis AI — reducing legitimate cargo clearance time from 4+ hours to 45 minutes while improving duty collection by estimated 20% [Source: World Bank KG trade facilitation diagnostics 2023]

## Drivers

- KG re-export trade: $2B+ annually routing goods from China to Russia via KG — significant customs compliance pressure
- EU C4CA connectivity program includes customs facilitation for Central Asia
- ADB regional customs harmonization programs
- KG joining EAEU customs union creates unified customs requirement alignment
- EBRD trade facilitation programs in KG
