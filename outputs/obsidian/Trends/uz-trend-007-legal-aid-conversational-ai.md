# Conversational AI for free legal aid delivery in Uzbek/Russian ('Yuridik yordam' chatbot under MOJ)

> [!important] Killer app
> Legal aid chatbot operating under MOJ mandate, available via EPIGU and Telegram, guiding citizens through complex legal procedures (divorce, inheritance, labor disputes, housing registration) in plain Uzbek, reducing need for paid legal consultations and increasing formal justice system access in rural regions

## Drivers

- УП-140 virtual legal consultant mandate (Jan 1 2026 deadline)
- ЗРУ-1115 (Jan 2026) AI law — creates regulatory clarity for AI in legal guidance
- MOJ digitalization push since 2017 (Karimov Inversion)
- ПП-320 grant funding for AI implementation in state bodies including MOJ
