# AI-powered tourism platform for Issyk-Kul resort zone and Silk Road heritage sites in Kyrgyzstan

> [!important] Killer app
> Kyrgyzstan tourism intelligence platform: AI trip planner for Issyk-Kul covering 300+ resorts and adventure sports providers; multilingual audio guide for Osh bazaar, Burana Tower, and Silk Road sites (Russian/English/Chinese/Arabic); visitor flow analytics for resort operators; real-time weather and trail condition alerts for trekking routes — serving 3.5M annual visitors and supporting KG's fastest-growing industry with estimated $1.5B annual tourism revenue [Source: World Travel and Tourism Council CA 2024]

## Drivers

- KG tourism growing 25% annually — 3.5M visitors in 2024 targeting 5M by 2027 [Source: Kyrgyz Tourism Agency 2024]
- Issyk-Kul is top Central Asian resort destination for Russian/CIS tourists
- UNESCO potential for Tian-Shan 'Mountains of Heaven' (already nominated)
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: tourism digitalization
- Post-2022: Russian tourist flows to KG replaced Turkish/European flows to Russia — tourism boom
