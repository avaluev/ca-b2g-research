# National Electronic Health Record rollout and AI diagnostic support for Kyrgyzstan Ministry of Health

> [!important] Killer app
> EHR platform with AI clinical decision support: unified patient record accessible across 800+ health facilities via Tunduk; AI triage for primary care prioritizing TB, maternal health, and cardiovascular disease (KG's top mortality causes); telemedicine module connecting rural family physicians with Bishkek specialists; medication interaction checker integrated with KG national drug formulary — addressing the 15%+ mortality premium of KG rural vs. urban populations [Source: BMGF Global Burden of Disease CA 2023]

## Drivers

- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028 (УП-90): digital healthcare as priority sector
- World Bank, UNICEF, and BMGF active health digitalization programs in KG
- KG health system: 6M population, 60% rural — EHR and telemedicine both essential
- AKDN (Aga Khan Development Network) active in mountain healthcare digitalization
- Tunduk integration mandated for all health facility information systems under Digital Code
