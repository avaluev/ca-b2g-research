# CBDC digital som infrastructure: wallet platform, AML/KYC AI, and fraud detection for January 2027 launch

> [!important] Killer app
> Digital som infrastructure suite: (1) CBDC wallet platform for NBKR supporting both retail and wholesale digital som; (2) AI-powered AML engine processing all digital som transactions in real-time against FATF 40 recommendations; (3) KYC AI onboarding integrating with GRS (State Registration Service) biometric ID database via Tunduk; (4) offline payment protocol for remote mountain areas (USSD fallback) — the single most technically complex and highest-value B2G procurement in Kyrgyzstan history

## Drivers

- KG-LAW-2025-074 (Apr 2025): CBDC framework law signed, digital som in force January 1 2027
- NBKR has exclusive emission rights and operates digital som platform — largest B2G opportunity in KG fintech
- Pilot project running during 2026 — procurement window for infrastructure is NOW
- World Bank and IFC technical assistance to NBKR for digital currency implementation
- Russian/CIS substitution: Visa/Mastercard sanctions concerns; MIR not viable; own rails critical
