# Kyrgyzstan SOC modernization and critical infrastructure protection under Digital Code Chapter on AI security systems

> [!important] Killer app
> Sovereign AI-SOC for KG critical infrastructure: threat detection covering government networks, Tunduk inter-agency exchange, NBKR financial systems, and energy grid; automated incident response playbooks in Russian/Kyrgyz; Digital Code Chapter 23 compliance certification service; integration with EAEU cybersecurity cooperation mechanisms — serving the compliance demand created by Cabinet Resolution 746 for all 40+ critical infrastructure operators in KG

## Drivers

- KG-LAW-2024-121 cybersecurity law: mandatory SOC for critical infrastructure operators
- KG-CABINET-2024-746: specific technical requirements for cybersecurity systems — active compliance demand
- Digital Code 2025 Chapter 23: AI systems in security context require danger assessment
- EU C4CA: €100M includes cybersecurity training for KG (Estonia EGA-led)
- UDP Digital Dept takes over cybersecurity mandate — new TORs being drafted
