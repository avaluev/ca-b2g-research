# Kyrgyz-language sovereign LLM for government AI applications (Digital Code Chapter 23 compliance driver)

> [!important] Killer app
> KyrgyzLLM: Kyrgyz-Russian bilingual foundation model built on transfer learning from Kazakh (LLaMA-based) and Turkish language models with KG-specific fine-tuning on parliamentary records, legal documents, and educational materials — enabling Kyrgyz-language AI applications across Digital Code-mandated domains (government services, education, healthcare) without routing citizen data through foreign cloud providers

## Drivers

- Digital Code 2025 Chapter 23: high-risk AI requires danger assessment — Kyrgyz-language explainability requires Kyrgyz models
- National AI Council (УП-91 2025) mandate includes AI capability development
- Post-2022: no Western vendor prioritizes Kyrgyz language — substitution window open
- UDP Digital Department needs AI tools deployable without foreign data dependency
- Kyrgyz is Turkic language closely related to Kazakh — transfer learning from larger Kazakh/Turkish models feasible
