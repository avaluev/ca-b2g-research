# E-prescription fraud detection AI and pharmacovigilance analytics under State Medical Insurance system

> [!important] Killer app
> Insurance fraud detection module processing all e-prescription and claims data from the state medical insurance system: identifies anomalous prescription patterns (over-prescribing physicians, phantom patients, duplicated claims), generates real-time alerts for insurance auditors, and produces monthly pharmacovigilance reports for MOH — reducing medical fraud losses estimated at 15-20% of insurance fund value

## Drivers

- ПП-311 (Sep 2024): State medical insurance digital claims — electronic queue and referral from Jan 2025
- ПП-415 Electronic Healthcare: e-prescription infrastructure being built by UZINFOCOM
- Insurance fraud is primary budget drain — digital insurance system creates detection opportunity
- ПП-320 AI grant funding available for healthcare AI
