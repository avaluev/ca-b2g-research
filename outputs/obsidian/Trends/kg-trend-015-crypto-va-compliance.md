# AI-powered virtual asset compliance platform for KGST stablecoin (USDKG) and crypto exchange operators

> [!important] Killer app
> Crypto compliance platform for KG's $2B+ annual virtual asset transaction volume: on-chain transaction monitoring and wallet risk scoring for USDKG stablecoin and KGS-denominated crypto exchanges; automated FATF Travel Rule compliance for cross-border transfers; FSA regulatory reporting dashboard; suspicious activity report generation — serving the 30+ licensed KG crypto operators and positioning KG as the compliant crypto hub of CIS that Western jurisdictions cannot serve

## Drivers

- KG-LAW-2022-012-VIRTUAL (amended 2026): virtual assets law operational with licensed exchanges
- USDKG stablecoin launched October 2025 — compliance infrastructure needed
- National Council on Virtual Assets and Blockchain (2025) oversees sector
- KG is CIS jurisdiction with most liberal crypto regulation — attracts region-wide compliance need
- FSA (Financial Services Agency) under Mineconomy needs AI supervision tools for crypto sector
