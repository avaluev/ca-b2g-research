# New digital government TORs and project pipeline from UDP Digital Development Department (post-April 2026 reorganization)

> [!important] Killer app
> First-mover positioning in UDP Digital Department: provide technical advisory for new TOR drafting covering at least 3 legacy Mintsifry programs (Tunduk modernization, e-procurement upgrade, digital health) — creating specification influence before RFPs are formally published. The 90-day window between liquidation and first new procurement is the highest-leverage window in KG digital market history since Tunduk was created in 2018

## Drivers

- КГ-УП-2026-MINCIFRY-ABOLITION (Apr 2026): Ministry of Digital Development liquidated; all digital functions transferred to UDP (Presidential Administration)
- UDP creates: (1) Department of Digitalization and Innovative Technologies; (2) Dept of Telecom Regulation; (3) Digital Infrastructure Development Fund
- Every existing Mintsifry project now needs new TORs under UDP governance
- Japarov concentration: digital policy now directly under Presidential Administration — faster decisions but higher political sensitivity
- Implementation deadline: July 31 2026 for full asset transfer and new structural regulations
