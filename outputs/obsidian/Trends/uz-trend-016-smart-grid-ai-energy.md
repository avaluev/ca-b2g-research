# AI grid optimization and smart metering for Uzbekistan national power grid under energy sector digitalization

> [!important] Killer app
> AI energy management system for Uzbekenergo: real-time grid anomaly detection reducing non-technical losses (theft detection, meter fraud); 24-48 hour demand forecasting enabling optimal dispatch of hydro/solar/gas mix; integration with smart meter rollout covering 3M+ commercial consumers — projected 10-15% reduction in technical losses worth ~$180M annually [Source: EBRD Uzbekistan energy efficiency assessment 2023]

## Drivers

- ПП-358 AI Strategy: energy optimization as explicit priority sector
- UZ energy demand growing 5% annually — grid losses estimated at 15-20% [Source: World Bank Uzbekistan Energy Sector Review 2023]
- ПП-320 AI grant funding available for energy sector applications
- Renewable energy expansion (solar target 8GW by 2026) requires AI load balancing
- Uzbekenergo and Uztransgaz (state utilities) digitalization mandated under Digital Uzbekistan-2030
