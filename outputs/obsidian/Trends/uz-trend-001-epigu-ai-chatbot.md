# AI-powered citizen services chatbot for EPIGU portal (1000+ services, Russian-Uzbek bilingual)

> [!important] Killer app
> An AI assistant embedded in the EPIGU.uz portal that handles the top 50 most common citizen service requests (certificates, permits, registrations) in conversational Uzbek and Russian, reducing average service completion time from 3 days to same-day via automated back-end routing through Tunduk-equivalent data exchange layer

## Drivers

- УП-189 (Oct 2025) mandates 100 AI projects by end-2026 with Jan 1 2026 AI procurement start
- ПП-286 (Sep 2025) sets 70% electronic government services target with mahalla-level delivery
- ПП-320 (Oct 2025) allocates $50M state-body AI grants — EPIGU chatbot is top-tier priority
- УП-6079 Digital Uzbekistan-2030 foundational mandate
- Russian/CIS vendor retreat post-2022 creating vacuum for local-language AI deployment
