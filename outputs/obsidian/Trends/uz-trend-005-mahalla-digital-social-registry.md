# AI-driven Mahalla-level digital social registry and targeted social assistance platform

> [!important] Killer app
> AI-powered platform that automatically identifies mahalla-level households eligible for targeted state assistance by cross-referencing employment, utility payment, and health data — cutting false positive/negative rates in social targeting from 35%+ to under 10% while routing applications through automated workflow to the responsible agency

## Drivers

- ПП-286 (Sep 2025): 30+ government services delivered at mahalla level in 'Digital Government' program
- ПП-320 AI grants: social protection is priority AI domain
- UZ has 10,000+ mahallas (community units) — scale creates massive data and AI opportunity
- World Bank Social Protection Digital project pipeline
- Russian-language community governance tradition requiring localized UX
