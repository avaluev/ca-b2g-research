# AI-powered labor migration management and remittance digitization for Kyrgyzstan (800K+ migrants)

> [!important] Killer app
> Kyrgyz labor migration digital platform: AI skills portfolio builder for migrant workers (translates Russian-language work experience to international equivalents); digital labor contract compliance monitoring for KG workers abroad; remittance routing via digital som rails reducing cost from 7%+ to <2% on the $3B annual KG remittance corridor; returning migrant reintegration job matching — potentially adding $140M+ annually to KG families through reduced remittance costs alone [Source: World Bank Remittance Cost Database 2024]

## Drivers

- KG labor migration: 800K+ workers abroad primarily in Russia — remittances = 30% of GDP [Source: World Bank Remittances Data 2024]
- Post-2022: Russia-KG migration corridor disrupted, diversification to Turkey/UAE/Korea accelerating
- ILO migration management program in KG
- Digital som CBDC can reduce 7-10% remittance cost to 1-2% via digital rails
- KG Ministry of Labor migration digitalization program
