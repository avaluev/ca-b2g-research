# AI-enhanced national weather forecasting system (Mar 1 2026 deadline under УП-189)

> [!important] Killer app
> AI weather forecasting system for Uzbekistan: ML post-processing of ECMWF global model output with bias correction for Uzbekistan's complex topography (Fergana Valley vs. Aral lowlands vs. Tian Shan foothills); agriculture-specific forecasts for irrigation scheduling; extreme event early warning (heatwaves, frost, storms) with automated sectoral alerts to agriculture, energy, and emergency services — this is a named decree deliverable creating a guaranteed first contract

## Drivers

- УП-189: Weather forecasting AI as specific deliverable with Mar 1 2026 deadline
- UZ meteorological network Uzhydromet covers 35M people + agriculture/energy sectors
- Climate extremes increasing — accurate weather AI prevents agricultural losses and disaster preparation
- ПП-320 grants available for this specific project
- ECMWF and WMO technical assistance programs for Central Asian NMHSs
