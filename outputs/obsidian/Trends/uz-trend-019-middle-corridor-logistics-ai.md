# Middle Corridor logistics AI for Uzbekistan's position as Central Asian transit hub (Trans-Caspian International Route)

> [!important] Killer app
> Middle Corridor logistics intelligence platform: AI-powered customs risk profiling at border crossings (UZ-KZ, UZ-TM, UZ-AF), predictive transit time estimation for shippers, capacity optimization for UTY freight routing, and real-time exception management — directly supporting UZ ambition to become regional logistics hub and reducing transit friction cost estimated at $2.3B annually [Source: World Bank Trade Logistics Assessment CA 2023]

## Drivers

- Middle Corridor volumes grew 400% 2021-2024 as Russia-Ukraine war rerouted China-Europe cargo
- UZ is central transit country — Tashkent railway hub processing 25M tons annually
- JICA smart logistics program in Uzbekistan (transport digitalization priority)
- EU Team Europe Connectivity4CentralAsia (C4CA) €100M program includes transport digitalization
- Digital Uzbekistan-2030 transport sector target
