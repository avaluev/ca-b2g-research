# Telemedicine AI for Kyrgyzstan mountain communities: AKDN/SDC-funded rural health connectivity

> [!important] Killer app
> Mountain telemedicine network: AI triage app for community health workers in 1,500+ remote Kyrgyz villages; satellite-enabled video consultation connecting village workers to district hospital specialists; maternal health monitoring for high-altitude pregnancies; integrated supply chain alert for medicine stockouts in mountain pharmacies — addressing the 2x higher maternal mortality rate in mountain vs. urban KG [Source: WHO Kyrgyzstan health assessment 2023]

## Drivers

- 70%+ of KG population in rural/mountain areas with <1 specialist per 10,000 population
- AKDN (Aga Khan Foundation) active in mountain health since 1990s — direct procurement pathway
- SDC (Swiss Cooperation) KG sustainable mountain development program includes health digital
- Starlink satellite connectivity expanding in mountain regions — enables telemedicine where fiber impossible
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: rural digital inclusion as priority
