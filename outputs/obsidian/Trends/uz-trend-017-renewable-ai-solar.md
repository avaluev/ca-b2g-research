# AI-optimized renewable energy forecasting for Uzbekistan solar and hydropower grid integration

> [!important] Killer app
> Renewable integration AI: combines satellite-based solar irradiance forecasting with hydropower reservoir level monitoring and demand forecasting to optimize the day-ahead energy mix — enabling Uzbekistan to maximize use of 8GW solar capacity without costly backup reserves, potentially avoiding $120M+ in annual peaking plant operating costs [Source: IRENA renewable integration cost estimates 2024]

## Drivers

- UZ government target: 8GW solar by 2026 under Presidential climate commitments
- Solar variability requires AI demand and supply forecasting for grid stability
- EBRD and ADB green energy lending programs active in Uzbekistan 2025-2027
- UN Climate commitments — UNFCCC NDC implementation requires grid AI
- ПП-320 AI grants applicable to renewable energy optimization
