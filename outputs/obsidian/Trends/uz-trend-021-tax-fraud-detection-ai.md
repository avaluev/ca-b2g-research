# AI tax audit selection and e-invoice fraud detection for State Tax Committee (ГНК) Uzbekistan

> [!important] Killer app
> AI tax analytics platform for GNK: graph-based VAT carousel fraud detection processing all e-invoice transactions in real-time; ML risk-scoring of all registered taxpayers for targeted audit selection (replacing random selection); automated unreported income detection by cross-referencing asset registries with declared income — estimated 15-20% increase in tax collection (~$400M annually) based on Estonia/Georgia tax AI deployments [Source: IMF Fiscal TA Central Asia 2024]

## Drivers

- ПП-358 AI Strategy 2030: 'shadow economy reduction via tax AI' as explicit goal
- УП-189: AI for banking/finance fraud and tax listed as priority domains
- GNK e-invoicing system covers 200K+ registered companies — big data for fraud detection
- UZ shadow economy estimated 40-50% of GDP [Source: IMF 2023] — government urgency high
- ПП-320: AI grant funding for tax administration modernization
