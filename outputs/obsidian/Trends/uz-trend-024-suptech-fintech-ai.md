# AI-powered SupTech/RegTech for Central Bank of Uzbekistan financial supervision and reporting automation

> [!important] Killer app
> Supervisory technology platform for CBU: automated collection and validation of regulatory reporting from 30+ licensed banks; ML-based early warning system detecting bank distress signals 6-9 months ahead; integrated stress-testing module for quarterly assessments; AML/CFT supervision analytics — enabling CBU to modernize from sample-based to real-time risk-based supervision as fintech sector scales past $2B in annual transaction volume

## Drivers

- CBU regulatory modernization with IFC technical assistance
- Electronic commerce law (ЗРУ-792) and digital signature law (ЗРУ-793) expanding regulated digital financial services
- Fintech licensing regime under development — CBU needs AI supervision tools
- Western SupTech vendors (FICO, Moody's Analytics) reducing CIS engagement
- ПП-358: fintech regulatory sandbox and AI in banking finance as strategic direction
