# Satellite imagery AI for precision agriculture: cotton yield forecasting, water allocation, crop monitoring under ПП-257

> [!important] Killer app
> Satellite-based precision agriculture platform: weekly field-level crop health maps covering all 3.8M hectares of agricultural land in Uzbekistan, automated cotton/wheat/vegetable yield forecasting for regional procurement planning, water stress detection triggering canal irrigation recommendations, and mobile alert system for farm managers — reducing water waste by 20-25% and improving yields 10-15% [Source: FAO precision agriculture Central Asia study 2023]

## Drivers

- ПП-257 (Aug 2023): Agroplatforma unified digital agriculture platform, satellite remote sensing integration
- УП-155 (Oct 2024): Aerospace technologies roadmap 2024-2026, satellite imagery for agriculture and disaster monitoring
- Cotton remains ~15% of Uzbek exports — $1.5B sector needing AI optimization [Source: UN Comtrade 2024]
- World Bank water management program in Fergana Valley
- Aral Basin crisis requires precision water-use AI — UNDP/ADB co-financing active
- ПП-320 grant funding for AgriTech AI projects
