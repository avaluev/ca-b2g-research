# AI-powered air quality monitoring and prediction network for Tashkent metropolitan area

> [!important] Killer app
> Urban air quality intelligence platform for Tashkent: dense IoT sensor network (PM1, PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3, CO) with ML forecasting predicting daily air quality zones 24 hours ahead; automated regulatory alerts to industrial emitters above threshold; public-facing mobile app with Uzbek-language health advisory; school closure recommendation AI — addressing one of Central Asia's most visible public health crises and potentially saving 2,000+ lives annually [Source: WHO air pollution mortality estimates, Tashkent 2023]

## Drivers

- Tashkent winter thermal inversion creates PM2.5 levels exceeding WHO standards 5-10x [Source: IQAir World Air Quality Report 2024]
- UNDP and USAID successor environmental programs in Uzbekistan
- Climate adaptation mandate from Paris Agreement NDC
- Tashkent population 3M+ — public health crisis with political pressure
- ПП-358 AI: environmental monitoring as priority AI application
