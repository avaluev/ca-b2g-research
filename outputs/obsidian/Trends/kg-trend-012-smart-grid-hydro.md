# AI grid optimization for Kyrgyzstan hydropower-dominated energy system (Toktogul-centered grid)

> [!important] Killer app
> Kyrgyzstan hydropower optimization AI: ML model predicting seasonal energy balance 3-6 months ahead based on glacier melt forecasts, precipitation patterns, and reservoir levels; automated dispatch optimization across 6 major hydro cascade stations; CASA-1000 export scheduling maximizing revenue from 1,300MW export corridor to South Asia — potentially adding $50-80M annually to KG's energy export revenue [Source: EBRD energy sector assessment KG 2023]

## Drivers

- KG electricity: 90%+ hydropower dependent — extreme seasonal variability
- Winter energy deficit recurring: KG imports 1.5-2 TWh annually from Kazakh/Tajik/Russian grids
- EBRD and ADB energy lending programs active in KG grid modernization
- CASA-1000 project (KG-TJ hydro export to Pakistan/Afghanistan) requires better grid management
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028 includes energy sector digitalization
