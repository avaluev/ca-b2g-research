# AI-assisted desertification and Aral Sea basin soil monitoring for Karakalpakstan and Khorezm regions

> [!important] Killer app
> Aral Basin degradation monitoring AI: satellite-based tracking of salt crust formation and dust storm source zones; vegetation monitoring for saxaul afforestation programs covering 1.5M+ hectares; automated reporting for MFSA donor coordination; groundwater depletion monitoring — supporting the $600M international environmental program in one of the world's largest ongoing ecological disasters

## Drivers

- Aral Sea crisis: 2M hectares of dry seabed generating toxic dust storms affecting 3M+ residents
- UN Special Trust Fund for Aral Sea Region (MFSA) active program
- World Bank Aral Sea regional environmental program
- UNDP climate adaptation program in Uzbekistan
- UZ International Fund for Aral Sea (IFAS) chairmanship 2024-2026 creates political window
