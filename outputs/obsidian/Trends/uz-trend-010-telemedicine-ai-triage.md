# Telemedicine platform with AI-assisted triage for rural Uzbekistan under new healthcare model

> [!important] Killer app
> Hybrid telemedicine system with AI triage: patient initiates consultation via mobile app or EPIGU; AI symptom checker assesses urgency and directs to appropriate care level (self-care, GP teleconsult, emergency referral); connects to specialist telemedicine session with automatic translation between Uzbek dialects and medical Russian — prioritizing oncology, obstetrics, and cardiology where rural specialist gaps are most deadly

## Drivers

- УП-88 (May 2025): New healthcare model nationwide rollout including remote areas
- ПП-38 (Jan 2024): Electronic referral systems and telemedicine infrastructure
- Rural Uzbekistan has specialist shortage — AI triage is cost-efficient substitution
- UNICEF digital health program in Uzbekistan (donor co-financing)
- COVID-era telemedicine infrastructure now available for permanent service
