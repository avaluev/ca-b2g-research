# Geospatial NSDI AI-powered imagery analysis and digital cadastre modernization ($35M World Bank, 2025-2030)

> [!important] Killer app
> NSDI analytics layer combining: automated land use change detection (identifies illegal construction, agricultural land conversion, irrigation encroachment) from satellite imagery; AI-powered property valuation model for tax assessment; 3D urban model generation for Tashkent and regional centers — directly supporting $35M World Bank investment and providing defensible cadastre AI moat for 5+ years

## Drivers

- УП-155 (Oct 2024): Agency for Space Research and Technologies created under MoDT; satellite imagery for urban planning and agriculture
- World Bank Geospatial Data Project $35M 2025-2030 (Cadastre Agency as implementing body)
- ПП-258 (2023): Geospatial infrastructure digitalization
- Land registry fraud reduction is Presidential priority
- Digital Uzbekistan-2030 requires NSDI as digital infrastructure backbone
