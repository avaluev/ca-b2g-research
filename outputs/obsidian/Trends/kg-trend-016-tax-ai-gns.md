# AI tax fraud detection and e-filing analytics for Kyrgyzstan State Tax Service

> [!important] Killer app
> Tax analytics platform for GNS: ML risk scoring of 200K+ KG registered businesses for audit prioritization; cross-sector income reconciliation using Tunduk data integration (business registry, property, vehicles); real-time VAT refund fraud detection; automated compliance nudge system reducing filing errors — estimated 10-12% increase in tax collection adding $60-80M annually to KG fiscal revenues [Source: IMF fiscal capacity assessment KG 2024]

## Drivers

- KG tax gap estimated at 30-35% of potential collection [Source: IMF KG Article IV 2024]
- GNS digitalization program with ADB technical assistance
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: tax system digitalization
- e-filing system operational — creates data foundation for ML analytics
- IMF TA program supporting revenue mobilization in KG
