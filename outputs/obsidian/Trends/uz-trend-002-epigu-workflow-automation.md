# AI-driven workflow automation for inter-agency document routing via UZINFOCOM integration layer

> [!important] Killer app
> Intelligent document processing engine that auto-classifies incoming citizen applications, extracts required data fields, routes to responsible agency sub-system via UZINFOCOM, and generates decision drafts — cutting internal government processing time from 10+ days to 48 hours for top-30 service types

## Drivers

- ПП-162 (May 2023) target: 70% government services electronic by end-2025
- ПП-286 (Sep 2025): 41 priority digital projects 2025-2026, mahalla-level service delivery
- UZINFOCOM designated as single integrator for multiple state systems
- Up to $50M in ПП-320 grants for AI in state bodies — automation is highest ROI use case
