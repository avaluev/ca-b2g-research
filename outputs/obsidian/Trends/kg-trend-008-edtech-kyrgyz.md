# AI-powered Kyrgyz-language educational platform for school curriculum: KOICA and UNICEF co-funded

> [!important] Killer app
> Kyrgyz-language adaptive learning platform: personalized curriculum alignment with KG national standards; AI tutor for math and Kyrgyz language (most critical learning gaps per PISA data); offline tablet app for 800+ rural schools without reliable internet; teacher analytics dashboard — addressing KG PISA scores 22% below OECD average, particularly in rural areas where teacher shortage is severe [Source: OECD PISA 2022 central Asia supplemental data]

## Drivers

- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: digital education as priority
- KOICA KG digital governance multi-phase program includes education component
- UNICEF 'Adolescent Skills Digital' program in KG
- KG has 2,200+ schools; rural schools have 1 teacher covering multiple grades — AI tutoring fills gap
- Kyrgyz language instruction is mandatory — Kyrgyz-language AI models rare globally
