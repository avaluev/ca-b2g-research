# High-Tech Park Kyrgyzstan AI talent pipeline and startup incubation for domestic B2G market

> [!important] Killer app
> HTP-anchored B2G AI factory: incubation program for 10-15 AI startups building specifically for KG government procurement (Digital Code compliance tools, Tunduk integration modules, municipal analytics) — operating under HTP tax regime, with Digital Infrastructure Development Fund as anchor investor, and UDP Digital Dept as first customer pipeline. Replicates the IT Park Uzbekistan model in a smaller-scale KG context

## Drivers

- HTP KG operational with tax regime for IT companies (0% income/corporate tax)
- Digital Code 2025: AI company registration and compliance creates HTP anchor demand
- Digital Infrastructure Development Fund (created Apr 2026) provides capital
- KG diaspora in Russia, Turkey, UAE: post-2022 returnees enriching domestic talent pool
- National AI Council (КГ-УП-2025-091) supports HTP expansion
