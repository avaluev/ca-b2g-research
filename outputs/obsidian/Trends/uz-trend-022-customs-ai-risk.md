# AI customs risk profiling for State Customs Committee (ГТК) Uzbekistan — valuation and contraband detection

> [!important] Killer app
> Customs risk profiling AI: ML model trained on historical GTC data flagging consignments for physical inspection based on 50+ risk factors (shipper history, route anomaly, HS code mismatch, declared value vs. reference price); X-ray image analysis AI reviewing scanner outputs for contraband detection — estimated 40% improvement in interdiction rate while reducing legitimate trade clearance time from 2.4 to 0.8 hours average [Source: World Bank Trade Facilitation Report 2023]

## Drivers

- ПП-358 AI Strategy: customs as explicit AI implementation domain
- Middle Corridor growth — Uzbekistan customs processing $18B+ annual trade volume [Source: UN Comtrade 2024]
- GTC computerization program with World Bank TA
- EU C4CA connectivity program includes customs facilitation component
- Chinese vendor Huawei SafeGate competing in region — Russian CIS substitution opportunity
