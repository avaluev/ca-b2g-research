# AI glacier monitoring and water flow prediction for Kyrgyzstan's Naryn River basin (hydropower critical)

> [!important] Killer app
> Glacier and water intelligence platform for KG: satellite-based glacier mass balance monitoring for 7,000+ glaciers; ML seasonal inflow forecasting for Naryn River feeding Toktogul reservoir (60-90 day ahead); automated drought/flood risk alerts for irrigation systems downstream; climate adaptation planning tool for 2030-2050 water availability scenarios — protecting $800M+ annual hydropower revenue from climate-driven variability [Source: KG Ministry of Energy Annual Report 2024]

## Drivers

- KG glaciers are retreating 0.3-0.7km annually — directly threatening Naryn River flow [Source: UN Environment Programme 2024]
- Toktogul reservoir (1,900MW) generates 40% of KG electricity — depends on Naryn River inflow
- ADB climate adaptation program in KG
- UNDP climate finance program for glacier monitoring
- CRETOC (Central Asian regional climate tech) research consortium interested in AI applications
- SDC sustainable mountain development includes glacier risk monitoring
