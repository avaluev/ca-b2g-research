# AI air quality monitoring and prediction for Bishkek winter inversion episodes (ranked #1 most polluted city globally multiple days 2023-2024)

> [!important] Killer app
> Bishkek air quality intelligence network: dense IoT sensor grid (PM2.5/PM10/NOx/CO) with ML prediction of winter inversion events 48 hours ahead; public mobile app with health advisory in Kyrgyz/Russian; automated alerts to schools and hospitals; source attribution model identifying highest-impact heating stations for regulatory action — directly addressing Bishkek's public health crisis with documented 1,500+ annual excess deaths from air pollution [Source: WHO Global Health Observatory 2024]

## Drivers

- Bishkek ranked #1 most polluted city globally multiple days (IQAir data 2023-2024) [Source: IQAir World Air Quality Report 2024]
- Winter heating season: coal burning causes PM2.5 levels 50-100x WHO safe limits
- SDC (Switzerland) active environmental program in Bishkek
- UNDP climate adaptation in KG includes urban environment component
- Bishkek mayor facing severe public pressure on air quality — political motivation high
- OSF (Open Society) civic tech programs — public air quality data as civic infrastructure
