# AI-powered personalized learning and tutoring for school curriculum in Uzbek language (2026/2027 rollout under УП-189)

> [!important] Killer app
> Adaptive AI tutoring platform for Uzbek school curriculum: tracks individual student mastery per topic, automatically adjusts difficulty and generates personalized exercises in Uzbek/Russian, flags students at dropout risk to teachers, and provides automated formative assessment — targeting the 65%+ of schools outside Tashkent where qualified STEM teacher shortage is severe [Source: UNESCO Education in Central Asia 2023 assessment]

## Drivers

- УП-189 (Oct 2025): AI education in school curricula from academic year 2026/2027
- 15 AI laboratories in higher education institutions by end-2026
- ПП-51 (Feb 2024): One Million Programmers program and digital education subsidies
- ПП-320: grants available for AI in education sector
- KOICA $14M IT education ecosystem program (co-financing)
- Korea partnership via KOICA directly relevant to curriculum AI
