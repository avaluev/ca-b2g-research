# AI-assisted clinical decision support and diagnostic imaging for Electronic Healthcare platform (ПП-415 implementation)

> [!important] Killer app
> Clinical decision support system integrated with the Electronic Healthcare platform: AI triage at primary care level predicts disease severity and appropriate referral pathway; radiology AI (TB detection, cancer screening) deployed at district hospital level where specialist shortage is acute; medication interaction checker integrated with e-prescription module — collectively reducing diagnostic errors by 25%+ [Source: WHO Central Asia health tech deployment benchmarks, 2024]

## Drivers

- ПП-415 (Dec 2023): Electronic Healthcare platform under UZINFOCOM, implementation deadline 2026
- УП-88 (May 2025): New nationwide healthcare model pilot Jul 2025; full rollout 2026
- ПП-311 (Sep 2024): State medical insurance digital claims processing — fraud detection imperative
- ПП-185 (May 2025): Population registration in Electronic Healthcare by end-2025
- UNICEF and World Bank co-financing confirmed in ПП-415
