# AI for MChS emergency services: earthquake early warning, flood prediction, dispatch optimization for Fergana Valley

> [!important] Killer app
> Integrated disaster early warning and response platform for Uzbekistan: ML-based seismic P-wave detection triggering 8-15 second earthquake early warning for Tashkent and Fergana Valley populations; satellite-based flood inundation modeling for 72-hour advance warning; AI dispatch optimization routing emergency responders 40% faster — protecting 35M population in one of the world's most seismically active regions

## Drivers

- Uzbekistan in high-seismic zone (Fergana Valley, Tashkent) — 1966 Tashkent earthquake precedent
- ПП-358 AI Strategy lists emergency/disaster management as AI implementation domain
- UNDP disaster risk reduction program in Uzbekistan with digital component
- Climate change increasing Fergana Valley flood frequency — AI early warning critical
- МЧС (Ministry of Emergency Situations) digitalization under Digital Uzbekistan-2030
