# AI in Uzbekistan courts: legal analysis, judicial act generation, virtual legal consultant (УП-140 Program 2025-2027)

> [!important] Killer app
> AI judiciary platform with three tiers: (1) case auto-classification and assignment routing; (2) judicial act drafting assistant generating structured court decisions from case facts in Uzbek/Russian; (3) citizen-facing legal consultant chatbot answering procedural questions and document preparation — reducing court backlog by 30%+ based on Kazakhstan's e-court experience

- УП-140 (Aug 2025): Comprehensive program — Digital Court prototype by end-2025; legal analysis platform Dec 1 2025; judicial act generation software end-2025; virtual legal consultant Jan 1 2026; nationwide rollout 2026-2027
- UZINFOCOM designated single technology integrator for judiciary AI
- Supreme Court Digital Technologies and AI Department newly created
- ПП-320 AI grant funding available for judicial sector
