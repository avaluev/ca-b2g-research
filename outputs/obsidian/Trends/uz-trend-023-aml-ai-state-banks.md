# AI-powered anti-money laundering and FinCEN compliance for Uzbekistan state-owned banks post-sanctions environment

> [!important] Killer app
> AI-native AML platform for Uzbekistan's 6 state-owned banks (collectively holding 80%+ of banking assets): real-time transaction monitoring with ML-based suspicious activity detection; customer risk scoring using alternative data sources; automated SAR generation in CBU-compliant format; network analytics identifying money laundering rings — enabling banks to maintain SWIFT access and expand correspondent banking relationships with regional and Islamic banks

## Drivers

- UZ state banks (NBU, Asaka, Ipoteka, Agrobank) must maintain correspondent banking access
- FATF grey-listing risk for CA jurisdictions without robust AML — direct regulatory pressure
- ПП-358 AI Strategy: banking/finance fraud prevention explicitly listed
- Western AML vendors (NICE Actimize, Oracle Mantas) deprioritizing CIS market post-2022
- CBU (Central Bank UZ) regulatory modernization program
