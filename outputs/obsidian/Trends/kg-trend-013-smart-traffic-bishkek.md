# AI smart traffic management for Bishkek metropolitan area (900K population, rapid urbanization)

> [!important] Killer app
> Bishkek smart traffic platform: adaptive signal control at 200+ major intersections reducing average travel time 18-22%; AI accident detection triggering emergency response alerts; commercial vehicle routing enforcement protecting residential streets; Bishkek-specific congestion pricing pilot — with potential expansion to Osh (second-largest city) under ADB regional urban program, creating a replicable inter-city smart mobility solution

## Drivers

- Bishkek traffic accidents: 8,000+ annually with 300+ fatalities [Source: Ministry of Internal Affairs KG 2024]
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: smart city pilots for Bishkek
- ADB urban development program in Bishkek includes transport component
- Bishkek mayor's office active on smart city agenda
- Korean KOICA smart city reference (Mongolia, Central Asia experience)
