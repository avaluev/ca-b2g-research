# AI early warning for landslides, mudflows and earthquakes in Kyrgyzstan mountain regions

> [!important] Killer app
> Mountain hazard early warning platform: satellite SAR-based landslide precursor detection with 72-hour warning for 500+ priority hazard zones; glacial lake outburst flood sensor network covering 20 highest-risk GLOF lakes; automated community alert via USSD/SMS reaching 200K+ people in hazard zones without internet; real-time damage assessment AI from satellite imagery post-event — protecting the 1.2M people living in KG high-hazard zones from increasingly frequent climate-amplified disasters

## Drivers

- KG: 2,000+ registered landslide hazard zones; $100M+ annual damage [Source: UNDP Kyrgyzstan DRR report 2023]
- UNDP disaster risk reduction program in KG
- SDC sustainable mountain development includes disaster risk component
- ADB climate and disaster risk reduction lending program in KG
- Climate change accelerating glacial lake outburst flood (GLOF) frequency — AI early warning critical
