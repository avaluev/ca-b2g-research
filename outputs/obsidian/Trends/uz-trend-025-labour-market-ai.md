# AI-powered labor market matching and migrant reintegration platform ('Mahalliy ish') under Ministry of Employment UZ

> [!important] Killer app
> AI skills-matching platform covering the full migration cycle: foreign job opportunity matching for intending migrants (Korea, Germany, UAE destination focus); digital skills portfolio for returning migrants translating foreign work experience to Uzbek labor market equivalents; domestic SME-to-worker matching for returnees — serving 500K+ annual migration cycle with estimated 30% improvement in wage outcomes for matched workers [Source: ILO return migration impact study CA 2023]

## Drivers

- 3.5M+ Uzbek labor migrants abroad (primarily Russia, Korea, UAE, Turkey) [Source: ILO 2024 labor migration statistics]
- Presidential priority: returning migrant reintegration and domestic employment
- KOICA 'Enhancing Youth Employment' $14M program includes labor market digital tools
- ПП-320 AI grants for employment sector AI
- ПП-358 AI Strategy: labor market as AI implementation domain
