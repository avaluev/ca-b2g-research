# MyID Mobile-ID digital passport rollout and biometric e-signature for government services

> [!important] Killer app
> Mobile-ID module extending existing ID-card infrastructure to smartphones — enabling full legal-grade digital signature from any mobile device for the 36M+ adult population, replacing notarized paper processes across 720+ government services currently on EPIGU

## Drivers

- УП-6065 (Sep 2020): mandatory biometric ID by Jan 2023, old documents invalid from Jan 2031
- ЗРУ-793 (Oct 2022): new electronic digital signature law — legally valid across government
- ПП-162 (May 2023): digital services tied to MyID authentication
- UZINFOCOM as MyID technical operator
