# Uzbek/Russian speech recognition and NLP for government document transcription and citizen services

> [!important] Killer app
> Uzbek-Russian multilingual ASR and NLP platform: accurate speech-to-text for court hearings (УП-140 mandate), citizen service center audio routing, and parliamentary session transcription; coupled with NLP entity extraction for key legal terms — enabling automated court record creation and reducing court clerk workload by 60%+, directly serving the 2026-2027 Digital Court rollout

## Drivers

- УП-140 judicial AI: court hearing transcription is first concrete use case
- УП-189: language corpus development mandate includes speech data
- ПП-320 grants for language AI projects
- Uzbek has 3 scripts (Cyrillic, Latin, Arabic) creating complexity that Western ASR ignores
- Regional context: Uzbek diaspora at Apple/Google/Meta rare — no mainstream vendor prioritizes Uzbek ASR
