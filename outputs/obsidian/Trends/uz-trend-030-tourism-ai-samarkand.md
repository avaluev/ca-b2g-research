# AI-powered multilingual tourism guide and cultural heritage digitization for UNESCO World Heritage sites in Uzbekistan

> [!important] Killer app
> AI heritage tourism platform: augmented reality mobile guide for Registan, Shah-i-Zinda, Gur-e-Amir providing historically accurate multilingual narration in 10 languages; AI restoration visualization showing monuments in their historical state; personalized itinerary generator cross-referencing visitor interests with Silk Road sites; real-time crowd management for popular sites — supporting UZ ambition to reach 10M annual tourists by 2030 [Source: UZ Tourism Development Strategy 2023]

## Drivers

- UZ tourism growing 35% 2019-2024 — Samarkand, Bukhara, Khiva now 'top-10 rising destinations' [Source: UNWTO 2024]
- Samarkand Expo 2025 and SCO Summit mandate tourism digital infrastructure
- ПП-320 AI grants applicable to tourism sector
- UNESCO digital heritage preservation programs in UZ
- Post-Expo 2025 Samarkand government commitment to digital tourism
