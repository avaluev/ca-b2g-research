# AI governance and compliance certification framework under ЗРУ-1115 (Uzbekistan's first AI law, Jan 2026)

> [!important] Killer app
> AI compliance certification suite for the 100+ government AI projects under ПП-320: automated model card generation in ЗРУ-1115-compliant format; bias testing toolkit for Uzbek demographic categories; explainability report generator for regulatory submissions; ongoing monitoring dashboard — acting as the gating mechanism for AI grant disbursement and creating recurring advisory revenue stream

- International AI governance frameworks (EU AI Act awareness) influencing UZ approach
