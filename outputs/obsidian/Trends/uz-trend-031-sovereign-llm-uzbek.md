# Uzbek-language sovereign LLM for government AI applications: legal, medical, administrative domains

> [!important] Killer app
> Uzbek-language foundation model (UzLLM): trained on 50B+ Uzbek/Russian tokens from government documents, Uzbek literature, legal corpus, and medical knowledge bases; deployable at UZ AI Cluster; API gateway for all 100+ AI projects mandated under УП-189; domain fine-tuning for judiciary (УП-140), healthcare (ПП-415), and citizen services (ПП-286) — creating the shared AI infrastructure layer that makes all other government AI applications feasible without depending on foreign cloud providers

## Drivers

- УП-189 (Oct 2025): language corpus development by end-2026 explicitly mandated
- AI Cluster New Uzbekistan University (Mar 1 2026) requires sovereign compute and Uzbek models
- ЗРУ-1115 (Jan 2026) AI law — legally significant decisions require explainable AI; Uzbek-language explainability requires Uzbek models
- Data localization law (ЗРУ-547/1125) — cloud LLM APIs from OpenAI/Google violate data localization for state data
- Alliance for AI Development under УП-189 — direct vehicle for sovereign LLM co-investment
