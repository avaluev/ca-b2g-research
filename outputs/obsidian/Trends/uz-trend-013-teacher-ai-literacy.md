# AI literacy and digital competency training program for 500,000+ Uzbekistan schoolteachers

> [!important] Killer app
> National AI teacher training program: online course platform with proctored certification covering AI fundamentals, classroom AI tool use (grading assist, differentiated learning), and AI ethics discussion facilitation — targeting all 500K+ teachers ahead of 2026/2027 curriculum rollout, with regional training hubs in 14 regions of Uzbekistan

## Drivers

- УП-189: AI in school curricula from 2026/2027 — teachers must be trained first
- ПП-51 (Feb 2024): qualified specialists training support including non-state organizations
- ПП-320 training component under Alliance for AI Development
- KOICA partnership includes teacher training component
- UNESCO ICT in Education benchmarks: UZ ranks 56th — government target to reach top-30
