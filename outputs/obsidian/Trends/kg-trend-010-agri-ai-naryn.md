# AI precision agriculture for Kyrgyzstan livestock and smallholder farming (Naryn, Issyk-Kul, Osh regions)

> [!important] Killer app
> AI precision agriculture platform for KG smallholders: satellite-based pasture health monitoring tracking 1.8M+ cattle movement across mountain pastures; yield forecasting for wheat/potato/corn in Chui and Osh valleys; automated agricultural insurance claims processing using satellite damage assessment; digital land registry integration for loan collateral verification via Tunduk — potentially increasing farmer income by 15-20% through better crop and livestock management [Source: BMGF Kyrgyzstan agricultural productivity study 2023]

## Drivers

- Agriculture: 30% of KG GDP, 40% of employment [Source: World Bank KG Country Overview 2024]
- BMGF agricultural insurance digitalization program in KG
- World Bank CASA-1000 and irrigation management programs include digital monitoring
- ADB rural development programs in KG with digital agriculture component
- SDC mountain agriculture development — digital tools for pastoralist tracking
- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: agriculture digitalization as priority
