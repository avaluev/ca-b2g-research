# AI digital social registry and targeted assistance platform for Kyrgyzstan rural municipalities (Айыл аймагы)

> [!important] Killer app
> AI social targeting platform for Айыл аймагы: automated means-testing AI cross-referencing employment, asset ownership, utility data, and family composition via Tunduk; mobile case management for rural social workers; payment integration with NBKR digital som infrastructure (trend 014) enabling direct digital benefit transfers; AI fraud detection for duplicate benefit claims — increasing social assistance coverage accuracy from 40% to 70%+ for the 1.5M Kyrgyzstanis below the poverty line [Source: World Bank KG poverty data 2023]

## Drivers

- KG Digital Transformation Concept 2024-2028: local governance digitalization
- World Bank Social Protection Digital project in KG
- SDC local governance digital support program
- KG has 480 Айыл аймагы (rural municipalities) — digital registry cuts administrative duplication
- Social assistance targeting: only 40% of intended KG beneficiaries receive full entitlements [Source: World Bank KG Social Protection Assessment 2023]
