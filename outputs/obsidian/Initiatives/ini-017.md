# AI Civil Servant Training Program (UZ)

_Deliver Russian-Uzbek bilingual AI literacy + applied training for civil servants under UP-2024-095 + live UZ-T-2026-008 ($1.8M)._

## Problem

UZ-UP-2024-095 mandates new civil-service approach with digital competency. Live tender UZ-T-2026-008 ($1.8M, civil-servant AI training) is open. Saudi SDAIA national capability program is anchor; KZ AI strategy 2024 has parallel program. Diaspora-bridge mentorship is key differentiator.

## Solution concept

Civil-servant AI training: (1) tiered modular curriculum (executive, analyst, frontline) in Russian-Uzbek; (2) hands-on labs using sovereign LLM (cross-link INI-006); (3) diaspora mentor seminar series. Architectural anchor: SDAIA capability + KZ AI strategy training. UZ adaptation: bilingual + diaspora-bridge mentor layer.
