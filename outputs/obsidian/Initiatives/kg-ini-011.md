# KG Cybersecurity SOC & Critical Infrastructure

_Modernize KG national SOC and critical-infrastructure protection under live RFP KG-T-2026-010 ($900K) and Cybersecurity Strategy framework._

## Problem

KG-LAW-2024-121-CYBERSEC (Cybersecurity Law) and KG-CABINET-2024-746-CYBERSEC (critical information infrastructure requirements) and KG-CABINET-2025-CYBERSEC-STRATEGY mandate national SOC and CIIP. Live RFP KG-T-2026-010 ($900K, cybersecurity audit and strategy for state bodies) is open. Estonia National Cyber Defence Centre (case-est-cyber-defense) is the global reference. Tunduk modernization (linked KG-INI-003) creates new attack surface. Digital som CBDC (linked KG-INI-002) makes financial system high-value target. SOC modernization is foundational for the entire UDP digital strategy.

## Solution concept

Three-component delivery: (1) SOC modernization — SIEM upgrade with AI-assisted threat detection (anomaly detection, beaconing detection, lateral movement), Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual operator console; (2) critical infrastructure protection — vulnerability assessment and managed-detection-response for top 20 critical-infrastructure operators (NBKR, Tunduk, Kyrgyztelekom, Toktogul hydropower, MoH, Cabinet); (3) incident response playbook + cybersecurity strategy update aligning with KG-CABINET-2025-CYBERSEC-STRATEGY refresh. Architectural anchor: Estonia NCSC + CERT-EE + 24/7 SOC operational pattern. Adapted for KG centralized governance, Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual UX, Tunduk inference fabric integration.
