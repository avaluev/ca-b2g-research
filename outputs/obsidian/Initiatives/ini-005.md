# AI Customs Risk-Scoring (SafeGate UZ)

_Deploy AI risk-scoring for customs declarations at SCC, replicating Kazakhstan eGümrük (case-kz-egumen-border) plus India ICEGATE patterns._

## Problem

Uzbekistan's customs leakage and inefficiency is estimated at 15-25% of formal import duties, with manual risk-flagging unable to keep pace with Belt & Road traffic growth. Forthcoming RFP UZ-TF-2026-010 ($4.2M, AI customs risk-scoring SafeGate) is in pipeline for late 2026. Kazakhstan eGümrük is direct architectural precedent.

## Solution concept

AI customs risk-scoring stack: (1) declaration anomaly detection (HS code mismatch, valuation outliers, broker-pattern flags); (2) container/truck routing risk score (origin × commodity × broker × historical pattern); (3) inspector-facing dashboard with explainability. Architectural anchor: Kazakhstan eGümrük preserved as core. UZ adaptation: SCC's existing customs declaration system integration, Russian-Uzbek bilingual inspector UX, UzCloud deployment.
