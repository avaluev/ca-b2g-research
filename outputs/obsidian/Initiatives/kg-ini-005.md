# ADB e-Procurement Phase 2 AI Analytics

_Deliver AI-powered fraud detection, supplier scoring and analytics for KG's State Procurement Agency under the ADB Phase 2 successor to Project 55109-001 (Aibek Abdybakirov TTL)._

## Problem

ADB-KG-55109-001 ($650K Phase 1) has completed initial e-Procurement digitalization. Phase 2 will procure AI analytics, fraud detection, and supplier scoring on top of the digitalized procurement platform. Live RFP KG-T-2026-002 ($150K, IT audit) is the precursor. Forthcoming Phase 2 award expected mid-2026. KG-LAW-2022-027 (new procurement law) and KG-LAW-2025-243 / KG-LAW-2025-262 (legislative amendments) create the regulatory frame for AI-assisted procurement decision support. Kazakhstan Smart Bridge tax integration (case-kz-smartbridge-tax) and India e-procurement reference patterns inform the architecture.

## Solution concept

Three-component AI overlay on the digitalized e-procurement platform: (1) supplier risk scoring — ML model rating each registered supplier on prior contract performance, beneficial-ownership red flags, and price-anomaly history; (2) bid anomaly detection — clustering algorithms flagging suspicious price patterns, shell-company linkages, and bid-rotation rings; (3) procurement decision support dashboard for the State Procurement Agency leadership in Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual. Architectural anchor: Kazakhstan SmartBridge tax-procurement integration and Korea KONEPS analytics overlay. Adapted for KG's central procurement model (KG-PROCUREMENT-DEPT under Cabinet) and integration with Tunduk for beneficial-ownership cross-reference.

## Risk register

- **commercial**: Phase 1 vendor extends scope into analytics rather than tendering Phase 2 separately. — _Mitigation_: Engage ADB TTL early to confirm tendering of Phase 2 as separate procurement; lobby for ICB modality which favors specialized AI analytics vendors over digitalization generalists.
- **political**: Procurement reform is politically sensitive — anti-corruption flag-raising can generate blowback against the procurement agency leadership. — _Mitigation_: Frame system as decision-support not auto-decision; preserve human-in-the-loop for all enforcement actions. Anchor relationship at the procurement department director level so political cover is institutional.
- **technical**: KG procurement data quality is uneven; supplier registration records have gaps. — _Mitigation_: Phase 1 of pilot scoped to Q4 2025-forward records where digitalization is complete. Build data-quality scoring as part of supplier risk score so gaps are visible not silent.
