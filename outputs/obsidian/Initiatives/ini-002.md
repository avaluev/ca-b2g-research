# AI in Courts Phase 1 (UP-140)

_Land Phase 1 implementation of AI-assisted civil-court document analysis and case-load triage under UP-140's Cifrli Adliya program._

## Problem

UP-140 (May 2025) commits the Supreme Court to deploy AI in civil-court document analysis, case routing, and judicial-decision support by Q4 2027, with $15M earmarked. The pilot RFP (UZ-T-2026-002, $8M, live) is open and will award in Q3 2026. No competing vendor has demonstrated Uzbek-Cyrillic + Russian dual-script legal NLP at production scale; Estonia's SALME (case-est-salme) and Kazakhstan e-Court (case-kz-ecourt) provide proven blueprints.

## Solution concept

Deliver a hybrid AI judicial-support stack: (1) Uzbek+Russian bilingual legal-NLP (case classification, statute citation extraction, decision-template suggestion) trained on the Supreme Court's existing case archive; (2) judge-facing workflow that drafts case summaries and flags inconsistencies in pleadings; (3) citizen-facing legal aid chatbot routing (links to UNDP-UZ-AI-COURTS donor program). Architecturally adapted from Estonia's SALME (semantic indexing of judicial decisions) preserved as core, but adapted for Uzbek-Cyrillic + Latin + Russian and Supreme Court appellate procedure. Sovereign data residency enforced through UzCloud deployment.
