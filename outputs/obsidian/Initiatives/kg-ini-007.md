# Glacier & GLOF Early Warning AI (Deltares-Style)

_Deliver AI-powered glacier monitoring and Glacial Lake Outburst Flood early-warning system for Ministry of Energy / Emergency Service under forthcoming KG-TF-2026-008 ($2.8M), replicating Netherlands Deltares Flood AI._

## Problem

Kyrgyzstan has 8,000+ glaciers and 2,000+ glacial lakes; GLOF events are existential risk for downstream Ferghana Valley population (UZ side) and KG hydropower infrastructure. KG-UP-2025-001 (state services improvement) and Digital Code (KG-LAW-2025-178) provide regulatory frame. Forthcoming KG-TF-2026-008 ($2.8M, AI disaster prevention — early warning) is in pipeline. case-kg-glof-warning documents the existing partial system. Netherlands Deltares Flood AI (case-nl-deltares-flood-ai) is the world reference. Geopolitical leverage: KG controls headwaters for Syr Darya basin which feeds UZ cotton industry — making KG glacier monitoring a regional security asset that ICWC (Interstate Commission for Water Coordination) and Central Asian states value.

## Solution concept

Three-layer system: (1) satellite + IoT sensor data fusion — Sentinel-2/Landsat satellite imagery + ground IoT sensors at high-risk glacial lakes, processed through computer-vision models for ice volume, lake area, and structural integrity; (2) GLOF risk prediction model — physics-informed ML (Deltares-style) producing 72-hour and 7-day risk forecasts per lake; (3) early warning operator console for KG Ministry of Emergency Situations, regional ICWC counterparts, and downstream hydropower operators (Toktogul, Naryn) in Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual. Architectural anchor: Deltares physics-informed flood AI (case-nl-deltares-flood-ai) preserved as core; adapted for KG glacial-lake context, Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual UX, ICWC regional sharing, and integration with KG-MINENERGY hydropower telemetry.
