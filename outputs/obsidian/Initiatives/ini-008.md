# Healthcare AI Diagnostic Platform (UZ)

_Deploy AI-assisted clinical decision support across primary care and oblast hospitals, replicating Kazakhstan Damumed (case-kz-damumed) on $200M WB Health Modernization rails._

## Problem

UZ healthcare is undergoing major reform under PP-2024-038 + UP-2025-088, with $200M WB Health System Modernization (WB-UZ-HEALTH-P178562) co-financing. Live tender UZ-T-2026-003 ($6.5M, digital medical info system extension) is open; forthcoming UZ-TF-2026-003 ($8.5M, AI e-prescription with monitoring) follows. Kazakhstan Damumed proves the architecture; UZ adaptation needed.

## Solution concept

AI clinical decision support stack: (1) primary-care AI triage (chief complaint to differential diagnosis, integrated with E-Health Center system); (2) prescription appropriateness checker (integrated with UZ-TF-2026-003 e-prescription tender); (3) imaging AI (chest x-ray, retina) for oblast hospitals where radiologists are scarce. Architectural anchor: Damumed preserved as core. UZ adaptation: Russian-Uzbek bilingual UX for clinicians, integration with UZ E-Health Center, UzCloud deployment.

## Pitch hook

> [!quote] Hook
> Kazakhstan Damumed proved AI clinical decision support reduces oblast-hospital diagnostic error by 25%+. UZ has 12,000 primary-care clinicians and a $200M WB modernization envelope. RFP UZ-T-2026-003 ($6.5M) closes Q3 2026 — we deliver Russian-Uzbek bilingual Damumed-class stack on UzCloud.

## Risk register

- **political**: Health minister rotation (UZ MoH has rotated multiple times since 2017). — _Mitigation_: Anchor at E-Health Center director (uz-ehealth-center-head) who survives ministerial cycles.
- **technical**: Clinical data quality and EHR adoption uneven across oblasts; AI training data biased toward urban. — _Mitigation_: Federated training; phase 1 limited to data-rich oblasts; expand as data quality improves.
- **commercial**: Damumed has KZ-UZ health cooperation channel and may underbid. — _Mitigation_: Lead with Russian-Uzbek bilingual + UZ guidelines; partner positioning rather than direct compete.
