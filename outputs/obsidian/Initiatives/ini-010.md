# Cybersecurity SOC Modernization (UZ)

_Deliver SOC modernization for UZ critical infrastructure under UP-2026-038 (Cybersecurity Strategy), live tender UZ-T-2026-006 ($5.5M)._

## Problem

UZ-UP-2026-038 (Feb 2026, Cybersecurity Strategy) is in active-window status; PP-2024-038-CYBERSEC-BANKING mandates banking-sector minimum infosec requirements. Live tender UZ-T-2026-006 ($5.5M, SOC threat detection and prevention) is open. Estonia NCSC (case-est-cyber-defense) is direct architectural anchor. UNICON.UZ is the local certification authority and operational partner.

## Solution concept

SOC modernization stack: (1) AI-powered threat-detection (UEBA, anomaly detection on government and critical-infrastructure network logs); (2) Russian-Uzbek bilingual analyst console (NIST CSF-aligned playbooks); (3) integration with State Cyber Center and UNICON.UZ certification regime. Architectural anchor: Estonia NCSC NIST-CSF-aligned SOC + KZ analog. UZ adaptation: bilingual analyst UX, UZ critical-infrastructure list, sovereign deployment on UzCloud.
