# AML Banking Sector AI (KG-TF-2026-005)

_Deliver AI-powered AML/CFT compliance system for KG commercial banks under forthcoming KG-TF-2026-005 ($1.8M), aligned with FATF and digital som compliance._

## Problem

Forthcoming KG-TF-2026-005 ($1.8M, AML system update for commercial banks) is in pipeline. KG-LAW-2025-074-NBKR-CBDC mandates AML at the digital som level. NBKR FSA (KG-FSA) supervises commercial-bank AML. Regional and FATF compliance pressures, plus the labor migration remittance corridor, make AML a recurring procurement need. Brazil Pix AML (case-br-pix), Singapore CBDC (case-sg-cbdc-project-ubin), and Kazakhstan AIFC (case-kz-aifc-fintech) are precedents.

## Solution concept

AML AI stack for commercial banks: (1) transaction-monitoring ML with anomaly detection across remittance, e-commerce, and merchant flows; (2) sanctions screening with sub-second latency on OFAC, EU, UN, NBKR lists; (3) Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual case-management UX for compliance officers; (4) integration with digital som CBDC AML rail (linked KG-INI-002). Architectural anchor: Singapore CBDC AML pattern + Kazakhstan AIFC fintech regime.
