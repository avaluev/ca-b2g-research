# Digital Code Secondary Regulations & AI Registry

_Co-author KG Digital Code secondary regulations on AI auditability and model registry, then deliver the AI registry platform under live RFP KG-T-2026-008 ($650K) and forthcoming KG-TF-2026-004 ($850K)._

## Problem

KG-LAW-2025-178-DIGITAL-CODE (Digital Code) and KG-LAW-2025-179-DIGITAL-CODE-ENACTMENT entered into force in 2025-2026 but require secondary regulations on AI auditability, model registry, and certification. KG-AI-SECONDARY-REG-WORKING-GROUP (Tier 7 institution) is drafting these now. Live RFP KG-T-2026-008 ($650K, Digital Code secondary legislation TA) is open and forthcoming KG-TF-2026-004 ($850K, AI registry and certification system) follows. The vendor who shapes the secondary regulations and then delivers the AI registry platform owns the standards-shaping window. trend kg-trend-003-digital-code-secondary-regs has 7 months window remaining — among the tightest in the KG landscape.

## Solution concept

Two-track delivery: (1) Technical Assistance to KG-AI-SECONDARY-REG-WORKING-GROUP — paid TA contract on drafting AI auditability provisions, model registry schema, and certification process. Apply EU AI Act and Singapore IMDA model AI governance framework as reference; adapt for KG centralized governance and Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual deliverables. (2) Implementation contract for the AI registry platform itself — versioned model registry deployed on Tunduk inference fabric (linked to KG-INI-003), with audit-trail logging, certification workflow, and Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual operator console for the National AI Council (KG-NATIONAL-AI-COUNCIL). Architectural anchor: Singapore PAIR government LLM governance model + Indonesia Satu Data registry pattern; adapted for KG Digital Code requirements.

## Risk register

- **political**: Secondary regulation drafting is politically contested — civil society, donors, and the Working Group all have input rights and timelines may slip. — _Mitigation_: Anchor at the Working Group through formal TA contract (procurement-grade), not informal advisory. Couple with AUCA academic co-author letter for civil-society legitimacy.
- **commercial**: EU AI Act implementation consultancies (Bird & Bird, Latham & Watkins) bid via European prime contractor pathway. — _Mitigation_: Partner with one European law firm as legal-content subcontractor while we hold the Russian-Kyrgyz drafting and registry platform delivery; bid as JV not pure software vendor.
- **technical**: AI registry must align with downstream platforms (Tunduk inference fabric, EHR AI, CBDC AML AI). If schema is locked early without platform input, rework is expensive. — _Mitigation_: Architect schema with explicit versioning and migration paths; require Tunduk SE technical lead (kg-tunduk-tech-lead) review before schema lock-in.
