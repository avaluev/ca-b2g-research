# Mahalla Digital Social Registry AI (UZ)

_Deliver AI-driven Mahalla-level digital social registry under PP-2025-286, replicating Kazakhstan e-Otinish (case-kz-smart-social) and India e-Shram._

## Problem

PP-2025-286 (active-window) commits to digital Mahalla and social-services registry. UZ has 9000+ Mahalla committees serving as front-line social-service delivery; current registry fragmented across MoH, MoSocProt, Mahalla Foundation. Forthcoming RFP UZ-TF-2026-012 ($5.8M, AI social-protection registry) follows. Kazakhstan e-Otinish + India e-Shram are direct precedents.

## Solution concept

AI-driven social registry stack: (1) Mahalla-level case officer mobile app with AI-assisted vulnerability scoring; (2) inter-agency data fetch via X-Road-equivalent (MyID + Sud.uz + EPIGU integration); (3) Russian-Uzbek bilingual conversational intake (call center + chatbot). Architectural anchor: KZ e-Otinish for vulnerability scoring + e-Shram for unorganized-worker registration. UZ adaptation: Mahalla-committee front-line layer (uniquely UZ).
