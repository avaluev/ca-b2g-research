# Mountain Telemedicine AI Triage (AKDN/SDC)

_Deploy AI telemedicine triage for KG mountain communities under AKDN-KG-DIGITAL-SCHOOLS2030 and forthcoming KG-TF-2026-010 ($1.8M), replicating Bangladesh a2i and Rwanda Babyl precedents._

## Problem

Kyrgyzstan is >90% mountainous with scattered rural population and acute shortage of specialists outside Bishkek and Osh. AKDN-KG-DIGITAL-SCHOOLS2030 ($15M) and EU-KG-SDC-GOVERNANCE ($16.2M) co-finance digital health and rural inclusion. Forthcoming tender KG-TF-2026-010 ($1.8M, mountain telemedicine IoT + AI diagnostic) is in pipeline. Bangladesh a2i Telemedicine + community clinics (case-bd-a2i-health), Rwanda Babyl AI chatbot (case-rw-babyl), India eSanjeevani (case-in-esanjeevani), and Estonia e-Health (case-est-ehealth) are precedent cases. Mountain telemedicine is one of the highest-impact AI-for-good use cases globally.

## Solution concept

Three-tier delivery: (1) ayil ambulatory clinic kit — point-of-care diagnostic device pack (smartphone-grade ECG, otoscope, dermascope) running offline-first AI triage models in Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual; (2) telemedicine bridge with Bishkek Republican Hospital and Osh Inter-Oblast Hospital for specialist consult; (3) AI symptom triage chatbot routing patients between self-care, ambulatory visit, telemedicine bridge, and emergency referral — Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual, deployed via Telegram/WhatsApp for accessibility. Architectural anchor: Bangladesh a2i community-clinic model + Rwanda Babyl chatbot triage + India eSanjeevani national platform integration. Adapted for KG mountain offline constraints, Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual UX, Tunduk-bound patient identity, AKDN's existing presence in Naryn/Osh oblasts.
