# Digital Som CBDC Wallet & AML/KYC Stack

_Deliver wallet, AML/KYC, fraud-detection and crypto-compliance AI stack for the digital som CBDC under NBKR ahead of mandatory 1 January 2027 go-live (Constitutional Law 74 of 16.04.2025)._

## Problem

KG-LAW-2025-074-NBKR-CBDC (16 April 2025) is a Constitutional Law amendment establishing the digital som with mandatory go-live on 1 January 2027. NBKR holds exclusive emission and platform-operation authority. Pilot is running through 2026. Live tender KG-T-2026-004 ($3.5M, digital som software infrastructure) is open and forthcoming KG-TF-2026-002 ($4.5M, Phase 2 offline transactions and AML/KYC) follows in 2026. Constitutional law deadlines cannot slip without legislative amendment — this is the rarest mandatory-by-law procurement event in Central Asia. Brazil Pix (case-br-pix), Singapore Project Ubin (case-sg-cbdc-project-ubin), and India UPI (case-in-upi) provide proven CBDC architecture blueprints.

## Solution concept

Deliver a four-layer digital som platform stack: (1) consumer wallet (Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual, KYC-bound, MIR/SBP-interoperable for diaspora remittance corridor); (2) AML/KYC AI engine integrating NBKR sanctions lists and Tunduk identity fetch; (3) fraud-detection ML for transaction anomaly scoring at scale (target: 5M+ active wallets by 2028); (4) crypto-virtual-asset compliance layer cross-referencing the KGST stablecoin and the Virtual Assets Registry mandated by KG-LAW-2022-012-VIRTUAL and KG-LAW-2026-003-VA-AMEND. Architecturally anchored on Brazil Pix's centralized rail + Singapore Ubin's wholesale-to-retail bridge. Adapted for KG's hydropower-funded sovereign infrastructure preference and the labor migration remittance use case (Russia → KG corridor, $2.8B annually) where the digital som can capture 5–15% of transaction flow.
