# UDP TOR Drafting Co-Author (Mintsifry Successor)

_Pro-bono co-author the post-Минцифры TORs that the Presidential Administration's UDP Digital Department must publish by 31.07.2026 for every inherited program — Tunduk modernization, e-procurement, digital health, AI strategy, Digital Code secondary regulations._

## Problem

On approximately 30.04.2026 the Ministry of Digital Development of the Kyrgyz Republic was liquidated by KG-UP-2026-MINCIFRY-ABOLITION. All functions, programs, donor relationships, and Tunduk governance were transferred to the Digital Transformation Department of the Administration of Presidential Affairs (UDP) under Adilbek Asanbekov. UDP has 1 month to publish structural regulations and 3 months to align every Cabinet decision and donor TOR. They have no in-house technical capacity to draft 20+ procurement TORs in that window. The vendor who provides credible pro-bono drafting input shapes the specification of every downstream contract for the next 24 months — a one-time concentration event that mirrors PP-320 in UZ but is even more leverage-rich because it is structural, not just a fund.

## Solution concept

Three-track engagement executed in 8 weeks: (1) Submit a Technical Assistance offer letter to UDP Digital Department head Adilbek Asanbekov via diaspora warm intro through Talant Sultanov (former Mintsifry Minister, CMU-educated, now bridges ex-Mintsifry team into UDP). Offer is pro-bono drafting of TORs for three priority programs: Tunduk 2.0 modernization, e-procurement AI analytics, and the National AI Strategy framework draft. (2) Submit parallel Technical Assistance registration with WB Digital CASA PIU (Sandra Sargent TTL) and ADB e-Procurement PIU (Aibek Abdybakirov TTL) so UDP can introduce us as a pre-qualified WB/ADB-accepted vendor inside the donor procurement channels. (3) Embed a senior advisor on-site in Bishkek for 60 days during the structural regulation drafting window, co-located with UDP staff, producing TOR drafts that comply with WB/ADB procurement modalities (QCBS, ICB, RFP). Architecturally anchored on Estonia eGA twinning experience (EGA-KG-TWINNING is the active Estonia-Kyrgyzstan technical assistance vehicle). What is preserved: eGA's TOR drafting playbook for X-Road / Tunduk-class systems. What is adapted: KG-specific donor procurement rules, Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual deliverable requirements, and UDP's centralized governance structure.

## Risk register

- **political**: UDP Digital Department leadership stabilization is incomplete. Asanbekov's deputy structure and decision authority are still being formalized. A second reorganization within 12 months cannot be ruled out. — _Mitigation_: Build relationship at the deputy level (kg-presidential-digital-dept-dep, Timur Koichubaev) and at the Tunduk technical lead (kg-tunduk-tech-lead, Mirlan Asanbekov) — institutional memory survives leadership rotation. Anchor parallel relationship at WB Digital CASA PIU which is donor-side and unaffected by KG reorganization.
- **commercial**: Pro-bono drafting can be absorbed without delivering paid follow-on work if competitor vendors (eGA, Cybernetica, large international firms) push UDP toward conventional procurement channels. — _Mitigation_: Time-bound the pro-bono engagement to specific deliverables (3 TORs, 60 days). Couple every pro-bono deliverable with a paid TA proposal under EGA twinning or donor TA channel. Make the engagement registered, named, and citable in subsequent bids.
- **technical**: Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual capability claims must be benchmarked against Yandex GPT, GigaChat, and any forthcoming Kyrgyz NLP efforts. Failure to demonstrate measurable Kyrgyz coverage discredits the entire engagement. — _Mitigation_: Publish an open Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual NLP benchmark dataset and evaluation protocol with co-authorship from AUCA (kg-auca-rector, Andrew Wachtel) and HTP linguistic resident. Make the benchmark third-party verifiable before any TOR submission.
