# EPIGU AI Citizen Chatbot

_Replace EPIGU's rule-based citizen bot with a sovereign Russian-Uzbek LLM chatbot covering 1000+ services, leveraging Bürokratt as architectural anchor._

## Problem

EPIGU (Uzbekistan's e-government portal) handles ~5M citizen interactions annually but its existing chatbot is rule-based and Uzbek-only. Live RFP UZ-T-2026-005 ($2.8M, AI chatbot for EPIGU in Russian and Uzbek) is open with submission Q2 2026 and forthcoming UZ-TF-2026-004 ($9.5M, agentic expansion to 1000+ services) follows in 2027. Estonia's Bürokratt — the world's reference virtual government assistant on X-Road — is direct architectural blueprint.

## Solution concept

Deliver Bürokratt-class agentic chatbot stack: (1) Russian-Uzbek bilingual conversational layer trained on EPIGU's existing service taxonomy; (2) X-Road-compatible inter-agency data fetcher (UZ has built MyID + Sud.uz interconnects which are X-Road-equivalent); (3) human-handoff escalation to Public Services Agency contact center; (4) audit-trail compliance with personal-data law (UZ-LAW-2019-547 + UZ-LAW-2026-1125). Architectural anchor: Bürokratt's modular bot + government LLM separation. UZ adaptation: triple-script Uzbek + Russian, MyID identity verification, integration with EPIGU's existing 350-service catalog scaling to 1000+.
