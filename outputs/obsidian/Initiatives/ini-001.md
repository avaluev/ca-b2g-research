# AI Fund Criteria Co-Design (PP-320)

_Co-design selection criteria for the $100M National AI Project Support Fund and pre-position as one of the first three flagship recipients._

## Problem

PP-320 (May 2025) created a $100M state Fund to back 50 AI projects through 2028, with selection rules drafted between May-September 2026 by MoDT under MoEF supervision. No external vendor has yet shaped the rubric; the Fund's evaluation council is forming and will lock in criteria favoring incumbents unless alternative voices arrive in the next 90 days.

## Solution concept

Three-track engagement: (1) submit a written technical brief to MoDT and the AI Center on which selection axes drive measurable national AI capability vs. vanity grants (sovereign-LLM enablement, Russian/Uzbek language coverage, government-deployable agentic workflows, sntz.ai-style stack); (2) volunteer as evaluator or observer to the Fund's working group via diaspora warm intro (Tursunov/Google, Toshmatov/Stanford); (3) submit one of the first three flagship project applications in Q4 2026 anchored on a sovereign Uzbek-Russian LLM + government agentic chatbot product line to be deployed at EPIGU and the Supreme Court. The criteria-shaping phase is the highest-leverage 90-day window in the entire UZ AI economy.
