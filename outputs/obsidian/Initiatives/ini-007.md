# Sovereign AI Compute Cluster (NUU + UzCloud)

_Establish the sovereign AI compute cluster at NUU AI Cluster connected to UzCloud, anchoring the foundation-model and verticals stack._

## Problem

UZ has no production-grade sovereign AI compute cluster; current government AI workloads dependent on Huawei Cloud Stack (CHINA-DSR-UZ-GOVCLOUD) or commercial cloud abroad. Live RFP UZ-T-2026-001 ($50M, GPU compute cluster) is open and forthcoming UZ-TF-2026-005 ($35M, consolidated data center) follows. NUU AI Cluster is the policy-blessed academic anchor.

## Solution concept

Deliver a hybrid GPU compute cluster: (1) ~500 H100/H200 or equivalent GPUs (or Huawei Ascend hybrid for sanction-resilience); (2) UzCloud-integrated multi-tenant orchestration; (3) NUU AI Cluster academic co-host for research uses; (4) government tenant priority with private-sector secondary tenancy. Architectural anchor: combination of Singapore PAIR (case-sg-pair-govt-llm) compute design + Saudi SDAIA (case-sa-sdaia-ai-edu) academic-government hybrid.
