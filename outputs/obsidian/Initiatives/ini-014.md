# AI Precision Agriculture (UZ-Cotton + Wheat)

_Deliver satellite + IoT AI for cotton, wheat, and water-management at MinSelxoz, replicating Kazakhstan KZ-Cotton-AI + Israel Netafim, $500M WB Agri rails._

## Problem

UZ-PP-2025-099 + UZ-PP-2024-227 + UZ-PP-2023-162-AGRI commit to digital agriculture; WB-UZ-AGRI-DIGITAL ($500M) is the donor envelope. Live tender UZ-T-2026-014 ($4.8M, AI agri monitoring) is open and forthcoming UZ-TF-2026-007 ($6.2M, satellite agri AI) follows. KZ Crop Monitoring (case-kz-cotton-ai) + KZ Smart Agri (case-kz-smart-agri) + Israel Netafim are direct precedents.

## Solution concept

AI precision-agri stack: (1) satellite imagery + IoT crop-health AI (cotton wilt detection, wheat yield prediction); (2) water-allocation AI (link to MinVoda); (3) Russian-Uzbek bilingual farmer-extension app. Architectural anchor: KZ Crop Monitoring (Sentinel + ML) + Netafim precision irrigation. UZ adaptation: cotton-specific models, Aral basin water mgmt, Russian-Uzbek bilingual extension.
