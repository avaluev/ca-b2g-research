# Sovereign Uzbek-Russian LLM (Foundation Model)

_Build the sovereign Uzbek-Russian foundation model for government deployments, anchored by UP-189 and forthcoming tender UZ-TF-2026-011 ($12M)._

## Problem

UP-189 (May 2025) commits to AI development with sovereign capability priority. Forthcoming tender UZ-TF-2026-011 ($12M, sovereign Uzbek-Russian language model for government use) is in pipeline. No existing model has demonstrated production-quality bilingual Uzbek-Cyrillic + Uzbek-Latin + Russian coverage; UAE Falcon and Saudi SDAIA models are direct architectural anchors with known training methodology.

## Solution concept

Train and deliver a 7B-13B parameter Uzbek-Russian sovereign LLM optimized for government use cases: legal NLP, citizen services, education, healthcare conversation. Architectural anchor: Falcon LLM training methodology (data curation, instruction tuning, RLHF) preserved. UZ adaptation: triple-script Uzbek + Russian corpus, government domain specialization, NUU AI Cluster co-publication for academic credibility.
