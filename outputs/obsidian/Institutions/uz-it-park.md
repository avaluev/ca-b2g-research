# IT Park Uzbekistan

**RU**: ИТ-Парк Узбекистана  
**Local**: O'zbekiston IT Parki  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Special economic zone and accelerator for IT/AI companies. Tax benefits: 0% income/payroll tax for residents. 700+ resident companies, $300M+ export target. Key institution for landing AI vendors in Uzbekistan — IT Park residency is the standard market-entry mechanism. Post-2017 institution with Western-educated management.

**Head**: [[People/uz-farhod-ibragimov|uz-farhod-ibragimov]]
