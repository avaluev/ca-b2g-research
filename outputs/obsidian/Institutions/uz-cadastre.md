# State Committee for Cadastre (under Ministry of Economy and Finance)

**RU**: Государственный комитет по кадастру (при Министерстве экономики и финансов)  
**Local**: Davlat Kadastr Qo'mitasi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Implementing WB-financed Geospatial NSDI project ($35M, 2025-2030). Buyer for satellite imagery AI, land parcel digitalization, GIS platform, geospatial data analytics. Key procurement authority for CW-009 (geospatial tenders). Post-2017 restructured agency with modern mandate.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-moef|UZ-MOEF]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era restructuring — new scope as lead NSDI agency
- WB Geospatial NSDI project ($35M) launched 2025 — active procurement
- Geoportal modernization tender issued
- Satellite imagery AI analytics procurement window (CW-009)
