# Ministry of Investments, Industry and Trade of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство инвестиций, промышленности и торговли Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Investitsiyalar, sanoat va savdo vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Oversees industrial digitalization, e-commerce development, FDI attraction for tech sector. Key counterpart for SME digitalization programs. Post-2017 restructured super-ministry merging investment, industry, trade portfolios. Karimov Inversion: new leadership, new scope.
