# High Tech Park of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Парк высоких технологий Кыргызской Республики (ПВТ)  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Жогорку технологиялар паркы  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Special economic zone for ICT/AI companies. Tax privileges for resident companies. Primary market-entry mechanism for foreign AI vendors in Kyrgyzstan (equivalent to IT Park UZ). Smaller scale than UZ IT Park but similar function.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-cabinet|KG-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Resident company program expansion
- AI startup incubation programs
- Digital Code (Цифровой кодекс 2025) implementation regarding HTP operation
