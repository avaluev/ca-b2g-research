# Center for the Development of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Economy (under Ministry of Digital Technologies)

**RU**: Центр развития искусственного интеллекта и цифровой экономики при МЦТ  
**Local**: Sun'iy intellekt va raqamli iqtisodiyotni rivojlantirish markazi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Post-2017 institution created specifically to implement ПП-358 (AI Strategy to 2030). Runs AI testing environments, AI standards drafting, AI vendor certification. Key body for shaping AI procurement TORs. Convergent Window CW-003 (criteria for $100M AI fund distribution). PRIME target for pre-procurement vendor engagement.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-mintsifry|UZ-MINTSIFRY]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era institution — created 2024, entirely new mandate
- Implementation of ПП-358 AI Strategy to 2030 — TOR drafting authority
- Criteria drafting for $100M AI fund distribution (CW-003, window May-Sept 2026)
- AI vendor certification and testing framework development
- University AI lab curriculum standards under УП-189
