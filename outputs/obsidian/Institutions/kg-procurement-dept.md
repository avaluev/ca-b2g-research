# Department of State Procurement under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Департамент государственных закупок при Кабинете Министров Кыргызской Республики  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Министрлер Кабинети жанындагы Мамлекеттик сатып алуулар департаменти  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Operates zakupki.gov.kg electronic procurement portal. Responsible body under KG procurement laws (№72/2015, №27/2022). ADB project 55109-001 (e-Procurement digitalization) implements through this department. KEY ENTRY POINT for all B2G vendors — understand zakupki.gov.kg procedures.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-cabinet|KG-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- ADB project 55109-001 e-Procurement digitalization (Phase 1) — active implementation
- Zakupki.gov.kg portal modernization
- New public procurement law №27/2022 full implementation
- Electronic procurement mandatory format rollout
