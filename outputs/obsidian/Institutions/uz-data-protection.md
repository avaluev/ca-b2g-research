# Authorized Body for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Уполномоченный орган по защите персональных данных Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: Shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni himoya qilish bo'yicha vakolatli organ  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Enforces personal data protection law. Data localization requirements for AI systems processing citizen data. Any foreign AI vendor must comply with UZ data residency rules. Key certification body for GDPR-equivalent compliance in Uzbekistan.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-mintsifry|UZ-MINTSIFRY]]

- AI system data compliance requirements development
