# Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство внутренних дел Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Ichki ishlar vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Implements biometric ID card system (УП-6065). Operating State Center for Personalization (sub-unit). Buyer for facial recognition, biometric verification, border control AI. Oversees vehicle traffic violation automation, AI-assisted policing systems.

**Head**: [[People/uz-pulat-bobojonov|uz-pulat-bobojonov]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Mandatory biometric ID card rollout (УП-6065: mandatory from Jan 2023)
- Connected vehicle / DDA driver license digital system
- Traffic management AI / smart camera networks (in partnership with MoDT)
