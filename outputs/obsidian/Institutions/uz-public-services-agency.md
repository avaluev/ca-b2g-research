# Agency for Public Services under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Агентство государственных услуг при Президенте Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Prezidenti huzuridagi Davlat xizmatlari agentligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Operates Multifunctional Centers (МФЦ/ЦГУ) network and EPIGU e-government portal (720+ services). Key buyer for citizen-facing AI/chatbot, service automation, queue management systems. Post-2017 institution with modern mandate — direct analog to Azerbaijan ASAN Service. Convergent Window CW-007 (EPIGU agentic chatbot).

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-president|UZ-PRESIDENT]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era institution — modern mandate, fast procurement track
- EPIGU portal expansion to 720+ services
- AI citizen chatbot / conversational assistant procurement planned 2026 (CW-007)
- Multifunctional center (ЦГУ) network expansion
