## Mandate (AI / digital)

CRITICAL — The new digital authority for all of Kyrgyzstan from April 2026. Absorbs all Mintsifry functions: Tunduk oversight, e-government services, AI policy, cybersecurity coordination, telecom regulation. UДП Structural Regulations pending publication (≤31.05.2026). This is the single highest-leverage institution in KG for digital vendor engagement. New TORs for all legacy Mintsifry programs will be drafted here (CW-004).
