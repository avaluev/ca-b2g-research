# Ministry of Emergency Situations of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство по чрезвычайным ситуациям Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Favqulodda vaziyatlar vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Buyer for AI emergency dispatch systems, flood and earthquake early-warning AI, disaster risk modeling. Fergana Valley and Tien Shan seismic risk drives demand. Donor co-financed programs (UNDP, JICA, EU) for disaster risk reduction.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- AI-enhanced emergency dispatch pilots
- Early-warning system modernization for seismic and flood risks
- Participation in Central Asian disaster risk donor programs
