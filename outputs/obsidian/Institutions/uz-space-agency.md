# Agency for Space Research and Technologies of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Uzbekistan Space Agency)

**RU**: Агентство космических исследований и технологий Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Fazoviy tadqiqotlar va texnologiyalar agentligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Operates UzbekSat-1 satellite. Provides remote sensing data for agriculture, environment, cadastre. Key buyer for satellite imagery AI processing, Earth observation analytics. Post-2017 institution with growing mandate for commercial space applications.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era institution — new commercial satellite applications mandate
- UzbekSat imagery provision for NSDI Cadastre project
- Earth observation AI analytics partnership programs
