# National Council on Artificial Intelligence of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Национальный совет по искусственному интеллекту Кыргызской Республики  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Жасалма интеллект боюнча улуттук кеңеши  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Cross-cutting AI governance body. Reports to Presidential Administration (KG-UDP post-April 2026). Responsible body in decree queue for National AI Strategy (announced, pending final signature as of May 2026). Drafts AI policy standards and testing frameworks under Цифровой кодекс. THE pre-procurement engagement target for AI vendors — spec-setting authority.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-udp|KG-UDP]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- National AI Strategy draft development (pending Presidential signature as of May 2026)
- AI testing framework standards under Цифровой кодекс 2025
- Council reconstitution under UDP oversight after Mintsifry dissolution
- CW-010: secondary regulations drafting for AI auditability and model registries
