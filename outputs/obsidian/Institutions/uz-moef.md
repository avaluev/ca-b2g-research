# Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство экономики и финансов Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Iqtisodiyot va moliya vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Controls FRR (Fund for Reconstruction and Development) — source of $100M AI fund (ПП-320). Budget authority for all state AI/ICT programs. Oversees Cadastre Agency (NSDI $35M WB project). Key gatekeeper for foreign investment in digital sectors.

**Head**: [[People/uz-jamshid-kuchkarov|uz-jamshid-kuchkarov]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- FRR disbursement administration for ПП-320 ($100M AI fund)
- Budget allocation oversight for Digital Uzbekistan-2030 programs
- WB Geospatial NSDI project ($35M) counterpart ministry
