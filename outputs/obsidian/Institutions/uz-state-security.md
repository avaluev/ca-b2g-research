# State Security Service of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Служба государственной безопасности Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Davlat xavfsizlik xizmati  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Responsible body under ЗРУ-764 (Cybersecurity Law). Coordinates cybersecurity operations for classified and critical infrastructure systems. Clears vendors for sensitive government AI/data contracts. UNICON.UZ operates as civilian arm.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-president|UZ-PRESIDENT]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Co-oversight of ЗРУ-764 cybersecurity framework (amended ЗРУ-964, 2024)
- Critical infrastructure protection standards for AI systems
