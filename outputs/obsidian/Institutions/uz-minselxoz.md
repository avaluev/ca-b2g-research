# Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство сельского хозяйства Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Qishloq xo'jaligi vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Responsible under multiple decrees for agro-digital programs. Procures satellite imagery AI for cotton, wheat, and livestock monitoring. Key donor counterpart for WB/EBRD agritech programs. Remote sensing and field digitalization tenders.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Smart agriculture satellite imagery analytics programs
- Digital field monitoring rollout (cotton and wheat crops)
- Co-implementation of NSDI geospatial data for agricultural land records
