# Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Кабинет Министров Кыргызской Республики  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Министрлер Кабинети  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Issues Cabinet Resolutions implementing presidential decrees. Key vehicle for digital program implementation regulations. Under Japarov Concentration, Cabinet is subordinate to Presidential Administration on major decisions. Relevant for program-level budgeting.

**Head**: [[People/kg-adylbek-kasymaliev|kg-adylbek-kasymaliev]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-president|KG-PRESIDENT]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Implementation regulations for Цифровой кодекс (Digital Code 2025)
- Budget allocations for digital transformation programs
- April 2026 Mintsifry abolition formally executed through Cabinet process
