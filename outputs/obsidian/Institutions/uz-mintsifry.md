# Ministry of Digital Technologies of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство цифровых технологий Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Raqamli texnologiyalar vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Lead ministry for all AI/digital policy in Uzbekistan. Authorized body for e-commerce (ЗРУ-792), digital signatures (ЗРУ-793), cybersecurity (ЗРУ-764), AI strategy (ПП-358). Oversees MyID, EPIGU portal, IT Park, Uzinfocom, UzCloud, AI Center. Issues technical standards for all state digital systems. Renamed from Ministry of Information Technologies and Communications (MITC) post-2017 — karimov_inversion landmark institution.

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era institution — leadership Western-educated, hungry for visible wins
- Implementation of ПП-358 (AI Strategy to 2030) — primary responsible ministry
- Gov.uz domain migration for all state websites (2025)
- AI Center for digital economy created under MoDT
- MyID mobile-ID rollout 2025-2026
- EPIGU portal expansion to 720+ services
- Oversight of UzCloud sovereign cloud infrastructure
