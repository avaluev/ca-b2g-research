# Interdepartmental Commission on AI Development (under ПП-358)

**RU**: Межведомственная комиссия по развитию искусственного интеллекта (при ПП-358)  
**Local**: Sun'iy intellektni rivojlantirishning idoralararo komissiyasi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Cross-ministry AI development coordination body created by ПП-358 (AI Strategy to 2030). Sets priorities for M AI fund. Approves AI project criteria. Highest-leverage pre-procurement body for AI vendors — TORs originate here. CW-003 engagement target.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- ПП-358 AI Strategy implementation oversight
- M AI fund (ПП-320) criteria development (CW-003, window May-Sept 2026)
- AI governance framework for government AI deployments
