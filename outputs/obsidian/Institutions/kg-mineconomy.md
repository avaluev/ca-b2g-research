# Ministry of Economy and Commerce of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Министерство экономики и коммерции Кыргызской Республики  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Экономика жана коммерция министрлиги  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Oversees Financial Services Regulatory Authority (SRFR / FSA). Responsible for virtual assets regulation, stablecoin USDKG framework. Key counterpart for digital economy/e-commerce regulatory programs.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-cabinet|KG-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Virtual assets and stablecoin USDKG regulatory framework
- FSA supervision of digital financial services
- E-commerce regulatory framework development
