# National Coordination Council on Cybersecurity of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Национальный координационный совет по кибербезопасности Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Kiberxavfsizlik bo'yicha Milliy Muvofiqlashtirish Kengashi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Cross-cutting cybersecurity governance body created under ЗРУ-764. Coordinates cybersecurity policy across all state bodies and critical infrastructure. Relevant for any AI/data security vendor seeking government contracts.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-president|UZ-PRESIDENT]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Implementation oversight of cybersecurity law ЗРУ-764 as amended by ЗРУ-964 (20.09.2024)
- Cybersecurity standards for AI systems under УП-189 (2025)
