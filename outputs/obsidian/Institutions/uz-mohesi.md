# Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство высшего образования, науки и инноваций Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Oliy ta'lim, fan va innovatsiyalar vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Responsible for 15 university AI laboratories program (deadline 2026 per УП-189), AI Cluster at New Uzbekistan University, AI curriculum integration in higher education. Key counterpart for international AI education grants (KOICA, World Bank). Post-Karimov restructured ministry with new scope for science and innovation.

**Head**: [[People/uz-kongratbay-sharipov|uz-kongratbay-sharipov]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- Post-Karimov-era institution (renamed/restructured) — new scope includes innovation mandate
- Implementation of 15 university AI laboratories (deadline 2026, per УП-189)
- AI Cluster at New Uzbekistan University (deadline 01.03.2026)
- KOICA $14M IT Education Ecosystem partnership (youth employment program)
