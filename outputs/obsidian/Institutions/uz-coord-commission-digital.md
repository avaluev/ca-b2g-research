# Coordination Commission for Implementation of the Digital Uzbekistan-2030 Strategy

**RU**: Координационная комиссия по реализации Стратегии «Цифровой Узбекистан-2030»  
**Local**: «Raqamli O'zbekiston-2030» Strategiyasini amalga oshirish bo'yicha Muvofiqlashtirish Komissiyasi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Cross-cutting body that oversees the entire Digital Uzbekistan-2030 strategy. Has direct presidential reporting. Approves budget allocations under ПП-320 ($100M AI fund). Working groups spawn tenders. The HIGHEST leverage body for pre-procurement engagement — specs are set here before RFPs publish.
