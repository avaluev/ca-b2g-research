# Ministry of Justice of the Kyrgyz Republic

**RU**: Министерство юстиции Кыргызской Республики  
**Local**: Кыргыз Республикасынын Юстиция министрлиги  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Operates cbd.minjust.gov.kg (legal acts database — primary source for all KG legislation). Responsible body under personal data law (KG-LAW-2008-058). Legal digitalisation including GIZ digital justice program. E-notary, digital registration of legal entities.

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/kg-cabinet|KG-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- cbd.minjust.gov.kg maintenance — official legal database
- Personal data law implementation (KG-LAW-2008-058, as expanded by Цифровой кодекс)
- GIZ digital justice program counterpart
