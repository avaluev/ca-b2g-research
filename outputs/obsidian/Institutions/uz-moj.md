# Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство юстиции Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Adliya vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Responsible under УП-6065 (ID cards), ЗРУ-793 (digital signature), ЗРУ-792 (e-commerce law). Maintains legal normative acts on lex.uz. Key counterpart for е-justice / Digital Court (УП-140). Personal data protection regulation falls partially under MOJ scope.

**Head**: [[People/uz-akbar-tashkulov|uz-akbar-tashkulov]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- lex.uz platform maintenance and digital publication of all normative acts
- УП-140 (21.08.2025) co-implementer: Digital Court / AI in courts program
- Digital signature law implementation (ЗРУ-793, in force Jan 2023)
