# Ministry of Health of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Министерство здравоохранения Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Sog'liqni saqlash vazirligi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Lead ministry for E-Health platform, electronic prescription system, clinical decision support AI. Responsible body under ПП-5000 (2021) and ПП-415 (2023). Implementing e-prescription anti-fraud, AI triage, electronic medical records. Key procurement authority for Health AI tenders.

**Head**: [[People/uz-amrillo-inoyatov|uz-amrillo-inoyatov]]

**Reports to**: [[Institutions/uz-cabinet|UZ-CABINET]]

## Recent decisions (12 months)

- ПП-415 (28.12.2023) — E-Health digitalization: EPIGU health services, e-prescription rollout
- Electronic prescription system rollout 2024-2025
- AI-assisted clinical decision support pilot tender expected 2026
