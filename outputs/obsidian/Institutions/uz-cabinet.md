# Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan

**RU**: Кабинет Министров Республики Узбекистан  
**Local**: O'zbekiston Respublikasi Vazirlar Mahkamasi  

## Mandate (AI / digital)

Issues ПП (Cabinet Resolution) level decrees for digital implementation. Key vehicle: ПП-320 (30.10.2025) allocating $100M AI fund from FRR. Oversees all ministry-level digital programs. Approves state budget allocations for ICT and AI programs.
