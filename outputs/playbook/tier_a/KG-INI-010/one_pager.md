## Problem
KG social protection data is fragmented. 700,000+ social transfer recipients lack a unified digital vulnerability registry. AI-assisted targeting of social assistance is a high-priority UDP reform agenda item.

## Solution (Kazakhstan e-Otinish)
AI-driven vulnerability registry:
1. Tunduk-bound beneficiary identity
2. ML vulnerability scoring: income + health + family structure indicators
3. Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual case-officer mobile app
4. Inter-agency data fetch via Tunduk
