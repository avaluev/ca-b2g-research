## Problem (from УП-2025-189, lex.uz/7790236 — VERIFIED)

УП-2025-189 (October 2025) commits Uzbekistan to developing sovereign AI capability. No existing model has production-quality bilingual Uzbek-Cyrillic + Uzbek-Latin + Russian coverage. Yandex GPT and Sber GigaChat have Russian but not Uzbek; no Western model has meaningful Uzbek coverage. Forthcoming tender UZ-TF-2026-011 ($12M) is in the pipeline. The $100M AI Fund (PP-320) is the parallel funding mechanism for flagging the sovereign LLM as a flagship project.

## Solution (anchored on UAE Falcon LLM, case-ae-falcon-llm)

Train and deliver a 7B-13B parameter sovereign Uzbek-Russian LLM:
- Training data: Uzbek-Cyrillic corpus, Uzbek-Latin corpus (post-2018 official documents), Russian government-domain corpus
- Instruction-tuning: government domain (legal, healthcare, education, citizen services)
- Co-publication of training methodology and benchmark with NUU AI Cluster
- Deployment: government API on UzCloud — serves all downstream government AI verticals

**Downstream stack:** INI-002 (Courts legal NLP), INI-003 (EPIGU chatbot), INI-012 (Mahalla registry), INI-004 (Tax inspector UX) all use this LLM as the shared intelligence layer.

## Why This Is the Strategic Prize

This is not one contract — this is the foundation of all government AI contracts. The entity that trains and maintains the sovereign UZ LLM owns the horizontal layer across every vertical. UAE TII's Falcon LLM made the UAE a sovereign AI exporter; this is the same play at UZ scale.
