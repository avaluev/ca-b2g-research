# INI-001 — Pilot Proposal
**Pilot:** Sovereign Uzbek-Russian LLM v1 + EPIGU Citizen Chatbot (20 Services)
**Duration:** 9 months
**Estimated pilot contract:** $2,500,000–$5,000,000 (Fund flagship application)

---

## Scope

**Phase A — LLM Training and Benchmark (Months 1–4)**
- Deliver a 7B-parameter Uzbek-Russian sovereign language model, instruction-tuned on government domain corpus
- Training data: Uzbek-Cyrillic, Uzbek-Latin, Russian — government decrees, court decisions, citizen-services text
- Co-publish an open bilingual benchmark dataset with NUU AI Cluster
- Benchmark covers: intent classification, entity extraction, statute citation, citizen-query understanding
- Deployment: model served as a government API on UzCloud (sovereign data residency)

**Phase B — EPIGU Chatbot Pilot (Months 3–9, overlapping)**
- Deploy the LLM as the intelligence layer behind EPIGU's citizen chatbot
- Cover EPIGU's top 20 citizen services in Russian and Uzbek-Latin
- Integration: EPIGU service catalog API, MyID identity binding for personalized queries
- Human-handoff escalation to Public Services Agency contact center
- Audit-trail logging compliant with UZ-LAW-2019-547 data-residency rules

---

## Success Metrics (Binding)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Intent classification accuracy | ≥85% across 20 services (Russian + Uzbek) |
| Citizen-query containment | ≥70% without human handoff |
| Average response time | ≤3 seconds per query |
| Uzbek-Cyrillic / Latin parity | Within ±5% accuracy |
| UzCloud data residency | Full audit trail, zero foreign egress |
| NUU benchmark co-publication | Published and open-licensed by Month 4 |
| MoDT validator sign-off on Uzbek coverage | Named validators from MoDT or AI Center |
