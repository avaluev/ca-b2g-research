# INI-001 — 12-Month Relationship Sequence

---

## Month 1 (May–June 2026): Criteria Window — Maximum Urgency
**Objective:** Get technical brief into the hands of the AI Center director before the working group solidifies.

**Actions:**
1. Send decree-anchored LinkedIn message to AI Center Director (Toshmatov — verify identity first via IT Park contacts)
2. Simultaneously reach Abror Tursunov (Google DeepMind) and Akbar Toshmatov (Stanford HAI) for diaspora warm intro
3. Submit Technical Brief to MoDT official submission channel (mitc.uz or MoDT intake email)
4. Contact EBRD Central Asia digital team for framework alignment
5. Contact Ozod Tashpulatov (WB ECA) to align on WB language for the brief's KPI section

**Channel priority:** LinkedIn → diaspora warm intro → official submission → donor alignment

---

## Month 2 (June–July 2026): First Meeting
**Objective:** 30-minute virtual meeting with AI Center Director or Deputy Minister for AI & Innovation.

**Actions:**
1. Follow up on brief submission with meeting request: "20 minutes to walk through the benchmark methodology before the criteria close"
2. Deliver the NUU co-authored benchmark dataset as a gift — no strings attached
3. Request observer status or written submission slot in the Fund working group
4. Register IT Park JV entity (parallel track — must be complete before flagship application)

**Success signal:** Meeting confirmed OR written acknowledgment of brief receipt by AI Center / MoDT

---

## Month 3 (July–August 2026): Working Group Engagement
**Objective:** Named contributor to at least one criteria axis in the Fund working group.

**Actions:**
1. Submit formal written comments to working group through official channel
2. Attend any public consultation events (if open)
3. Publish the NUU bilateral benchmark dataset — creates public record of contribution
4. Begin flagship application preparation under PP-320 Fund

**Milestone:** Criteria working group closes September 2026. We want our name on the written input record.

---

## Month 4 (September–October 2026): Flagship Application
**Objective:** Submit one of the first three flagship AI Fund applications.

**Actions:**
1. Submit flagship application via IT Park JV: Sovereign Uzbek-Russian LLM + EPIGU agentic chatbot
2. Attach NUU benchmark co-publication, EBRD alignment letter, diaspora endorsements (Tursunov + Toshmatov)
3. Request meeting with AI Center evaluation panel to walk through the technical case
4. Simultaneously submit pre-qualification for EPIGU RFP UZ-T-2026-005 (INI-003, $2.8M) — parallel track

**Milestone:** Application submitted Q4 2026

---

## Month 5–7 (November 2026–January 2027): Evaluation Period
**Objective:** Stay in contact without being pushy; demonstrate progress.

**Actions:**
1. Publish technical progress updates (benchmark improvements, NUU training results) — public signals of capability
2. Attend Digital Bridge Tashkent if scheduled (September slot moved)
3. Maintain diaspora bridge contacts (Tursunov, Toshmatov) with monthly updates
4. Pursue EPIGU INI-003 RFP in parallel — winning smaller contract validates capability for Fund evaluation

**Signal:** If evaluation is quiet for 30+ days, use diaspora contact to check on timeline, not direct follow-up

---

## Month 8–10 (February–April 2027): Award Decision
**Objective:** Fund award Q1-Q2 2027.

**Actions:**
1. If awarded: activate pilot scope immediately; onboard NUU team
2. If not awarded but shortlisted: request evaluator feedback; identify criteria gaps
3. If rejected: assess whether criteria were locked against foreign vendors; pivot to direct EPIGU RFP track (INI-003)

**Fallback:** Even without Fund flagship award, EPIGU RFP ($2.8M, INI-003) and Courts RFP ($8M, INI-002) remain active. Fund criteria work builds credibility for those bids.

---

## Month 11–12 (May–June 2027): Deal Close or Pivot
**Objective:** Contract signature or clear pivot path.

**If contract awarded:** Begin 9-month pilot. Month 1 of execution = LLM training launch at NUU.
**If still in evaluation:** Escalate via MoEF (Kuchkarov level) — Fund is under MoEF supervision per PP-320.
**If full pivot:** Take INI-003 (EPIGU $2.8M) as the proving ground; use EPIGU deployment to reapply for Fund Year 2 cohort with production reference.
