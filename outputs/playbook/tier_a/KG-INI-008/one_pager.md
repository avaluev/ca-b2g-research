## Problem
KG's Digital Code (KG-LAW-2025-178) is enacted but 40+ secondary regulations are needed by end-2026. Chapter 4 (AI governance) specifically requires: AI model registry schema, auditability requirements, certification standards for government AI systems. No KG government body has the technical capacity to draft these.

## Solution
Co-author the Digital Code Chapter 4 secondary regulations:
- AI model registry schema and API specification
- Government AI auditability requirements (audit trail, model documentation)
- Certification standards for AI systems deployed on Tunduk
- Alignment with EU AI Act (Osmonov-era EU-leaning governance)

**Baktygul Tashmetova (Oxford OII)** is the academic bridge — she co-authored the Digital Code policy brief and is the most credible academic voice to legitimize our regulation drafting from outside government.
