# INI-017 — AI Civil Servant Training Program (UZ)
**Target institution:** Ministry of Higher Education / Presidential Training Center
**Target buyer:** Minister Kongratbay Sharipov (same as INI-011)
**Decree anchor:** УП-2024-095 (state civil servant training), ПП-2024-358
**Estimated initial contract:** $1,800,000 | 3-year revenue ~$8,000,000
**Scoring:** 7.85 | Speed 7 | Moat 6 | Defensibility 7 | Capital 7 | CIS Fit 7
**Precedent:** Saudi SDAIA AI capability building (case-sa-sdaia-ai-edu)

## Problem
UZ government civil servants lack AI literacy. УП-2024-095 mandates civil servant training modernization. Saudi SDAIA trained 10,000+ government employees in AI fundamentals. The Digital Bridge Tashkent 2026 is an ideal launch event.

## Solution
Civil servant AI training program:
1. E-learning platform: Russian-Uzbek bilingual AI fundamentals curriculum
2. Live workshop: hands-on AI tools for government workflows (document analysis, data analytics)
3. Certification: AI-literacy badge recognized by MoHESI
4. Train-the-trainer: 100 certified government AI trainers

**Synergy with INI-011:** Same infrastructure as the university AI labs program. Bundle proposal possible to reduce total cost.

## Hook (RU)
УП-2024-095 обязывает к модернизации обучения госслужащих. Saudi SDAIA обучил 10 000+ сотрудников AI-компетенциям. Мы предлагаем двуязычную (Русский-Узбекский) e-learning платформу с сертификацией MoHESI. Синергия с программой 15 AI-лабораторий (тендер УЗ-Т-2026-011).
