## Problem

Uzbekistan's customs leakage is estimated at 15-25% of formal import duties. Belt & Road traffic growth means manual risk-flagging is falling further behind. The SCC has a digital declaration system but no AI risk-scoring layer. Kazakhstan eGümrük proved the architecture delivers 30%+ fraud reduction on $5B+ annual customs flow.

## Solution (anchored on Kazakhstan eGümrük, case-kz-egumen-border)

Three-layer AI customs risk-scoring stack:
1. Declaration anomaly detection: HS code mismatch, valuation outliers, broker-pattern flags
2. Container/truck routing risk score: origin × commodity × broker × historical pattern
3. Inspector-facing dashboard with explainability (Russian-Uzbek bilingual)

Architecture preserved from eGümrük; adapted for UZ HS-code library, SCC declaration format, UzCloud.

**Partnership angle with Crimson Logic:** Crimson Logic has SCC incumbency on customs IT infrastructure. Position as AI overlay on their system, not a replacement — reduces their opposition and our integration complexity.
