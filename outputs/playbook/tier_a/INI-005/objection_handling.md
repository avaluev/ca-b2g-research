# INI-005 — Objection Handling

## Objection 1: "Crimson Logic already knows our systems"
**Response:** "We agree — that is why we propose Crimson Logic as the IT integration layer and ourselves as the AI intelligence layer. Their system knowledge and our AI capability are complementary. We have already explored a partnership conversation with their regional team. We are not asking you to replace Crimson Logic; we are asking you to add AI on top."

## Objection 2: "Customs data is classified — no foreign vendor"
**Response:** "Same response as tax data: full UzCloud deployment, no data egress, architecture reviewed by Uzinfocom before bid submission. Customs data never leaves the SCC network perimeter."

## Objection 3: "We don't have reliable data on which customs declarations turned out to be fraudulent"
**Response:** "Our Phase 1 starts with the structural graph approach — which does not require labeled fraud data. We detect patterns (HS code mismatch, valuation outliers, circular broker networks) that are structurally suspicious regardless of whether they have been flagged before. Phase 2 adds the supervised learning layer once inspectors validate the Phase 1 flags and we accumulate labeled outcomes."

## Objection 4: "This is not in our budget cycle"
**Response:** "The ADB-UZ-EGOVT pipeline can fund this as a technical grant component, reducing budget exposure. Alternatively, we can phase the deployment to match your budget cycle: Phase 1 as a $1.5M proof-of-concept, full deployment in the next budget year."

## Objection 5: "What is your Kazakhstan reference?"
**Response:** "We can provide the eGümrük architecture documentation and, with your permission, arrange a reference call with the Kazakhstan customs team. The architecture is documented in publicly available ADB technical assistance publications."
