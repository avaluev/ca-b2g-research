## Problem

KG's Digital Code (KG-LAW-2025-178) mandates an AI registry, AI auditability, and AI governance framework — but there is no sovereign Kyrgyz-Russian language model to underpin government AI services. Kyrgyz language is severely under-resourced: no production LLM covers Kyrgyz-Cyrillic at government-quality. The KG sovereign LLM is both a governance necessity (Digital Code) and a strategic asset (CBDC wallet support, EHR triage, procurement analytics, mountain telemedicine).

## Solution (anchored on UAE Falcon LLM methodology)

Build a 7B-parameter Kyrgyz-Russian sovereign LLM:
- Training data: Kyrgyz-Cyrillic government corpus + Russian government domain corpus
- Instruction tuning: government-service domain (CBDC wallet support, procurement, health triage)
- Co-publication: benchmark with AUCA (Andrew Wachtel, rector) and HTP AI resident
- Deployment: Tunduk inference fabric serving all downstream KG government AI
