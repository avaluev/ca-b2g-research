## Problem
KG has 2,000+ glaciers and faces accelerating GLOF (Glacier Lake Outburst Flood) risk under climate change. The 2024 Tuluk Lake GLOF caused $3M+ in damages. No AI-based early warning system exists.

## Solution (Deltares NL hydrological ML, case-nl-deltares-water)
AI-powered GLOF early warning:
1. Satellite imagery change detection (Sentinel-1/2): rapid glacier lake expansion monitoring
2. Hydrological ML: flood routing and downstream impact modeling
3. SMS/Telegram alert system for at-risk communities (Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual)
4. Integration with MChS emergency response protocols

**Differentiation:** Deltares architecture proven on 15+ GLOF systems globally. KG-specific: Tunduk-bound community alert distribution, Kyrgyz language SMS alerts.
