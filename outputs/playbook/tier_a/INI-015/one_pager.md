## Problem
UZ electricity system is under strain: 30%+ technical losses, peak demand management manual, renewable integration (Navoi solar) creating grid instability. Smart meters are being deployed but the AI demand-forecasting and grid-optimization layer does not exist.

## Solution
AI grid optimization:
1. Demand forecasting: ML model on consumption patterns, weather, industrial schedules
2. Fault detection: anomaly detection on transmission sensor data
3. Smart meter analytics: theft detection, demand-response programs
4. Renewable integration: AI dispatch optimization for solar/hydro mix
