## Problem
UZ cotton and wheat are state-priority crops. Yield variability and water use inefficiency are major challenges. Kazakhstan Smart Agriculture demonstrates that satellite-based AI field monitoring + soil sensor analytics can increase yields 15-20% and reduce water use 25%.

## Solution
AI precision agriculture stack:
1. Satellite field monitoring: NDVI crop stress detection, yield prediction (ESA Sentinel)
2. Water use optimization: AI irrigation scheduling per field
3. Pest and disease early warning: image-based AI diagnosis
4. Russian-Uzbek bilingual farmer advisory app

**Note on Russian/CIS fit:** Agricultural extension in UZ is shifting to Uzbek-language. Farmer-facing UX should be Uzbek-primary, Russian-secondary. CIS fit score adjusted to 7.
