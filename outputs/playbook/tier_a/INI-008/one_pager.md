## Solution (anchored on Kazakhstan Damumed, case-kz-damumed)

1. Primary-care AI triage: chief-complaint → differential diagnosis, integrated with E-Health Center
2. Prescription appropriateness checker (links to forthcoming UZ-TF-2026-003 e-prescription)
3. Imaging AI: chest X-ray, retinal screening for oblast hospitals lacking radiologists
4. Russian-Uzbek bilingual clinician UX; UzCloud data residency; MyID-bound patient consent

**Differentiation vs. Damumed:** Russian-Uzbek bilingual clinical UX + UZ-specific clinical guidelines integration. Damumed may extend from KZ; we differentiate on bilingual + UZ guidelines, not on opposing them.
