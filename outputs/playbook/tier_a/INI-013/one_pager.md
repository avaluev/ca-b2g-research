# INI-013 — AI EdTech Platform for UZ Schools (Bhashini-Style)
**Target institution:** Ministry of Public Education UZ
**Target buyer:** Minister of Public Education UZ (uz-minschool-minister)
**Decree anchors:** ПП-2024-054, ПП-2025-051-SCHOOL
**Estimated initial contract:** $5,500,000 | 3-year revenue ~$18,000,000
**Scoring:** 8.05 | Speed 8 | Moat 7 | Defensibility 7 | Capital 8 | CIS Fit 7 (education is partially de-Russifying — audit adjustment applied)
**Precedent:** India DIKSHA platform (case-in-diksha) — national school AI platform for 200M+ students

## Problem
UZ has 9,500+ schools and a digital education gap between urban and rural. ПП-2024-054 mandates digital education transformation. India DIKSHA serves 200M+ students on a sovereign platform with vernacular language support — the same architecture need for Uzbek-Latin + Russian bilingual coverage.

## Solution
Sovereign AI EdTech platform:
1. Uzbek-Latin + Russian bilingual curriculum delivery
2. Adaptive learning AI: personalized content difficulty based on student performance
3. Teacher dashboard: class performance analytics in Russian-Uzbek bilingual
4. Device-agnostic: works on low-spec tablets and smartphones common in rural UZ schools

- ISDB SMARTED co-financing creates a path for donor-backed procurement
- Samsung (Alisher Tashkentov, KAIST + Seoul) is an informal KOICA/Samsung equipment partner — use for hardware layer
