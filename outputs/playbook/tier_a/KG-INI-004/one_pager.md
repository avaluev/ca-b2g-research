## Problem

KG lacks a national EHR. Data is fragmented across hospital systems. KG's TB burden is among the highest in the CIS region; AI imaging triage for TB is the highest-ROI single AI deployment in KG health. Live tender KG-T-2026-006 ($4.03M) is the procurement vehicle. Mountain geography (>90%) makes telemedicine + offline mobile capability essential.

## Solution (Estonia e-Health + Kazakhstan Damumed + Bangladesh a2i Telemedicine)

1. National EHR: Tunduk-bound identity (KG citizen ID), patient data store, Russian-Kyrgyz bilingual clinician UX
2. AI diagnostic suite: chest X-ray TB screening (priority given KG TB burden), retinal screening, ECG arrhythmia detection — served as Tunduk inference fabric services
3. Telemedicine triage for mountain communities: offline-capable mobile app for ayil ambulatories (AKDN co-financed)

**Key for outreach to Osmonov:** His Turkish/EU medical education background means he may be receptive to EU/WHO standards framing. Approach with WHO-aligned clinical AI standards and Estonian e-Health architecture (Estonia is in EU).
