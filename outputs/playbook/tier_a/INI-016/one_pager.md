## Problem
UZ has physical biometric ID cards (УП-6065) and the MyID digital identity layer, but mobile ID adoption is lagging. Poland mObywatel proved that a sovereign mobile ID app on smartphones, replacing physical cards, increases e-government service uptake by 40%+. ASR is responsible for rollout.

## Solution
MyID mobile-ID national rollout:
1. Mobile app: MyID on Android + iOS, offline-capable for low-connectivity areas
2. AI identity verification: liveness check + document match, reducing fraud
3. Integration: all 350+ EPIGU services bound to MyID for personalized delivery
4. Russian-Uzbek bilingual UX
