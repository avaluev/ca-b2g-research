## METHODOLOGY

Forecast tenders are predicted from four signal types:

1. **Decree implementation deadline approaching**: Decrees with `half_life_status: active_window` and implementation deadlines in the next 6–18 months. Each such decree creates a procurement mandate.
2. **Donor program disbursement schedule**: Active donor programs with announced budgets and stated procurement pipelines.
3. **Presidential speeches and ministry announcements**: Official public commitments that pre-announce procurement scope.
4. **Convergent Window (CW) analysis**: Cross-decree + cross-donor opportunity clusters identified in `state/blueprint/target_lists.json`.

Verification tags: `INFERRED` = derived from public signals; `L2_VERIFIED` = cross-confirmed by two independent sources.
