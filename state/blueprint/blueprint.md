# Central Asia B2G Research Harness — Strategic Blueprint
**Wave 0 output. Author: blueprint-architect. Reasoning model: opus. Synthesis date: 2026-05-03.**

This document scaffolds the parallel multi-agent research effort to map the AI/digital B2G opportunity surface in **Uzbekistan (UZ)** and **Kyrgyzstan (KG)**. It is a planning artifact, not a findings document. Every claim carries a verification tag — `[VERIFIED]` (cross-confirmed against ≥2 official/primary sources within last 12 months), `[L2_VERIFIED]` (single primary source with credible secondary corroboration), `[INFERRED]` (reasoned from indirect evidence; downstream agents must confirm), or `[UNVERIFIED]` (working hypothesis only).

The blueprint is deliberately opinionated. Karpathy first-principles framing: in both UZ and KG, the **decree → budget → procurement** chain is the only thing that matters for B2G revenue. Everything else (think tank reports, donor strategy papers, conference keynotes) is signal about that chain. The harness exists to identify decrees in the **active 0-to-18-month window** with budget allocation and unawarded procurement, then map the named-individual decision dyad (government counterpart × donor TTL × diaspora advisor) that controls the contract.

## Section 1 — Political Economy Snapshot per Country

### Uzbekistan (220 words)

UZ is in year nine of the **Karimov-to-Mirziyoyev institutional rewrite** that began September 2016. The state apparatus is unrecognizable from its pre-2017 form: every economic ministry has been split, merged, or rebranded; new agencies (Agency for Strategic Reforms / АСР, IT Park, Innovation Agency, Cadastre Agency, Public Services Agency) were created with hand-picked, often Western- or Korea-educated leadership. The current operating doctrine is **"Стратегия Нового Узбекистана 2022–2026"** [VERIFIED] complemented by the AI-specific **Стратегия развития технологий искусственного интеллекта до 2030** approved by ПП-358 on 14.10.2024 [VERIFIED via lex.uz]. The October 2025 wave (УП-189 of 22.10.2025 + ПП-320 of 30.10.2025) added a **fresh $100 M Reconstruction & Development Fund allocation for 2026–2027** and committed to 100 AI implementations in government bodies by end-2026 [VERIFIED]. UZ ranked **9th globally** in the World Bank GovTech Maturity Index 2025 — Group A "High GovTech Maturity," first in Central Asia [VERIFIED]. Population 36+ M, GDP 2025 >$145 B [L2_VERIFIED via Spot.uz/Mirziyoyev]. Politics: presidential, low intra-elite contestation, high vertical discipline once a decree is signed; the bottleneck is design quality at the decree-drafting stage, not implementation will.

### Kyrgyzstan (220 words)

KG is in year five of the **Japarov Concentration**, in which the post-2021 constitutional rewrite shifted real authority to the Presidential Administration / Управление делами президента (UDP). On approximately **30 April 2026**, President Sadyr Japarov signed the decree **"О неотложных мерах по эффективной реализации цифровой трансформации"** abolishing the Ministry of Digital Development as a standalone body and transferring all of its functions — digitalization policy, state digital services, telecom regulation, cybersecurity, archives, satellite tech, AI, and innovation — to the UDP [VERIFIED]. This is the most consequential institutional event in CA digital governance in the last 24 months and inverts the entire UNDP/ADB/EU project-counterpart map overnight. Earlier wave: **Concept of Digital Transformation 2024–2028** approved by Presidential Decree №90 of 5.04.2024 [VERIFIED]; **Цифровой кодекс** adopted by Jogorku Kenesh on 18.06.2025, registered 31.07.2025 [VERIFIED]; CBDC framework (digital som) law signed April 2025, in force from January 2027 [VERIFIED]; national stablecoin USDKG and KGST launched October 2025 [L2_VERIFIED]. Population ~7 M, GDP growth ~11% [L2_VERIFIED]. Politics: smaller country, fewer gatekeepers, faster contracts, smaller ticket sizes; the dissolution of Minтsifры makes the UDP digital department the **single highest-leverage institutional target** for the next 12 months.

## Section 6 — Research Risk Register

### R-001 — Stale Org Chart Risk (severity: HIGH)
**Description.** Both UZ and KG had major institutional events in the last 12 months (UZ: УП-189/ПП-320 created new bodies; KG: April 2026 dissolution of Минцифры). Russian-language Wikipedia, TAdviser, and even some donor portfolio docs still cite pre-restructuring names. Downstream agents who use AI/LLM training data without anchoring to lex.uz / cbd.minjust.gov.kg / president.kg will produce stale records.
**Mitigation.** Every institution record requires `last_verified_date` ≥ 2026-04-01 OR explicit `status: restructured` flag. Validate-state.py must enforce.

### R-002 — Vanity Strategy Risk (severity: HIGH)
**Description.** Decrees are signed for political signaling; budgets are not always actually allocated; "100 AI projects" can mean "100 PowerPoint slides." Confusing strategic intent with implementation reality is the #1 way this harness produces useless output.
**Mitigation.** Every decree record requires both `budget_allocated_usd` AND `budget_disclosed: true/false`. Procurement-harvester must search for actual tender notices, not press releases. Initiative records require traceability to either a live tender, donor disbursement schedule, or budget-line confirmation.

### R-003 — Donor PR Trap (severity: MEDIUM)
**Description.** Donor "press release ecosystems" celebrate signed loans as if implementation has begun. Often the project has 3-7 year disbursement and the actual procurement window is years later.
**Mitigation.** Every donor program record must distinguish `period_start` vs `disbursed_usd` vs `pipeline_tenders[]`. The financial-modeler equivalent here is: revenue available is in `pipeline_tenders[]`, not `total_budget_usd`.

### R-004 — Language-Gap Trap (severity: HIGH)
**Description.** English-only research undercounts the field by 40-60% in both countries. Russian-language sources are mandatory; Uzbek (especially Uzbek-Cyrillic for older population, Uzbek-Latin for younger) and Kyrgyz add another 10-20%. LinkedIn presence is sparse for CA officials; alternative platforms (Telegram, VKontakte, Facebook) are higher-yield for KG; Twitter/X for younger UZ technocrats.
**Mitigation.** CLAUDE.md rule: ≥50% Russian-language sources. Add: every Tier 1 person record must have ≥1 native-language (UZ/KY) source attempted. Search-strategies.md spells out the operators.

### R-005 — LinkedIn Fabrication Risk (severity: HIGH, security-critical)
**Description.** Strong temptation in agents to fabricate LinkedIn URLs because the mention pattern `linkedin.com/in/{name}` is so regular. Fabrication contaminates downstream outreach and destroys credibility.
**Mitigation.** CLAUDE.md rule: no fabrication. Every `linkedin_url` field is either empirically verified by visiting the URL, or `null` with `linkedin_status: "not_found"`. Validate-state.py adds a check: any `linkedin_url` that doesn't pattern-match `https://(www\.|[a-z]{2}\.)?linkedin\.com/in/[a-zA-Z0-9-_]+/?` is rejected; agents must have visited and noted in source.

### R-006 — Decree Number Hallucination (severity: HIGH)
**Description.** Decree numbers (`УП-189`, `ПП-358`, `№90`) are pattern-regular and tempting to autocomplete. Hallucination breaks the chain to lex.uz / cbd.minjust.gov.kg.
**Mitigation.** Every decree record must include the lex.uz or cbd.minjust.gov.kg URL in `sources[]`. Validate-state.py adds a check that the URL is reachable.

### R-007 — Tier Misclassification (severity: MEDIUM)
**Description.** Confusing a Working Group (Tier 7) with a regulator (Tier 6) or a SOE (Tier 5) with an agency (Tier 4) leads to mis-routed pitches and wrong contract templates.
**Mitigation.** Tier definitions in state_schema.json. Institution-mapper agent applies them strictly; reflexion-auditor checks.

### R-008 — Survivorship Bias in Cases (severity: MEDIUM)
**Description.** Case-tournament will be tempted to include only successful global cases (Estonia, Singapore, India). Including failed and partial cases (Aadhaar privacy fights, Australia robo-debt, Toronto Sidewalk Labs cancellation, Latvia eHealth restart) is essential for risk calibration.
**Mitigation.** case-tournament agent must include ≥20% partial-failure / cautionary cases.

### R-009 — Diaspora-Advisor Privacy & Speculation (severity: HIGH, security-critical)
**Description.** It is tempting to speculate about who is "really" the diaspora advisor behind a strategy. CLAUDE.md forbids speculation about personal political loyalties. People who are publicly positioned (named in speeches, on Presidential Council, co-authored papers) are admissible; whisper-network claims are not.
**Mitigation.** Every diaspora-advisor record requires ≥1 public document trail. Reflexion-auditor flags any unverified diaspora claim.

### R-010 — Geo-Politics Drift (severity: MEDIUM)
**Description.** UZ and KG are pivots in great-power competition (Russia, China, US/EU, Turkey, Korea, India, GCC). The harness should not have a covert preference; it should map all corridors.
**Mitigation.** Donor-pipeline coverage of the full donor stack. People-intelligence covers Korean, Chinese, Indian advisors equally to Western.

### R-011 — Procurement-Path Misclassification (severity: MEDIUM)
**Description.** Many high-value B2G engagements in CA happen via direct contracting under emergency procurement provisions, donor-rules procurement (which has its own rules), or PPP. Treating them all as ICB tender shopping is wrong.
**Mitigation.** procurement-harvester captures procurement modality per item. Tender records distinguish ICB / NCB / QCBS / CQS / direct / RFP / mixed.

### R-012 — Currency / Budget Inflation Drift (severity: LOW)
**Description.** UZS and KGS depreciation makes USD-converted budgets fluctuate. Always preserve `currency_original` and `value_original`.
**Mitigation.** schema enforces both. Budget figures pre-2024 must be re-converted at 2024 rates for analysis.
