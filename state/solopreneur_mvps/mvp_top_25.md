## Patterns across the Top 25

### What works in Central Asia (lessons from the ranking)

1. **Decree-driven cliff demand wins.** Eight of the top 10 anchor on a decree that just took effect (UZ-PP-2025-286 mahalla rollout, KG-LAW-2025-178 Digital Code, UZ-UP-2025-189 AI). Citizens have new obligations they cannot meet themselves.
2. **Wizard-of-Oz beats SaaS for first-week revenue.** Eight of the top 25 are Wizard-of-Oz. The fulfillment burden is a feature: it gates competition while you build customer relationships.
3. **Bilingual (Uzbek/Kyrgyz + Russian) is the moat.** Every top-25 entry uses local language as a differentiator. Western competitors and AI tools without Uzbek/Kyrgyz cannot serve these customers.
4. **Diaspora-bridge is a price-premium category.** GovDocTranslator, MigrantWageGuard, FreelancerInvoice command higher prices because their customers earn in USD/RUB.
5. **Local payment rails (Click/Payme/Optima/Mbank) are non-negotiable.** Stripe alone does not work; bilingual UX with local QR-code payment is the table stake.

### Speed-to-revenue ranking (90-day cash)

Wizard-of-Oz and Demo Video MVPs generate first revenue in 5–14 days. Subscription SaaS takes 60–90 days. Manifesto/content takes 90+ days. For a solo founder with under $500 capital, prioritize the Wizard-of-Oz tier first, then layer in subscription products.
