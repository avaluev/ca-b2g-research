## Key Analytical Notes

### The UZ Stack Logic
Three decrees (ПП-358 → УП-189 → ПП-320) form an interlocking funding stack. ПП-358 created the AI Strategy and committed $50M. УП-189 committed another $100M and set 100 projects and 15 labs targets. ПП-320 distributes the $100M through Coordination Commission. Reading these together: the Commission becomes the de-facto AI fund manager for 2026–2027. **Shaping the grant distribution criteria is the single most leveraged activity possible in UZ right now.**

### ЗРУ-1115 — Regulatory Novelty
Uzbekistan now has the first AI-specific law in the Central Asia region (January 2026). Its prohibition on exclusively AI-driven decisions affecting individual rights creates immediate demand for hybrid human+AI workflow design consulting. Vendors offering "AI that keeps humans in the loop" have a built-in pitch.

### The KG Institutional Reset
The April 30, 2026 Mintsifry liquidation created a 90-day window (May–July 2026) where:
- All existing donor program counterparts are uncertain
- New department heads have not been named
- Procurement pipelines are frozen pending new TORs
This is simultaneously the highest-risk period (deals paused) and highest-leverage period (whoever gets to the new UDP digital department head first shapes the 2027 procurement agenda).

### Decree Numbers Not Verifiable
The following items from the blueprint target list could not be assigned confirmed decree numbers from official sources as of May 2026:
1. Cabinet Resolution of 31.01.2025 on gov.uz website migration — referenced in gazeta.uz/IT Park but decree number not found on lex.uz search
2. УП-?? on creation of Agency for Strategic Reforms (pre-2021) — ASR exists and is verified via asr.gov.uz but founding decree number not confirmed
3. Presidential Decree on establishment of MyID legal framework — MyID operates under UZINFOCOM but no standalone decree found; legal basis appears to be lex.uz ЗРУ-395 + КМ resolutions

These are flagged for Wave 5 reflexion-auditor verification.

### KG Digital Code as Standards Platform
The 101 definitions in Chapter 1 of the Digital Code establish the vocabulary for all future KG digital regulation. Vendors who help shape the secondary legislation drafting — especially around AI system classification (high-risk definition) and data processing requirements — will have a structural advantage in future procurements.
