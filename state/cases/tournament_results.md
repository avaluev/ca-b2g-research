## Notable "Why Runner-Up Lost" Notes

1. **Estonia X-Road vs Kazakhstan eGov for UZ**: X-Road is integration infrastructure; eGov.kz is the citizen experience. UZ officials need both but quote the visible portal to Presidents. Deploy X-Road as Phase 1 infrastructure; launch eGov-equivalent portal as Phase 2 visible win.

2. **Singapore Singpass vs India Aadhaar for UZ digital ID**: Singpass is architecturally superior for private-sector data sharing. But Aadhaar has the open API and SCO diplomatic track already in place for UZ. Aadhaar wins on political economy.

3. **France Albert vs UAE Falcon for UZ sovereign LLM**: Albert is government deployment; Falcon is the model foundation. UZ needs BOTH: Falcon as the base, Albert-architecture as the deployment framework. They are not competing — they are a stack.

4. **Kazakhstan Enbek vs India e-Shram for UZ labor**: Enbek is the formal sector digital labour exchange. e-Shram is the informal sector registry. UZ needs BOTH in sequence: Enbek first for quick wins, e-Shram second for the 5M+ informal workers.

5. **Rwanda Irembo vs Albania e-Albania for KG**: Irembo's private concession model is risky for KG. Albania's government-operated fast-platform model is safer and EU-fundable. Albania wins on political economy despite Rwanda's innovation points.
